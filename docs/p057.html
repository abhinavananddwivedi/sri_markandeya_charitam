Verse 49
Shlokamanuensis's hand
निशम्य तस्यानुनयं महामुनिः सुविस्मितः सास्मितमाह बोधयन् । भवाटवीं दुर्विषहां दुरिन्द्रियै- र्निरीक्ष्य मा भीतिममुं व्रजाऽधुना ॥ ४९ ॥
English Translation (of the Sanskrit)AI-assisted translation
Hearing his entreaty, the great sage, much surprised, said with a smile, to enlighten him: "Do not be afraid now on seeing the forest of worldly life, hard to bear because of the wicked senses; go." (49)

English Translation (of the Hindi)AI-assisted translation
Hearing his request, the great householder sage was surprised and, explaining again, said with a smile: "Do not be afraid now on seeing the forest of worldly life, which is hard to bear because of the wicked senses." (49)

मुनि मृकण्ड की अनुनय पूर्ण बातें सुनकर गृही मुनि साश्चर्य मुस्कराते हुए उन्हें यों समझाते हुए कहने लगे, वत्स! यह संसार रूपी महाजंगल बड़ा भयंकर है, इसमें रहकर बिना इन्द्रियों पर विजय प्राप्त किए हुए व्यक्तियों द्वारा शान्तिपूर्ण जीवन जीना सर्वथा दुष्कर है, यह देखकर तुम भयभीत मत होवो। ४९
English NotesAI-assisted translation
Hearing the sage Mṛkaṇḍa's pleading words, the householder sage, smiling in surprise, began to explain to him: "Child! This great forest of the world is very fearsome; living in it, a peaceful life is wholly difficult for people who have not conquered their senses. Do not be afraid on seeing this." (49)
Verse 50
Shlokamanuensis's hand
विशालधीर्निर्जितषड्रिपुर्भवान् विभासि बालोऽपि विशुद्धबुद्धिभाक् । उपासितप्राज्ञवरो विवेकतो यतस्ततो वत्स सुखं निवत्स्यसि ॥ ५० ॥
English Translation (of the Sanskrit)AI-assisted translation
"You are broad-minded and have conquered the six enemies; though a boy, you shine with a pure intellect; with discernment you have served the best of the wise; therefore, child, you will live happily wherever [you go]." (50)
Translationamanuensis's hand
आप विशाल बुद्धि वाले हो आपने कामादि छह शत्रुओं को जीत लिया है। बालक होने पर भी विशाल बुद्धि सम्पन्न हो। अपने विवेक से तुमने श्रेष्ठ जनों की उपासना की है, अतः हे वत्स! जहाँ कहीं भी सुख पूर्वक निवास करोगे ॥ ५० ॥
English Translation (of the Hindi)AI-assisted translation
"You have a broad mind; you have conquered the six enemies, desire and the rest. Though a boy, you are endowed with a broad mind. By your discernment you have served the best people; so, child, you will live happily wherever you are." (50)
Notesgrandfather's hand
प्रिय वत्स! तुम अत्यन्त विशाल बुद्धि वाले हो, तुमने अपनी उदात्त साधना से शरीरस्थ कामादि छः शत्रुओं पर निर्बाध विजय प्राप्त कर लिया है, यद्यपि देखने में अभी सामान्य बालक से लगते हो किन्तु बुद्धिवैभव में तुम अत्यन्त श्रेष्ठ हो। तुमने अपने सद्विवेक द्वारा गुरुओं की सम्यक् पूर्वक आराधना की है, अतः प्रिय वत्स! तुम जहाँ कहीं भी रहोगे पूर्ण सुख शान्ति, समृद्धिपूर्ण जीवन जिओगे। ५०
English NotesAI-assisted translation
"Dear child! You have a very broad mind; through your noble discipline you have won an unhindered victory over the six enemies within the body, desire and the rest. Though you still look like an ordinary boy, in wealth of intellect you are most excellent. With your good discernment you have worshipped your teachers properly; so, dear child, wherever you live, you will live a life full of happiness, peace and prosperity." (50)
