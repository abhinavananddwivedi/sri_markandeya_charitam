English NotesAI-assisted translation
"Lord! You two, who bear the very form of God before our eyes, remain constantly watchful through your exceedingly fierce austerity for the welfare of the whole world. O Lord, sole concentrated mass of all radiance! Dwelling in the world for its welfare, you two are [not] honoured by embodied beings (creatures) who desire to obtain boons for themselves." [The Hindi reads "सम्माननीय नहीं हैं", "are not honoured"; the verse's rhetorical question, "by whom are you not to be honoured?", is evidently meant.] (35)

English NotesAI-assisted translation
"Lord! It is a matter of great joy that today I am fulfilled by your grace. Now I am very assured about my future rise and feel happy.
