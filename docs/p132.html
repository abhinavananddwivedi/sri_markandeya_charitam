English NotesAI-assisted translation
Many young men (horsemen), mounted on (driving) many first-rate horses that had been made ready before the wedding procession, ready in the contest of "I'll ride first, I'll ride first", and exceedingly skilled and expert in the various gaits (paces) of the horse, mounted their horses, kept themselves always ready to guard the bridegroom, and set themselves eagerly to his protection at the dhauritaka (a gallop).
