English NotesAI-assisted translation
By worshipping the beginningless, primal god, Lord Sadāśiva Mṛtyuñjaya, and receiving only a particle of the causeless grace that came from his pleasure, the sage Mārkaṇḍeya became long-lived to the end of seven kalpas. Today, with my inner awareness full, I constantly offer my bow to that Lord Tryambaka Mṛtyuñjaya, sole ruler of creation, preservation and dissolution, as shown and worshipped by my forefathers (my grandfather and others). (3)
