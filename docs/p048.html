Verse 31
Shlokamanuensis's hand
इतीरितो मार्गमिमं समागतो भवन्तमालोक्य तदुक्तिसम्मितम् । दधे मनोऽहं गृहमेधिकर्मणि प्रशाधि विप्रोऽसि गुरुर्मतो मया ॥ ३१ ॥
English Translation (of the Sanskrit)AI-assisted translation
"So told, I came along this road; seeing you, who match their words, I have set my mind on the work of a householder. Instruct me, brahmin; I have taken you as my teacher." (31)

English Translation (of the Hindi)AI-assisted translation
"On being told so by those two, I came by this road. Seeing you, who fit their words, I set my mind on the householder's dharma. Teach me, for you know; I have accepted you as my teacher." (31)

English NotesAI-assisted translation
"On such teaching from those excellent sages, I set out on this road. Finding you, who are most fitting according to their words, I too have made up my mind to follow the householder's dharma. Now give me proper teaching, for you have full knowledge of the householder's dharma; from today I have accepted you as my revered teacher." (31)
Verse 32
Shlokamanuensis's hand
इतीरयित्वा विरते गृहीमुनि- र्मनस्युदासीनवदास्थितश्चिरम् । पुनर्बभाषे तदुपायदर्शना- न्मुने समाधन्वतया न कन्यका ॥ ३२ ॥
English Translation (of the Sanskrit)AI-assisted translation
When he had said this and stopped, the householder sage remained long in his mind as if indifferent; then, seeing a way to it, he spoke again: "Sage, [I have] no daughter [to give] in this matter." (32)
Translationamanuensis's hand
ऐसे कह कहकर मृकण्डु के मौन हो जाने पर उस गृहस्थ मुनि ने मन में कुछ उदासीन भाव में चिरकाल तक स्थित रहा। उसके उपाय के दर्शन से पुनः बोल पड़े कि हे मुनिवर! मेरे दुर्भाग्य से कोई कन्या नहीं है ॥ ३२ ॥
English Translation (of the Hindi)AI-assisted translation
When Mṛkaṇḍu, having said this, fell silent, that householder sage remained for a long time in a somewhat downcast mood. Seeing a way for him, he spoke again: "Excellent sage! By my misfortune I have no daughter." (32)
Notesgrandfather's hand
इतनी बात कहकर मुनि मृकण्ड के चुप हो जाने पर वह गृहस्थ मुनि कुछ काल तक मन ही मन अत्यन्त उदासीन रहे किन्तु पुनः उनके मन को आश्वस्त करने की दृष्टि से फिर कह उठे कि हे मुने! संयोग की बात है कि दुर्भाग्य से मेरी कोई कन्या नहीं है। ३२
English NotesAI-assisted translation
When the sage Mṛkaṇḍa had said this much and fell silent, that householder sage was very downcast in his mind for some time; but then, with a view to reassuring him, he spoke again: "Sage! As it happens, by misfortune I have no daughter." (32)
