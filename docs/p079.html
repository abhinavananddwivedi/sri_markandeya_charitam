English NotesAI-assisted translation
With profound humility, Sage Mṛkaṇḍu submitted: "O Lord! Kindly instruct me: To which earthly king should I go to ask for land suitable for building a hermitage? From where are the various construction materials to be gathered? And how will craftsmen and laborers perform my work skillfully, attentively, and enthusiastically without monetary wages (pāriśramika)?" (38)
