Verse 38
Shlokamanuensis's hand
याच्यतां नरपतिः पुनः कतः सम्भृतेश्च कृत-सम्भरेत्। कारुकैश्च परिश्रमेण वा कुर्वते स्म मम कार्यमुत्सुकम्॥ ३८॥

वृत्त: रथोद्धता। पाद २–३ छन्द में नहीं बैठते (पाद २ में केवल ९ अक्षर); पाण्डुलिपि में फीके हैं; पुनः पठन आवश्यक।

English Translation (of the Sanskrit)AI-assisted translation
"From which king should [land] be asked? From where [should] the materials be gathered? And how would workmen, by their labour, eagerly do my work?" [Partly corrupt.] (38)

"From which sovereign king should land be petitioned? From where should the building materials be procured? And how will artisans and craftsmen carry out my work attentively without wages?" (Verse 38)

English NotesAI-assisted translation
The sage Mṛkaṇḍu very humbly asked: "Lord! Please tell me which king I should go to and ask for land fit to build a house on; where the other materials for building that house should be gathered from; and how craftsmen will be able to do my work well and eagerly without payment (wages)." (38)
Verse 39
Shlokamanuensis's hand
इत्युदीर्य विरते हि वै मुनौ चारुचन्द्रवदनः पराशरः। हर्षयन् स्वजनमार्द्रया गिरा सस्मितः स तमभाषत द्विजम्॥ ३९॥
Manuscript NoteAI-assisted transcription
हस्तलेख पाठभेद/लिपि-टिप्पणी: पाद ३ में पाण्डुलिपि में `हर्षयन् स्वजनमार्जया गिरा` दृष्टिगोचर होता है, जो `स्वजनम् आर्द्रया गिरा` (अथवा मार्दवया गिरा) का वाचक है। वृत्त: रथोद्धता
Manuscript Note (EN)AI-assisted transcription
Paleographic / Textual Note: In line 3, the amanuensis writes `हर्षयन् स्वजनमार्जया गिरा`, representing `स्वजनम् आर्द्रया गिरा` [gladdening his kin with tender, soothing words]. Meter: Rathoddhatā [– ⏑ – | ⏑ ⏑ ⏑ | – ⏑ – | ⏑ –]
English Translation (of the Sanskrit)AI-assisted translation
When the sage had said this and fallen silent, Parāśara, his face lovely as the moon, gladdening his own [visitor] with tender speech, spoke with a smile to that brahmin. (39)
Translationamanuensis's hand
ऐसा कह कर जैसे ही महर्षि मृकण्डु मौन हुए उसी समय चारुचन्द्र वदन पराशर महर्षि ने अपनी स्निग्ध वाणी से आनन्दित करते हुए मुस्कुराहट के साथ बोल पड़े॥ ३९॥
English Translation (of the Hindi)AI-assisted translation
As soon as Maharṣi Mṛkaṇḍu fell silent after saying this, Maharṣi Parāśara, whose face was lovely as the moon, gladdening him with his tender speech, spoke with a smile. (39)

English NotesAI-assisted translation
As soon as the sage Mṛkaṇḍu, having humbly said this much, fell silent, Maharṣi Parāśara, whose lotus face was in full bloom, began to speak with a smile, in a very loving, generous, sweet and tender voice, to that brahmin sage Mṛkaṇḍu who had come. (39)
