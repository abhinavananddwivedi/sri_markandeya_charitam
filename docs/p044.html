सुपाकसिद्धयै गृहिणीमुपागतः समादिदेशाथ सती निजस्नुषाः । परावृत्य सस्तूर्णमपूर्वदर्शनं मुनिं समूचेऽमृतसन्निभं वचः ॥ २३ ॥
English Translation (of the Sanskrit)AI-assisted translation
He went to his wife to have a good meal made, and that good woman directed her daughters-in-law; returning quickly, he spoke words like nectar to the sage of extraordinary appearance. (23)

English Translation (of the Hindi)AI-assisted translation
He went to his wife and told her to prepare a fine meal; his wife gave orders to her daughters-in-law. Returning quickly, he spoke words sweet as nectar to the sage of extraordinary appearance. (23)

English NotesAI-assisted translation
Right after this, that good householder went inside his house and asked his fortunate wife to make an excellent meal fit for that visiting brahmacārī; his wife in turn ordered her daughters-in-law to arrange the meal quickly. Coming quickly out of the house, the householder went to that brahmacārī, whom he was seeing for the first time, and began to speak to the guest in a very sweet voice like nectar. (23)
Verse 24
Shlokamanuensis's hand
यथाऽतिदीने द्रविणस्य दर्शना- दवग्रहे सस्य कृते च वर्षणात् । उपद्रुते सच्चरितेऽरेर्धर्षणा- त्तथा सुखं मे भवतोऽद्यदर्शनात् ॥ २४ ॥
English Translation (of the Sanskrit)AI-assisted translation
"As the very poor rejoice at the sight of wealth, as crops in a drought at rain, as a good man harassed [rejoices] at an enemy's defeat, so is my happiness today at seeing you." (24)
Translationamanuensis's hand
जैसे अतिदीनहीन व्यक्ति को धन के दर्शन से, और उसके लिए धन की वर्षा से, शत्रु के धर्षण से सच्चरित के उपद्रुत (बढ़) जाने पर सुख होती है वैसे ही आज मुझे आपके दर्शन से सुख हो रहा है ॥ २४ ॥
English Translation (of the Hindi)AI-assisted translation
"As a very poor and wretched person is happy at the sight of wealth, and at a shower of wealth for him, and as a good man is happy when, having been harassed, the enemy is overpowered, so I am happy today at seeing you." (24)

वर्णिन्! जैसे अतिशय दीन हीन व्यक्ति को प्रचुर धन के दर्शन से, अकर्षण की भीषण स्थिति में उपयुक्त वर्षा हो जाने से, उपद्रव पूर्ण स्थिति में उपद्रव करने वाले व्यक्ति को (शत्रु) को किसी सच्चरित्र व्यक्ति द्वारा शान्त कर देने से जो सुख प्राप्त होता है, हे ब्रह्मचारिन्! आज मुझे आपके दर्शन से वही अपूर्व सुख प्राप्त हो रहा है। २४
English NotesAI-assisted translation
"Brahmacārī! The happiness that a very poor, wretched person gets from seeing plenty of wealth, that [farmers] get when fitting rain falls in a terrible drought, and that [people] get when, in a time of troubles, the troublemaker (the enemy) is calmed by some person of good character: O brahmacārī, that same unprecedented happiness I am getting today from seeing you." (24)
