English Translation (of the Sanskrit)AI-assisted translation
Know at once these, adorned with garments bright as pure lightning, who labour finely at teaching the gods, glowing in great splendour on their elephant-drawn aerial cars, to be those bound for Bṛhaspati's (Jupiter's) world. (47)

English Translation (of the Hindi)AI-assisted translation
Those adorned with garments gleaming like very pure lightning, who toil at teaching scholars and gods, resplendent on [unclear: elephant-drawn] aerial cars, of great radiance: take them at once to be dwellers of Bṛhaspati's world. (47)

Wearing on their [unclear: limbs] yellow garments with the intense, radiant gleam of very pure, yellowish lightning, imparting the finest teaching, full of many kinds of learning, roaming on fine aerial cars drawn by elephants, very glorious: take these to be dwellers of Bṛhaspati's world. (47)
