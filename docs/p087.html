English NotesAI-assisted translation
After this, when Sage Mṛkaṇḍu had completed his daily rites, he first bowed to the lord of sages, Parāśara. Having received his blessing, he became still more radiant. At dawn he set out with his two cows and their calves (four cattle), together with his companion, Sage Āṅgirasa, a companion of the highest rank, and so proceeded in harmony with him. (1)
Verse 2
Shlokamanuensis's hand
सत्यपि स्वतपसि स्वसहाय- न्नाभ्यमन्यत यतो बहुदर्शी । ईश्वरोऽपि निजशक्तिसहायः सृष्टिकर्मणि बभूव समर्थः ॥ २ ॥
Manuscript NoteAI-assisted transcription
वृत्त: स्वागता।
Manuscript Note (EN)AI-assisted transcription
Metre: Svāgatā.
English Translation (of the Sanskrit)AI-assisted translation
Though his own austerity sufficed, being far-sighted he did not count on a helper of his own; yet even the Lord became able in the work of creation only with His own Śakti as helper. (2)

अपनी तपस्या के विद्यमान होने पर उन्होंने अपने सहायक स्वीकार नहीं किया क्यों कि वे बहुदर्शी थे। ईश्वर भी अपनी शक्ति की सहायता से ही सृष्टि कर्म में समर्थ हुआ ॥ २ ॥
English Translation (of the Hindi)AI-assisted translation
Since his own austerity was present, he did not accept a helper of his own, because he was far-sighted. Even God became able in the work of creation only with the help of His own power. (2)
Notesgrandfather's hand
मुनि मृकण्ड यद्यपि स्वयं अपनी तपस्या की अतिशयिता से तेजोदीप्त थे, वे यद्यपि सांसारिक सम्पूर्ण वस्तुओं क्रियाओं के पूर्ण अभिज्ञ थे, उन्हें किसी सहचर या अनुचर की आवश्यकता नहीं थी किन्तु मुनि पराशर जी के दिये सहयोगी मुनि आंगिरस को उन्होंने तिरस्कार नहीं किया, क्योंकि ईश्वर भी यद्यपि सृष्टि संरचना स्वयं कर सकता है किन्तु उसने भी अपने सृष्टि संरचना में अपनी शक्ति को ही सहायिका बनाया था। २
English NotesAI-assisted translation
Although Sage Mṛkaṇḍa was himself radiant through the intensity of his austerity, and although he knew all worldly things and actions fully and needed no companion or attendant, he did not spurn Sage Āṅgirasa, the companion given by Sage Parāśara. For although God can create the world by Himself, He too made His own Śakti His helper in creating it. (2)
