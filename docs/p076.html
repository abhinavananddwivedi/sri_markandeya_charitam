Metre: Rathoddhatā. Pāda 2 reads "मखिलं मनोरथम्", and pāda 3 "धातुरात्मज-" (earlier "धात्रजस्त्वया"). The opening of pāda 4 is unclear ("लक्ष्यसे" or "स्तच्छ्रुतो…"); "…भिधेहि सत्वरम्" is legible, and the earlier "तदपि ब्रूहि" does not scan.
English Translation (of the Sanskrit)AI-assisted translation
"Your appearance tells, truly and wonderfully, all your wishes. Are you Mṛkaṇḍu, the son of Dhātā? Tell me quickly." [The opening of pāda 4 is unclear.] (32)

English NotesAI-assisted translation
"Dear child! Your form itself shows your radiant vigour, and at the same time all the wishes in your heart are clearly visible to us. You are Mṛkaṇḍa, the good son of Maharṣi Dhātā, are you not? Please be so kind as to say all these things plainly." (32)

भूतभाव्यभवदादि कालजं ज्ञानमाकलयतोऽस्य भाषितम्। सान्निशम्य चकितोऽभिधाय निजां पूर्वजान् सुचरितानभासत॥ ३३॥
Manuscript NoteAI-assisted transcription
वृत्त: रथोद्धता। पाद ३ पाण्डुलिपि में "…चकितोऽभिधां निजां" जैसा है (छन्द-शुद्ध); पाद ३–४ का आरम्भ अस्पष्ट है, पूर्व पाठ यथावत्।
Manuscript Note (EN)AI-assisted transcription
Metre: Rathoddhatā. Pāda 3 looks like "…चकितोऽभिधां निजां" in the manuscript (which scans); the openings of pādas 3–4 are unclear, and the earlier reading is kept.
English Translation (of the Sanskrit)AI-assisted translation
Hearing the words of this one, who grasped knowledge born of time, past, future and present, [Mṛkaṇḍu,] amazed, named himself and spoke of his ancestors and their good conduct. (33)

English Translation (of the Hindi)AI-assisted translation
Hearing his words, which gathered up knowledge of past, future and present, born of time, Mṛkaṇḍu was amazed and began to speak words befitting the virtues of his ancestors. (33)

मुनि मृकण्डु महर्षि पराशर द्वारा भूत, भविष्य वर्तमान (तीनों कालों में होने वाले) ज्ञान, अर्थात् महर्षि पराशर की त्रिकालज्ञता द्वारा उनकी सारी बातों को सुन सुनकर अत्यन्त चकित हुए साथ ही अपने पूर्वजों की उत्तम गुणगौरवादि सुन कर तो और अधिक चकित हो ही उठे। ३३
English NotesAI-assisted translation
The sage Mṛkaṇḍu, hearing all that Maharṣi Parāśara said through his knowledge of past, future and present (what happens in all three times), that is, through Maharṣi Parāśara's knowledge of the three times, was very amazed; and on hearing the excellent virtues and glory of his own ancestors he became more amazed still. (33)
