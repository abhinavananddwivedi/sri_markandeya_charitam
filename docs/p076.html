Hearing his words, which gathered up knowledge of past, future and present, born of time, Mṛkaṇḍu was amazed and began to speak words befitting the virtues of his ancestors. (33)

English NotesAI-assisted translation
The sage Mṛkaṇḍu, hearing all that Maharṣi Parāśara said through his knowledge of past, future and present (what happens in all three times), that is, through Maharṣi Parāśara's knowledge of the three times, was very amazed; and on hearing the excellent virtues and glory of his own ancestors he became more amazed still. (33)
