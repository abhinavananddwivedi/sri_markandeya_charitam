Verse 45
Shlokamanuensis's hand
अतो द्विज! त्वां प्रति नोपदिश्यते तथैव शिष्यानुपदेष्टुमर्हसि । प्रवृत्तिमार्गे प्रथमो महामुने! भुवि त्वमाचार्यपदेऽभिषिच्यसे ॥ ४५ ॥
Manuscript NoteAI-assisted transcription
वृत्त: वंशस्थ। पाद २ "शिष्यानुपदेष्टुमर्हसि" (= शिष्यान् उपदेष्टुम्; छन्द शुद्ध)। पाद ३ "प्रवृत्तिमार्गे" (पूर्व पाठ "प्रकृतिमार्ग" भ्रान्त था); पाद २ का पूर्व पाठ "शिष्यांस्त्वमुपदिक्ष महोदया" भी भ्रान्त था।
Manuscript Note (EN)AI-assisted transcription
Metre: Vaṃśastha. Pāda 2 reads "शिष्यानुपदेष्टुमर्हसि" (= शिष्यान् उपदेष्टुम्; it scans). Pāda 3 reads "प्रवृत्तिमार्गे" (the earlier "प्रकृतिमार्ग" was wrong); the earlier pāda 2, "शिष्यांस्त्वमुपदिक्ष महोदया", was also wrong.
English Translation (of the Sanskrit)AI-assisted translation
"So, twice-born one, you are not [being] taught; you are yourself fit to teach disciples in the same way. Great sage! On earth you are consecrated to the office of first teacher of the path of active life (pravṛtti)." (45)

English Translation (of the Hindi)AI-assisted translation
"Twice-born one! You are not being taught; you are yourself able to teach disciples in the same way. Great sage! First, on this earth, we consecrate you as teacher in the office of the path of active life (pravṛtti)." (45)

English NotesAI-assisted translation
"So, best of the twice-born! We are giving you no teaching; you yourself are able to teach disciples properly. Great sage Mṛkaṇḍu! Today, having duly consecrated you on this earth to the office of first teacher of the path of active life, we bless you to conduct this simple path of active life as prescribed, and, wishing you well, we hope that you will constantly support and organise it." (45)
Verse 46
Shlokamanuensis's hand
त्वयोपनेया वटवो द्विजन्मनां तथा विनेयाश्च भवेयुरेव ते । य आप्लुता मार्गमिमं सुखप्रदं निवस्तुमर्हन्ति तवोपदेशतः ॥ ४६ ॥
Manuscript NoteAI-assisted transcription
वृत्त: वंशस्थ (चारों पाद शुद्ध)। पूर्व पाठ में पाद २ नहीं था, और पाद ३ "पुरस्कृता" भ्रान्त था (पाण्डुलिपि: "सुखप्रदं")।
Manuscript Note (EN)AI-assisted transcription
Metre: Vaṃśastha (all four pādas scan). The earlier reading lacked pāda 2, and its pāda 3 ending "पुरस्कृता" was wrong (the manuscript has "सुखप्रदं").
English Translation (of the Sanskrit)AI-assisted translation
"You are to give the sacred thread to the boys of the twice-born, and they should indeed become well disciplined. Those who are steeped in this joy-giving path are fit to dwell [here] by your teaching." (46)
Translationamanuensis's hand
आप द्विजन्मों के बालकों को उपनीत करें वे आपके द्वारा विनयी बनें। आपके उपदेश से जो इस मार्ग पर आरूढ़ [होंगे], वे ही निवास के योग्य होंगे ॥ ४६ ॥
English Translation (of the Hindi)AI-assisted translation
"Perform the upanayana of the boys of the twice-born; let them become disciplined through you. Those who [will] take up this path through your teaching, they alone will be worthy of dwelling [here]." (46)
Notesgrandfather's hand
अब आप द्विजन्मा (ब्राह्मणवटुकों) का सविधि उपनयन संस्कार सम्पादित करें, आप द्वारा सदुपदिष्ट वे द्विजवटुक विनयशील एवं शालीनता के प्रतीक बनें, वे इस अत्यन्त सुखप्रदान करने वाले इस प्रवृत्ति मार्ग पर आस्थावान बनकर आपके सदुपदेश से इस भूतल पर रहते हुए प्रवृत्तिमार्ग की सरस एवं प्रेरणाप्रद बनकर रहते हुए इसके संवर्धन संरक्षण में सतत प्रवृत्त होवें। ४६
English NotesAI-assisted translation
"Now duly perform the upanayana rite for the twice-born (brāhmaṇa boys). Well taught by you, may those twice-born boys become models of discipline and good conduct. Gaining faith in this most joy-giving path of active life, may they, living on this earth by your good teaching, become [exemplars] of the path of active life, delightful and inspiring, and remain constantly engaged in its growth and protection." (46)
