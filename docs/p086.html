Manuscript Note (EN)AI-assisted transcription
Paleographic / Textual Note: This marks the climactic concluding narrative verse of Canto 3. Having received the blessing, cow, companion sage, and directions, Sage Mṛkaṇḍu bows with joy, rapturous horripilation (pulakita-aṅga), and spends the wondrous night reflecting on the auspicious fulfillment of his divine mission.
English Translation (of the Sanskrit)AI-assisted translation
Having spoken thus, he went to cleanse himself, bowed gladly, his body thrilling and his mind on his own task; full of many thoughts, absorbed [unclear], his enjoyment of comfort gone, he slowly passed the night in sleep. [Partly corrupt.] (52)

English Translation (of the Hindi)AI-assisted translation
[The Hindi as transcribed is partly garbled.] Thus, bowed by the excellent sage Parāśara's command [unclear: "संवत्स"] (or his kindness), with joy in his trembling mind and his limbs thrilling, staying awake, absorbed in reflecting on obtaining a fine hermitage for himself, free of fear, the sage Mṛkaṇḍu passed the night like everyone else (was glad to lie down). English NotesAI-assisted translation
Thus, respectfully bowing his head to the wish-fulfilling, delightful words of the excellent sage Parāśara, the sage Mṛkaṇḍu, his body thrilling and his hair standing on end, thinking with affection, spent the whole night with sleepy eyes. Constantly thinking over in his mind the various things Maharṣi Parāśara had said, and ever sinking and rising in the feeling of attaining his wishes, the sage Mṛkaṇḍu, with a calm mind [unclear: "विस्मृति"], passed the whole long night as if it were a moment. (52)
