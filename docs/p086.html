English Translation (of the Sanskrit)AI-assisted translation
Having spoken thus, he went to cleanse himself, bowed gladly, his body thrilling and his mind on his own task; full of many thoughts, absorbed [unclear], his enjoyment of comfort gone, he slowly passed the night in sleep. [Partly corrupt.] (52)

English Translation (of the Hindi)AI-assisted translation
[The Hindi as transcribed is partly garbled.] Thus, bowed by the excellent sage Parāśara's command [unclear: "संवत्स"] (or his kindness), with joy in his trembling mind and his limbs thrilling, staying awake, absorbed in reflecting on obtaining a fine hermitage for himself, free of fear, the sage Mṛkaṇḍu passed the night like everyone else (was glad to lie down). English NotesAI-assisted translation
Thus, respectfully bowing his head to the wish-fulfilling, delightful words of the excellent sage Parāśara, the sage Mṛkaṇḍu, his body thrilling and his hair standing on end, thinking with affection, spent the whole night with sleepy eyes. Constantly thinking over in his mind the various things Maharṣi Parāśara had said, and ever sinking and rising in the feeling of attaining his wishes, the sage Mṛkaṇḍu, with a calm mind [unclear: "विस्मृति"], passed the whole long night as if it were a moment. (52)

श्रीचन्द्रभूषणसुधीसुतकेन बच्चू- रामस्य पौत्रसुभगेन नवंगुणाढ्यम् । संगुम्फितं सुमनसां मनसाऽङ्घ्रियुग्म- मानम्य तेन हि शिवस्य सुशिष्यकेण ॥ ५३ ॥ धानेश्वरीहृदयजेन शिवार्चकेन विद्वज्जनार्चनरता धरणीधरेण । काव्यं सुमङ्गलमयं सुप्रवृत्तिपूर्णं शश्वत् समा विजयतां शिवयोः प्रसादात् ॥ ५४ ॥ दीर्घजीविचरितामृतकाव्ये सद्द्विजोचितसदाचरणाढ्ये । एष तेन रचिते [unclear] सर्ग आशु गमितोऽस्ति तृतीयः ॥ ५५ ॥

Manuscript Note (EN)AI-assisted transcription
Metre: verses 53–54 Vasantatilakā, verse 55 Svāgatā. This is the same colophon triad as on pages 32 and 118; it was read from page 86 and divided into pādas to match them (the earlier "बन्धुपौत्रेण / ज्ञानेश्वरी चरणयोजन / संकाव्यं सुसङ्गलमयं…" was wrong). The last pāda of verse 55, "सर्ग आशु गमितोऽस्ति तृतीयः", scans as Svāgatā; the second half of the third pāda is unclear. The page has no Sanskrit "इति … सर्गः समाप्तः" rubric: the triad is followed by the amanuensis's "विशेष" note and "यहाँ तृतीय सर्ग पूर्ण हुआ". The rubric the earlier transcript had added has been removed.
English Translation (of the Sanskrit)AI-assisted translation
(53) By the son of the wise Śrī Candrabhūṣaṇa, the fortunate grandson of Baccū Rāma, this [poem], rich in the nine virtues, was woven, after bowing in his mind to the feet of the good-hearted, by that good disciple of Śiva. (54) By Dharaṇīdhara, born of the heart of Dhāneśvarī, worshipper of Śiva, devoted to honouring the learned: may this poem, full of auspiciousness and good purpose, be victorious for endless years by the grace of Śiva and Pārvatī. (55) In the poem "The Nectar of the Life of the Long-Lived", rich in the good conduct proper to good brahmins, composed by him [unclear], this third canto has swiftly been brought to its end.
