English NotesAI-assisted translation
Then the two, man and woman, joined both hands before their revered father, the Creator, and asked for their work and their names: "Father! What will our names be? And what will our work be?" Hearing this, the grandfather, the first creator, said: "Child! Your name will be Manu and this woman's name Śatarūpā. From now on you must carry out the new creation through union." (13)

English NotesAI-assisted translation
Thus the Creator, having ordered the couple to carry out human creation through union, became busy with the worry of where creation should take place. He was absorbed in various thoughts about bringing the submerged earth out of Pātāla when suddenly he sneezed, and from his mouth the strange form of a boar (varāha) appeared before him. Seeing it, the Creator was overjoyed and fully satisfied. (14)
