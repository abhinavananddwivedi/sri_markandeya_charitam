English NotesAI-assisted translation
Thus, although he kept striving constantly to run creation, his creation as a whole could not go forward; he became anxious about the growth of creation and, for making his matchless creation, took recourse to the one means, austerity, and remained absorbed in austerity for endless years. This went on for endless time; the merit of the austerity came to fruit, and his body divided into two parts: from the right part the man Manu appeared, and from the left part the woman Śatarūpā. (11)

प्रकृति की अन्तःप्रेरणा से समुत्पन्न उस युगल के अन्तर्मन में स्वतः आकस्मिक मनसिज (अर्थात् मन से ही स्वतः उत्पन्न होने वाली अनन्त अभिलाषा जो किसी भी चैतन्य को अथवा अचेतन को भी परस्पर संश्लिष्ट होकर अभिनव संरचना के लिए प्रेरित करती है, जिसे सामान्य रूप से 'काम' पद से अभिहित करते हैं, उसे इच्छा, अभिलाषा भी कह सकते हैं जो प्राणिमात्र का एकान्त गुण है) मनोभव उत्पन्न हो गया, उस काम से परस्पर सम्पृक्त होकर मैथुनी (युगल जोड़ी) दो प्राणी परस्पर मिल आत्मसंयम द्वारा नयी संरचना करने के लिए उद्यत हो उठे, जिसे देखकर प्रजापति चतुर्मुख स्रष्टा प्रसन्न हो उठे और निश्चय किया कि प्रजा उत्पादन की मेरी अद्भुत कृति पूर्णता प्राप्त करेगी, यों अपनी क्रिया की पूर्ण सफलता से विधाता प्रसन्न और पूर्ण निश्चिन्त हो गये। १२
English NotesAI-assisted translation
In the inner mind of that couple, born of the inner prompting of Nature, there suddenly arose of itself Manobhava, "the mind-born" (that is, the endless longing that arises of itself from the mind and moves anything, conscious or unconscious, to join with another for a new creation; it is generally called by the word "kāma", and may also be called desire or longing; it is the one quality common to all creatures). Joined to each other through that desire, the two creatures, a couple, came together and, through self-restraint, became ready to make a new creation. Seeing this, Prajāpati, the four-faced creator, was pleased and decided that his wonderful work of producing creatures would reach completion; so, with the full success of his work, the Creator was pleased and wholly free of worry. (12)
