Paleographic / Textual Note: In line 1, the amanuensis writes `ऋचिवांच्च मधुरं स्वरान्वितं`, which stands for `ऊचिवांश्च मधुरं स्वरान्वितं`. Line 2 notes the transparent, unblemished purity of Parāśara’s heart (nirvyalīkam). Meter: Rathoddhatā [– ⏑ – | ⏑ ⏑ ⏑ | – ⏑ – | ⏑ –]

And having found [his] mind free of guile, he spoke sweetly, in a tuneful voice: "Noble one, where your kindness falls, and your full gaze, what more is there to say of that?" (49)

English Translation (of the Hindi)AI-assisted translation
Having received the great sage's sweet, tuneful words and his guileless, clear mind, he said: "Noble one! Where your kind gaze falls, what need is there to speak of it?" (49)

English NotesAI-assisted translation
Constantly reflecting in his heart on the very charming, guileless and fully generous nature of Maharṣi Parāśara, with his very sweet voice, the sage Mṛkaṇḍu said: "Lord, where the gaze of your full kindness falls, what can be said of that place? That is, what is not possible through your favour?" (49)
