इत्युदीर्य विरते किल तस्मि- न्नत्युदारहृदये मुनिवर्ये । वाचमाकलयता रुचिरार्थां शक्तिजेन गदितं स्मितपूर्वम् ॥ ३९ ॥

वृत्त: स्वागता।

English NotesAI-assisted translation
When the most generous, eminent sage Mudgala had finished speaking and fallen quiet, Maharṣi Parāśara, the son of Śakti, began to speak thus, with a most gracious, very kindly, gentle smile. (39)
Verse 40
Shlokamanuensis's hand
स्वीकृतं सकलमेव मनःस्थं यद्भवद्भिरुदितं सुविभाव्य । यौतके विवदतां मनुजानां वेद्मि हन्त वरविक्रयमेव ॥ ४० ॥
Manuscript NoteAI-assisted transcription
वृत्त: स्वागता। पाद १ "स्वीकृतं सकलमेव मनःस्थं", पाद २ "...सुविभाव्य" (पूर्व निष्कासन का "निजमनोभवमन्त्रं" तथा "सुविचार्या" भ्रान्त थे)। पाद ३ "यौतके" (हस्तलेख)।
Manuscript Note (EN)AI-assisted transcription
Metre: Svāgatā. Pāda 1 reads "स्वीकृतं सकलमेव मनःस्थं" and pāda 2 "...सुविभाव्य"; the earlier export's "निजमनोभवमन्त्रं" and "सुविचार्या" were misreadings. Pāda 3 has "यौतके" (manuscript).
English Translation (of the Sanskrit)AI-assisted translation
"All that is in your heart, which you have spoken after careful thought, is accepted. Alas, among men who quarrel over dowry, I see nothing but the selling of the groom." (40)

आपलोगों ने निश्चय कर जो कुछ कहा उस मनोभाव को मैंने स्वीकार किया। दहेज के लिए विवाद करने वाले मनोभाव तो मुझे जान पड़ता है यह विवाह नहीं अपितु वर को बेचना ही है ॥ ४० ॥
English Translation (of the Hindi)AI-assisted translation
"I have accepted the intention you have expressed after deciding. As for the attitude of those who quarrel over dowry, it seems to me that this is not marriage but simply selling the groom." (40)

English NotesAI-assisted translation
"Best of sages! I have gladly accepted what you have very carefully settled in your mind about carrying out the marriage. For when people argue (debate) over dowry (yautuka), discussing more or less, it seems to me just like selling the groom." (40)
