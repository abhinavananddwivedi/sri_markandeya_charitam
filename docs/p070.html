Metre: Rathoddhatā (all four pādas scan). Pāda 2 reads "न्मेरुदण्डसरलीकृतानथ" (the earlier "द्व्यक्तमेरुदण्डसरलीकृतान्" was wrong).
English Translation (of the Sanskrit)AI-assisted translation
Some seated in the bound lotus posture, some with the spine made straight, their eyes closed in meditation, beholding within their hearts a light of surpassing brilliance. (20)

Here he saw yogis seated firmly in the lotus posture (kamalāsana), there ascetics aligning their spinal columns in straight yogic posture, and elsewhere others with eyes gently closed in meditation, beholding the intensely radiant Divine Light within the sanctuary of their hearts.
