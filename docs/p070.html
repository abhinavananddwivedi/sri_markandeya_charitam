Verse 19
Shlokamanuensis's hand
क्वापि मज्जनपरान् बहूंस्ततो आसनास्तरणतत्परान् क्वचित् । स्वस्तिकैरितिकराँश्च शोभनैः क्वापि तूपस्थितिपरान् विवस्वतः ॥ १९ ॥
Translationamanuensis's hand
कहीं पर उन्होंने ब्रह्मचारियों को स्नान करते, कहीं पर आसनों को बिछाते, कहीं पर स्वस्तिक रचना करते तथा कहीं पर उपस्थित करते (विवस्वतः - सूर्योपस्थान) देखा ॥ १९ ॥
English TranslationAI-assisted translation
He saw many bathing in the holy currents, others busily spreading meditation seats, some drawing auspicious svastika motifs with sacred materials, and elsewhere ascetics engaged in the midday worship of the Sun God (Vivasvat).

English NotesAI-assisted translation
Within the vibrant hermitage, he witnessed young brahmacārī novices taking their holy dip, others arranging mats and seats for revered preceptors, some fashioning auspicious svastika designs for holy rituals, and others deeply absorbed in the midday adoration (sūryopasthāna) of Lord Āditya, the Supreme Radiant Sun.
Verse 20
Shlokamanuensis's hand
क्वापि बद्धकमलासनान् क्वचि- द्व्यक्तमेरुदण्डसरलीकृतान् । ध्यानमीलितदृशो हृदन्तरे ज्योतिरुज्ज्वलतरं विपश्यतः ॥ २० ॥
Translationamanuensis's hand
कहीं पर उन्होंने कमलासन बाँधे हुए, कहीं पर मेरुदण्ड को सरल करते हुए, कहीं पर ध्यानमग्न हो हृदयान्तर में उज्ज्वल ज्योति को देखते हुए उन्हें देखा ॥ २० ॥
English TranslationAI-assisted translation
Here he saw yogis seated firmly in the lotus posture (kamalāsana), there ascetics aligning their spinal columns in straight yogic posture, and elsewhere others with eyes gently closed in meditation, beholding the intensely radiant Divine Light within the sanctuary of their hearts.
Notesgrandfather's hand
उन्होने कहीं पर कमलासन लगाये हुए, कहीं पर मेरुदण्ड की स्वस्थता के लिए सीधा करने का आसन सम्पादन करते हुए, कहीं अन्तःकरण में ध्यान की स्थिति में दृष्टि निमीलन द्वारा परम समुज्ज्वल सुदीप्त ज्योति का दर्शन करते हुए वहाँ आश्रम वासियों को देखा । २०
English NotesAI-assisted translation
He observed the dwellers of the hermitage engaged in diverse spiritual disciplines: some seated firmly in the padmāsana posture, others practicing erect alignment of the spinal cord (merudaṇḍa) for physical and pranic vitality, and others in deep contemplative absorption with eyes softly closed, perceiving the effulgent, transcendental Divine Light within their inner chambers.
