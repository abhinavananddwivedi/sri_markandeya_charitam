Verse 33

विमृश्य को येउति जयं विलक्षितः पुरन्दरो इल्याह्रततां न गर्दिशीम् । नमून सर्वाप्सरसां शिरोमणि र्हिदन्यथातत्य परं स्थलोमगम् ॥ ३३ ॥
Translationamanuensis's hand
क्रोध का भी जय है, यह विचार कर विस्मित हुए इन्द्र ने श्रेष्ठ उर्वशी का आदर किया। तब से उर्वशी अप्सराओं की शिरोमणि अर्थात् सर्वश्रेष्ठ हो गई। क्योंकि उसने इन्द्र के हृदय पर भी अपने सौन्दर्य का साम्राज्य स्थापित कर लिया ॥ ३३ ॥
English TranslationAI-assisted translation
Reflecting that anger had been utterly subdued by supreme forbearance, a wondrous Indra received the exquisite Urvaśī with deep reverence. Thenceforth, Urvaśī was hailed as the crest-jewel of all celestial damsels, establishing the sovereign empire of her celestial grace over the very heart of the King of the Gods.

English NotesAI-assisted translation
Though Indra had sent the apsaras to humiliate the meditating seers and fulfill his selfish designs, Lord Nārāyaṇa responded without a trace of wrath, presenting him with Urvaśī, whose peerless celestial beauty outshone all the heavenly damsels. Taking her as a divine gift, Indra revered her with profound honor; and by establishing her enchanting sway over the heavenly court and Indra's own heart, Urvaśī was exalted as the supreme jewel among all the apsaras.

इति श्रुतं विश्वजयेन गर्वित पराजित दैत्यमुते प्रभावतः । इषीकया चापविमुक्तया पुरा स्तुतौ युवां तेन पुनः प्रलादितौ ॥ ३४ ॥
Translationamanuensis's hand
विश्वविजय से गर्वोन्मत्त दैत्यसुत धनुष से छोड़े लोकबाण के प्रभाव से पराजित हुआ था यह सुना था, बाद में उन्होंने आप दोनों की स्तुति कर प्रसन्न किया था ॥ ३४ ॥
English TranslationAI-assisted translation
'It is also known that in ancient days, the demon prince drunk with the pride of conquering the universe was humbled by the potent reed-arrow released from your celestial bow; struck down, he sought refuge at your feet, offering hymns of praise until your compassionate hearts granted him pardon.'

English NotesAI-assisted translation
'It is famously recounted in the scriptures that when the demon prince became intoxicated with the ambition of universal conquest, you channeled supreme spiritual power, placing a single energized reed upon your bow and casting it. Struck down and shattered in pride, he hurried back to take shelter at your lotus feet, glorifying you with single-minded devotion until, moved with divine compassion, you graciously granted him forgiveness and peace.'
