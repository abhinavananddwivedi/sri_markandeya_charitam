English Translation (of the Sanskrit)AI-assisted translation
Reflecting that there was victory even in [the sages'] anger, Purandara (Indra), abashed, honoured the excellent Urvaśī. She became the crest-jewel of all the apsarases, and set her own beauty's foothold in his heart. (33)

Thinking, "There is victory even in anger", the abashed Indra honoured the excellent Urvaśī. From then on Urvaśī became the crest-jewel of the apsarases, that is, the very best, for she established the empire of her beauty even over Indra's heart. (33)

English NotesAI-assisted translation
Although Indra, king of the gods, had sent the apsarases to insult the sage, so that his wish would be fulfilled, the primal sage, even in anger, gave him Urvaśī, more matchlessly beautiful than even his apsarases. On receiving her, the king of the gods took it as his own victory; even though he had fallen short of his aim, he respectfully honoured Urvaśī, foremost of the divine women, whose beauty surpassed all, made her the foremost, the crest-jewel, among all the apsarases, and considered himself blessed, for she had established her empire even over the heart of the king of the gods. (33)
