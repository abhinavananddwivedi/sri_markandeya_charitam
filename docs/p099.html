Verse 25
Shlokamanuensis's hand
इत्युदीरितवति प्रणयेन भार्गवः समुचितं समुवाच । शक्तिजेन मुनिना न विनाऽह- मुत्सहे परवशः खलु वक्तुम् ॥ २५ ॥
Manuscript NoteAI-assisted transcription
वृत्त: स्वागता। पाद ३ "शक्तिजेन मुनिना न विनाऽहं-" और पाद ४ "-मुत्सहे" (हस्तलेख "भुत्सहे" जैसा)। पूर्व निष्कासन का "नोत्सहे" दोहरा निषेध बनाकर अर्थ उलट देता था।
Manuscript Note (EN)AI-assisted transcription
Metre: Svāgatā. Pāda 3 reads "शक्तिजेन मुनिना न विनाऽहं-" and pāda 4 "-मुत्सहे" (the manuscript looks like "भुत्सहे"). The earlier export's "नोत्सहे" created a double negative and reversed the sense.
English Translation (of the Sanskrit)AI-assisted translation
When he had spoken thus with affection, the descendant of Bhṛgu replied fittingly: "Without the sage born of Śakti (Parāśara), I cannot venture to speak, being indeed dependent on another." (25)

English Translation (of the Hindi)AI-assisted translation
When Mudgala had spoken with affection, Mṛkaṇḍu of the line of Bhṛgu gave a fitting reply: "I am not able to marry without the consent of Parāśara, the son of Śakti; being dependent, I am obliged to say so." (25)

English NotesAI-assisted translation
When he had spoken thus affectionately, the sage Mṛkaṇḍa of Bhṛgu's line began, with great courtesy, to say these fitting words: "O sage! My guardian is none other than Maharṣi Parāśara, the son of Śakti. Without his direction I cannot marry, or even speak of it; this is my constraint. Please understand." (25)

English NotesAI-assisted translation
Through his dear disciples they were received with due honour and lodged in excellent quarters, where the very best food and refreshments were arranged for them. In this way the guests who had come were honoured fittingly, with great joy and in various ways. (26)
