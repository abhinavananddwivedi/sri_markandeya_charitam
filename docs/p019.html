English NotesAI-assisted translation
"Lord! I, an ignorant man, have not the least knowledge of the present state of place and time, nor have I been able to form any settled idea of how my life ahead should go. Lord! On this earth I have not acquired even the least power. So, to remove my ignorance of every kind, I have come to take refuge with you two." (37)

English Translation (of the Sanskrit)AI-assisted translation
"The son born of Hiraṇyagarbha's skin was Bhṛgu; from him was born the one named Dhātā; from him I am Mṛkaṇḍu, that descendant of Bhṛgu. I propitiate you two, my teachers, and salute you." (38)

English NotesAI-assisted translation
