Verse 47
Shlokamanuensis's hand
निश्चितं वरणके समये सोऽ- तीव सान्निकटगं सुविभाव्य । प्राप्य सद्म भगवन्तमभीष्टं मुद्गलो हृदयतोऽनुजुहाव ॥ ४७ ॥
Manuscript NoteAI-assisted transcription
वृत्त: स्वागता। पाद १ "वरणके" में संशोधन है; पाद २ "सान्निकटगं" (अतीव समीप; अनुवाद "अतीव समीप" से पुष्ट)। पूर्व निष्कासन का "वरणमेव समये सोऽ- / तीव सानन्दकरं" भ्रान्त था।
Manuscript Note (EN)AI-assisted transcription
Metre: Svāgatā. Pāda 1 "वरणके" contains a correction; pāda 2 reads "सान्निकटगं" ("very near", confirmed by the Hindi "अतीव समीप"). The earlier export's "वरणमेव समये सोऽ- / तीव सानन्दकरं" was a misreading.
English Translation (of the Sanskrit)AI-assisted translation
Realising that the appointed time for the betrothal (varaṇa) was very near, Mudgala reached his home and invoked his chosen Lord from the heart. (47)

English NotesAI-assisted translation
On reaching home, Sage Mudgala, knowing that the time for the wedding preparations and the betrothal (varaṇa) was now very close, meditated on his chosen deity from the heart, offered praise with reverence, and kept calling on him for the fulfilment of his wish. (47)

English NotesAI-assisted translation
Through Sage Mudgala's great faith and devotion towards God, [unclear: all] the provisions for the betrothal were ready of their own accord, just as he wished, while he looked on. Seeing these provisions, which were finer than he could imagine and beyond his hopes, the sage and his family were overjoyed. (48)
