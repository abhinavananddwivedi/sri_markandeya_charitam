Somewhere he saw rows of fields with their dykes, waving with grain, giving delight to the farmers, about to bear fruit (with ears of grain), like a pregnant new bride covered in clothes for her husband's joy. (9)

English NotesAI-assisted translation
On this level land the sage Mṛkaṇḍu also saw endless rows of the farmers' fields, divided into small plots, in which crops waving with grain and swaying with ears, giving the farmers great joy, had charmed the farmers' minds. It seemed as if some brand-new, beautiful bride, covered in green clothes, were trying to draw her husband's mind toward herself. (9)

प्रपीय गाङ्गं पय उन्मुखं गृहे दिवा चरित्वा हरितं तृणाङ्कुरम् । गवां कदम्बं स विलोक्य निर्वृत- स्तथा हि मेने वसतिं समीपगाम् ॥ १० ॥
Manuscript NoteAI-assisted transcription
वृत्त: वंशस्थ (चारों पाद शुद्ध)। पाद १ "प्रपीय गाङ्गं" (पूर्व "प्रपाय गाङ्गेयं", एक अक्षर अधिक)।
Manuscript Note (EN)AI-assisted transcription
Metre: Vaṃśastha (all four pādas scan). Pāda 1 reads "प्रपीय गाङ्गं" (the earlier "प्रपाय गाङ्गेयं" has one syllable too many).
English Translation (of the Sanskrit)AI-assisted translation
Seeing a herd of cows that had grazed on green shoots of grass by day, drunk the Gaṅgā's water and were turning homeward, he was content, and thought a dwelling place was near. (10)
Translationamanuensis's hand
दिन में हरित घासों को चर कर गङ्गाजल पीकर घर जाने के उन्मुख (उद्यत) गायों के समूह को देखकर अत्यन्त सुखी हो उन्होंने निश्चित ही समीप में गाँव होगा ऐसा माना ॥ १० ॥
English Translation (of the Hindi)AI-assisted translation
Seeing a herd of cows, which had grazed on green grass in the day and drunk Gaṅgā water and were ready to go home, he was very happy and concluded that there must surely be a village nearby. (10)
Notesgrandfather's hand
दिन भर गङ्गा के तट पर रहने वाली हरी २ घासों को चरकर सान्ध्यवेला में परम पावनी भगवती गङ्गा का पवित्र निर्मल जल पीकर अपने निवास स्थान की ओर जाने के लिए उत्सुक गायों के समूह को देखकर मुनि अत्यन्त प्रसन्न हुए और निकट में ही किसी वसति (ग्राम) होने की सम्भावना भी समझी। १०
English NotesAI-assisted translation
Seeing a herd of cows that had grazed all day on the green grass of the Gaṅgā's bank and, at evening, had drunk the holy, clear water of the most purifying goddess Gaṅgā, and were eager to go to their dwelling, the sage was very pleased, and also understood that there was likely some settlement (village) close by. (10)
