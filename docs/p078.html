English Translation (of the Sanskrit)AI-assisted translation
"Taught by the great sage Nara, and seen on the way by the southerner (Dākṣi), know me to have come to you, intent on taking up that path of action and to join myself to it." (36)

English NotesAI-assisted translation
"Lord! What the most revered Maharṣi Nārada [cf. the Sanskrit "नारेण", Nara] told me, and what the sage Dākṣi again urged me to carry out on the road as I came, I want to join myself again to that same path of action for its full rise. So understand that I have come to you only for the growth of that path." (36)
