सन्निधाय च शिरः प्रदेशके वारिपूर्णकलशं सकटोरम् । सन्निवेश्य भगवत्स्मृतिं हृदि मातुरङ्कपतितो यथा शिशुः ॥ १३ ॥

And placing at his head a pot full of water with a bowl, and setting the remembrance of the Lord in his heart, [he slept] like a child that has fallen asleep in its mother's lap. (13)

English NotesAI-assisted translation
After this the sage Mṛkaṇḍa covered a full pot of water with a bronze bowl and placed it at his head; remembering the Lord, just as a child sleeps peacefully and without worry in its mother's lap, so he slept, settled and calm, in the lap of the goddess Earth, who is like a mother. (13)

निद्रया व्यपगतस्मृतिर्बभौ सत्समाधिमिव शीलयन्मुनिः । अरुणे समुदञ्चिते पुरो जाग्रतः स्व नियमं चकार सः ॥ १४ ॥
English Translation (of the Sanskrit)AI-assisted translation
His awareness lost in sleep, the sage shone as if practising true samādhi; when the dawn rose before him, he woke and performed his own observances. (14)

निद्रा से लुप्त स्मृति वाले महात्मा सत् समाधि का अभ्यास करता हुआ दीप्तिमान हो गये । आगे, अरुणोभा छा जाने पर जागकर अपने सन्ध्यावन्दनादि किये ॥ १४ ॥
English Translation (of the Hindi)AI-assisted translation
The great soul, his awareness lost in sleep, became radiant as though practising true samādhi. Then, when the glow of dawn spread, he woke and performed his sandhyā worship and other rites. (14)
Notesgrandfather's hand
यद्यपि मुनि मृकण्ड गहरी नींद में पूर्ण सो गये थे, उनकी स्मृतियाँ समाप्त हो गई थीं, लगता था कि मुनि अत्यन्त तेजोदीप्त उदात्ततम समाधि की अवस्था में समाहित हो गये हैं, प्रातःकाल अरुणोभा के पूर्व ब्राह्मवेला में ही उठकर प्रात्याहिक दैनन्दिन सन्ध्यावन्दनादि का कार्य सविधि सम्पन्न किया । १४
English NotesAI-assisted translation
Although the sage Mṛkaṇḍa had fallen fully into deep sleep and his awareness had ceased, it seemed that the sage was absorbed in a very radiant, most exalted state of samādhi. In the morning, before the glow of dawn, he rose at the brāhma hour and duly completed his daily morning sandhyā worship and other rites. (14)
