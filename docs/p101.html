English Translation (of the Sanskrit)AI-assisted translation
When the best of sages (Parāśara) arrived the next day, all the sages assembled; and he (Mṛkaṇḍu), having received them kindly, promptly took a pleasant, secluded seat. (29)

When his matter was first put before the assembly, the eminent sage Mudgala said: "O knower of all, past and future! What should be said by me? It does not occur to me." (30)

English Translation (of the Hindi)AI-assisted translation
When the subject of the marriage was first put before the assembly, the eminent sage Mudgala said: "It does not seem proper to me that I should say anything before those who know the three times, past, future and present." (30)
Notesgrandfather's hand
महर्षि पराशर के आने के बाद सभी महर्षियों की एक सभा बैठी जिसमें महर्षि मुद्गल के कन्या परिणय की चर्चा प्रारम्भ हुई, उस समय मुनिश्रेष्ठ मुद्गल ऋषि ने कहा, भगवन्! आप भूत भविष्य वर्तमान तीनों कालों के सर्वश्रेष्ठ ज्ञाता हैं अतः मैं आपके समक्ष क्या कहूँ स्वयं नहीं समझ पाता। ३०
English NotesAI-assisted translation
After Maharṣi Parāśara arrived, an assembly of all the great sages sat, in which the discussion of the marriage of Maharṣi Mudgala's daughter began. Then the best of sages, the ṛṣi Mudgala, said: "Lord! You are the foremost knower of all three times, past, future and present; so I myself do not know what I should say before you." (30)
