Verse 11
Shlokamanuensis's hand
गां बबन्धा वटमूल सन्ततौ दृश्यमेकादिशि रम्यमासनम् । शिरोदिशेकमनु कम्बल्योत्तरं कौशमास्तृण शयनाय संस्तरे ॥ ११ ॥
Translationamanuensis's hand
बरगद के जड़ों में गाय को बाँधा, एक वत्स को बाँधा, एक तरफ सुन्दर आसन या शिर के ओर कम्बल तथा अपने शयनार्थ कुशा का बिस्तर बिछाया ॥ ११ ॥
English TranslationAI-assisted translation
He tethered the cow (and calf) firmly amidst the network of banyan roots, and prepared a pleasant, tranquil seat in one direction. Then, arranging a blanket beneath towards the head, he spread out an auspicious bed of sacred kuśa grass for restful sleep.
Notesgrandfather's hand
ऋषि ने, सामने स्थित विशाल वटवृक्ष के जड़ में गाँय को सविधि बाँधा तथा एक ओर चित्तवृत्ति को पूर्ण शान्त स्वस्थ रखने वाला मनोहर आसन बनाया, साथ ही अपने शयन के लिए पूर्व सिरान्हा कर नीचे कम्बलासन पर उत्तम कुशासन का बिस्तर भी लगाया । ११
English NotesAI-assisted translation
The sage duly tethered the sacred cow at the roots of the grand banyan tree standing before him, and arranged a delightful seat on one side that fostered absolute stillness and poise of the mind. Furthermore, for his nightly rest, keeping his head oriented towards the east, he laid down a comfortable woolen blanket beneath and spread an excellent mattress of sacred kuśa grass over it.
Verse 12
Shlokamanuensis's hand
धौतपादकरपल्लवो मुदा वन्यमभ्यवजहार किञ्चन । संस्मरन्परतरं महो मुनिः सन्दधेऽनु शयनं विधिवतः ॥ १२ ॥
Translationamanuensis's hand
आनन्दपूर्वक हाथ-पैर धोकर कुछ वन्य कन्द-मूल-फल दिखाये (खाये), बाद में महात्मा अतिदिव्य ज्योति का स्मरण करते हुए शयन विधि पूर्ण किया ॥ १२ ॥
English TranslationAI-assisted translation
Joyfully washing his tender hands and feet, the sage partook of simple wild forest roots and fruits. Then, meditating upon the Supreme, transcendent Light of the Divine, the ascetic duly retired to sleep in accordance with sacred ordinance.
Notesgrandfather's hand
इसके बाद ऋषि ने परम आनन्द के साथ हाथ-पैर धोये, वन में सुगमता से प्राप्त होने वाले कन्द मूल-फलादि का भोजन किया, अनन्तर परमात्म प्रभु के दिव्य ज्योति का दर्शन करते हुए शान्त स्वस्थ निश्चिन्त होकर सो गये । १२
English NotesAI-assisted translation
Thereafter, with supreme delight, the sage washed his hands and feet, nourished himself with easily gathered forest tubers, roots, and fruits, and subsequently, beholding in contemplation the resplendent celestial light of the Supreme Lord, went to sleep in calm, composed, and unburdened peace.
