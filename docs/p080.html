English NotesAI-assisted translation
"Dear one! The whole kingdom of this region belongs to King Priyavrata, but you need not go and ask him for land; nor will you have to worry about the various materials for building a house; for capable people, each skilled in his own work, will do it themselves, because all the work of a brahmin is done without any payment." (41)
