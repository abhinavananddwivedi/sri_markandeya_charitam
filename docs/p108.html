Manuscript Note (EN)AI-assisted transcription
Metre: Svāgatā. This verse is in faint ink and several parts are unclear. A word is struck out at the start of pāda 1. The start of pāda 2 is unclear; "गौतमेन" fits the notes' "गौतमीय सहयोगी" and the Hindi's "उस गोत्रीय द्वारा". Pāda 3 "आसने समुपवेश्य मृकण्डुं" is clear. Pāda 4 reads "तं ...ऽभितो विविशुस्ते" (Hindi: "people sat around him"). The earlier export's "गोप्रदानैर्विधिवद् गोदानैः" is not in the manuscript.

English NotesAI-assisted translation
When Parāśara, the son of Śakti, had spoken thus, that sage, the esteemed companion of the Gautama line, prepared a very auspicious, finely adorned seat and the like. Having decorated the seat with fine incense, lamps and so on, Sage Mṛkaṇḍu sat on it, and the other sages sat attentively around him, encircling him. (43)

Manuscript NoteAI-assisted transcription
वृत्त: स्वागता। पाद २ "मङ्गलाचरणविधौ स्थिरचित्तः" (अनुवाद: "माङ्गलिक विधान किये जाने पर स्थिरचित्त"); पूर्व निष्कासन का "मङ्गलानि विधिवद् विरचित्य" हस्तलेख में नहीं है। पाद ३ के अन्त में "त=" (योजक चिह्न) है, और "मुद्गलः" से पहले एक अक्षर काटा गया है।
Manuscript Note (EN)AI-assisted transcription
Metre: Svāgatā. Pāda 2 reads "मङ्गलाचरणविधौ स्थिरचित्तः" (Hindi: "when the auspicious rite had been performed, steady-minded"); the earlier export's "मङ्गलानि विधिवद् विरचित्य" is not in the manuscript. Pāda 3 ends "त=" (a hyphen mark), and a letter is struck out before "मुद्गलः".
English Translation (of the Sanskrit)AI-assisted translation
When the auspicious rite had first been performed by the sages led by Dākṣi, Mudgala, steady of mind, with great humility placed that pair of gold coins (niṣka), with fruit, in [the groom's] joined palms. (44)

English NotesAI-assisted translation
The auspicious rites of blessing and the like had already been performed by the sages headed by Dākṣi. Then Sage Mudgala, calm and composed in mind, wishing for blessings, gave one pair of gold coins together with fine fruits and the like, which Sage Mṛkaṇḍu humbly received in the cup of his two hands (accepted). (44)
