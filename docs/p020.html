English NotesAI-assisted translation
When Mṛkaṇḍu, of Bhṛgu's line, had praised them with faith and with guileless devotion, the two primal sages were greatly pleased by his very noble devotion; casting on him their unbroken gracious glance, they began to speak, giving him most beneficial teaching, auspicious and conducive to his rise in this world and in the next. (39)

सदैव सत्यं वद धर्ममाचरे- न्न वै विरामोऽध्ययने भवेत्तव । गुरौ जनन्यां जनके च देवव- त्सदा भवेः पुत्रक वर्त्मनाऽऽत्मनः ॥ ४० ॥

Metre: Vaṃśastha (all four pādas scan). In pāda 1 a word before "सत्यं" is struck out. The earlier reading ("धर्ममाचर / गुरौ अनन्यो / वर्त्तमात्मनः") was wrong.
English Translation (of the Sanskrit)AI-assisted translation

English NotesAI-assisted translation
"Child! Be constantly devoted to speaking the truth; keep observing your own dharma as the rules prescribe; let no break ever come in your study of the Veda with its six auxiliaries (let no obstacle come in your study). Always remain faithful to your elders, and to your honoured, venerable mother and father, regarding them as gods. By this excellent path you will continually prosper greatly." (40)
