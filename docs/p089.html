With his companion, the sage gathered from the people all the materials for building. Wishing to act in accord with divine power, he meditated with an attentive mind. (6)

English Translation (of the Hindi)AI-assisted translation
With his helper, the excellent sage Mṛkaṇḍu brought all the building materials from the people. Wishing to act, holding back divine power [Hindi "रोककर", "holding back", renders Sanskrit "अनुरुध्य", which here more likely means "in accord with"], he meditated with an attentive mind. (6)

English NotesAI-assisted translation
Thus Sage Mṛkaṇḍa, with his companion Sage Āṅgirasa, gathered from the local people all the things needed to complete the building rites for the whole house. Recollecting divine power with a very attentive mind, he considered carefully how to build a new and larger house. (6)
