Translationamanuensis's hand
वे महात्मा मृकण्डु समस्त नित्यकर्म समाप्त कर (आप्तवाक्य यथार्थ वक्ता) यथार्थवादी को पाने के लिए शीघ्रता से दिन के दो प्रहर बीतने पर दिन के मध्य में तापहारी शुभाश्रम में आये ॥ १७ ॥
English TranslationAI-assisted translation
Having completed all his daily duties, he hastened with swift steps to meet that truthful and venerable seer. Traversing the road continuously for two yāmas (six hours), he reached the auspicious hermitage by midday, a sanctuary renowned for dispelling all afflictions.

English NotesAI-assisted translation
Rising very early and completing his routine sacred duties, Sage Mṛkaṇḍu hastened with keen anticipation to meet Maharṣi Parāśara—a beloved spiritual guide and speaker of absolute truth. Walking steadily for two yāmas (six hours) until midday, he arrived at Sage Parāśara's sacred hermitage, an abode eternally tranquil and famed for taking away all worldly sorrow.

दूरतः स किल गोमतीतटे मध्यमाह्निकविधौ सुलम्पटान् । वैष्णवान्मुनिगणान्समन्ततो ब्रह्मचारिनियमान् ददर्श सः ॥ १८ ॥
Translationamanuensis's hand
उन्होंने दूर से गोमती तट पर मध्याह्नकालिक विधि में संलग्न वैष्णव मुनिगणों के तथा चारों ओर ब्रह्मचारियों के नियमों को देखा ॥ १८ ॥
English TranslationAI-assisted translation
From afar along the banks of the sacred Gomatī, he beheld assemblies of Vaiṣṇava sages deeply absorbed in their midday rites, alongside disciplined celibate students (brahmacārīs) everywhere fulfilling their daily sacred observances.

उस मध्याह्न में पवित्र भगवती पुण्य सलिला गोमती के तट पर माध्यन्दिन (मध्याह्नकालिक) सन्ध्या वन्दनादि दैनिक कृत्य में निरत रहने वाले वैष्णव सन्तों, श्रेष्ठों, विविध ऋषिगणों, एवं ब्रह्मचारी वटुकों को भी सम्पादित किये जाने वाले नित्य नियमों को करते हुए दूर से ही देखा । १८
English NotesAI-assisted translation
At that midday hour, upon the banks of the holy and purifying Gomatī, he observed from a distance groups of pious Vaiṣṇava saints, revered elders, and diverse ascetics immersed in their midday (mādhyandina) prayers and oblations, as well as young brahmacārī initiates faithfully conducting their prescribed daily austerities.
