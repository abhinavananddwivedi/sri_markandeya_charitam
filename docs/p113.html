Verse 53
Shlokamanuensis's hand
धन्य एव वरिता यदिमानि प्रेषयत्यनुगतानि समृद्ध्या । सोऽस्य वश्यमधिकर्द्धिरुपेता येषु तानि किल यस्य भवेयुः ॥ ५३ ॥
Manuscript NoteAI-assisted transcription
वृत्त: स्वागता। पाद ३ "सोऽस्य वश्यमधिकर्द्धिरुपेता" ("वश्यम्" = अवश्यम्; अनुवाद "वह अवश्य ही अधिक समृद्धि से पूर्ण होगा")। पाद ४ "येषु तानि किल यस्य भवेयुः" — पाद ३ में "उपेता" के अन्त्य आकार और पाद ४ के "येषु" का व्याकरण कुछ शिथिल है; पाठ हस्तलेख के अनुसार। पूर्व निष्कासन का "सोऽयमप्यधिकर्द्धिरूपेतो / येषु तानि किल वरस्य स्युः" (१० वर्ण) भ्रान्त था।
Manuscript Note (EN)AI-assisted transcription
Metre: Svāgatā. Pāda 3 reads "सोऽस्य वश्यमधिकर्द्धिरुपेता" ("वश्यम्" = avaśyam; Hindi "he must surely be full of greater wealth"). Pāda 4 reads "येषु तानि किल यस्य भवेयुः". The final ā of "उपेता" in pāda 3 and the grammar of "येषु" in pāda 4 are somewhat loose; the text follows the manuscript. The earlier export's "सोऽयमप्यधिकर्द्धिरूपेतो / येषु तानि किल वरस्य स्युः" (10 syllables) was a misreading.
English Translation (of the Sanskrit)AI-assisted translation
"Blessed indeed is the chooser who sends these things, attended by such wealth; and he to whom such things may belong must surely be endowed with greater prosperity." (53)

"Blessed is this chooser, who is sending this, attended by wealth. He who has such equipment must surely be full of even greater wealth." (53)

अरे भाई वह वरण करने वाला बड़ा धनी प्रतापी समृद्ध एवं अत्यन्त उदात्त वृत्ति का होगा और उसकी सम्पत्ति भी तो इसी प्रकार की होगी तभी तो अपनी योग्यतानुरूप इतनी सामग्री भेज रहा है। वर की भी पारिवारिक स्थिति भी तो अतिशय समुन्नत होगी, वह ही पूर्ण समृद्ध धनी मानी एवं सामाजिक प्रतिष्ठापूर्ण ही होगा। तभी तो इतना विशाल सम्भार जा रहा है। ५३
English NotesAI-assisted translation
"Brother, the one making this betrothal must be very rich, powerful, prosperous and of very noble character, and his property too must be of the same kind; that is why he is sending so many things, as befits his standing. The groom's family position, too, must be very high; he must be fully prosperous, wealthy, respected and of high social standing. That is why so vast a consignment is going." (53)
Verse 54
Shlokamanuensis's hand
दर्दरे मुनिवरोऽस्ति मृकण्डु- रष्टसिद्धिसहितो वरणीयः । मुद्गलेन मुनिना हरिभक्ति- शालिना प्रहितमेतदतीव ॥ ५४ ॥
Manuscript NoteAI-assisted transcription
वृत्त: स्वागता।
Manuscript Note (EN)AI-assisted transcription
Metre: Svāgatā.
English Translation (of the Sanskrit)AI-assisted translation
"In Dardara lives the excellent sage Mṛkaṇḍu, possessed of the eight siddhis, who is to be chosen; this abundance has been sent by the sage Mudgala, rich in devotion to Hari." (54)
Translationamanuensis's hand
दर्दर में अष्टसिद्धि सम्पन्न वरणीय महर्षि मृकण्डु हैं, इसीलिए हरिभक्ति शाली मुद्गल ने ये उत्कृष्ट समृद्धिशाली सामग्री प्रेषित किया है ॥ ५४ ॥
English Translation (of the Hindi)AI-assisted translation
"In Dardara is Maharṣi Mṛkaṇḍu, endowed with the eight siddhis, who is to be chosen; that is why Mudgala, rich in devotion to Hari, has sent these excellent, rich provisions." (54)
Notesgrandfather's hand
दर्दर (ददरी बलिया सम्प्रति) में मुनि मृकण्ड नाम के अनन्त तपस्वी अष्टसिद्धियों के स्वामी अत्यन्त प्रतिष्ठित सम्भ्रान्त हैं, उनके वरण करने के लिए भगवान नारायण के एकनिष्ठ भक्त अतिशय उदार मुनि मुद्गल ने यह सारी सामग्री उनके वरण के लिए सम्पादित कर भेज रखी है। ५४
English NotesAI-assisted translation
"In Dardara (now Dadrī, in Ballia) lives a sage named Mṛkaṇḍa, of endless austerity, master of the eight siddhis, highly respected and noble. To choose him (as groom), Sage Mudgala, a single-minded devotee of Lord Nārāyaṇa and most generous, has prepared all these provisions and sent them for his betrothal." (54)
