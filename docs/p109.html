Manuscript Note (EN)AI-assisted transcription
Metre: Svāgatā. The start of pāda 1 is unclear (Hindi: "on the occasion full of joy"). The earlier exports' pādas ("प्रीतिं हरिरिविलाउगे...", "प्रीतिरीतिरसोल्लासैः कलयापि / नृत्यन्नुदितदर्शनादभूत्") are not in the manuscript.

English NotesAI-assisted translation
This was [unclear] of most charming smiles; happiness was shared all around and joy rained down. Everyone was very happy; satisfied with many kinds of food, all were immersed in joy. Manuscript NoteAI-assisted transcription
वृत्त: स्वागता। पाद २ अस्पष्ट है; उसके अन्त में दाहिनी ओर "सतेषु" अलग से लिखा है। पाद ३ "सम्प्रहृष्टहृदयः सुदयोर्मि-/रानतेन" (दया की लहरों वाला; टिप्पणी के "दया की अनन्त लहरियों से" से पुष्ट) — पूर्व निष्कासन का "सम्पश्यन् हृष्टहृदयः सुदयार्द्रो" (१२ वर्ण) भ्रान्त था।
Manuscript Note (EN)AI-assisted transcription
Metre: Svāgatā. Pāda 2 is unclear; at its end "सतेषु" is written separately on the right. Pāda 3 reads "सम्प्रहृष्टहृदयः सुदयोर्मि-/रानतेन" ("with waves of compassion", confirmed by the notes' "दया की अनन्त लहरियों से"); the earlier export's "सम्पश्यन् हृष्टहृदयः सुदयार्द्रो" (12 syllables) was a misreading.
English Translation (of the Sanskrit)AI-assisted translation
When the sacrificial priests [and others], delighted, had set out for their own homes, he, overjoyed at heart and with waves of kindness, bowed with his head lowered. (46)
