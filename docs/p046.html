Verse 27
Shlokamanuensis's hand
क्व ते जनिः कोऽस्ति पिताऽभिधापि का प्रतिष्ठसे कुत्र च किं चिकीर्षसि । ममैव वाऽनुग्रहवाञ्छया विभो ! समागताः सन्निह शंकरः स्वयम् ॥ २७ ॥
English Translation (of the Sanskrit)AI-assisted translation
"Where were you born? Who is your father, and what is your name? Where are you going, and what do you wish to do? Or, O lord, has Śaṅkara himself come here, wishing to show me favour?" (27)

English Translation (of the Hindi)AI-assisted translation
"Where were you born? Who is your father? What is your name? Where are you setting out for? What do you want? Or have you, Śaṅkara himself, come to my house wishing to show me favour?" (27)

ततः स वर्णी स्मितपूर्वमुज्जगा- दवेहि धातुस्तनयं महामुने ! । अधीत्य विद्यामधिगम्य सत्यतः परामथो लोकगतेर्बुभुत्सया ॥ २८ ॥
English Translation (of the Sanskrit)AI-assisted translation
Then that brahmacārī said with a smile: "Know me, great sage, as the son of Dhātā; having studied, and truly attained the highest knowledge, [I set out] then, wishing to understand the course of the world." (28)
Translationamanuensis's hand
तब उस ब्रह्मचारी ने स्मितपूर्वक बोले- पढ़ें हे महामुनि! मुझे आप धाता का पुत्र जाने। लोकगति को जानने की इच्छा से मैने यावत् विद्या की प्राप्ति कर ली ॥ २८ ॥
English Translation (of the Hindi)AI-assisted translation
Then that brahmacārī said with a smile: "Great sage! Know me as the son of Dhātā. Wishing to know the course of the world, I have obtained all the knowledge I could." (28)
Notesgrandfather's hand
यह सुनते ही उस ब्रह्मचारी ने अत्यन्त सहज मृदु मुस्कान के साथ कहना शुरू किया, हे महर्षिप्रवर! आप मुझे श्री धाता का पुत्र जानें, मैंने आचार्य पादों से ग्रहण पूर्वक लोकगति का सम्यक् ज्ञान प्राप्त करने के लिए परा तथा अपरा (आत्मज्ञान सम्पादिका परा विद्या सम्पूर्ण लौकिक ज्ञान की सम्पादिका अपरा) अर्थात् श्रेय और प्रेय दोनों प्रकार की विद्याओं का सम्यक् अनुशीलन किया है। २८
English NotesAI-assisted translation
Hearing this, that brahmacārī began to speak with a very natural, soft smile: "Best of great sages! Know me as the son of Śrī Dhātā. To gain a proper knowledge of the course of the world, receiving it from my revered teachers, I have thoroughly studied both the higher and the lower (the higher knowledge that brings knowledge of the self, and the lower that brings all worldly knowledge), that is, both kinds of learning, of the good (śreyas) and of the pleasant (preyas)." (28)
