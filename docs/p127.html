Verse 17
Shlokamanuensis's hand
तदनु दाक्षिनिदेशमवाप्य तै- र्द्विजवरैः किल पञ्चभिरार्द्रया । चरणजानुशुभांसशिरस्सु सो- विततनुः शुशुभे सुहरिद्रया ॥ १७ ॥
Manuscript NoteAI-assisted transcription
वृत्त: द्रुतविलम्बित। तृतीय पाद के अन्त में "सो" के बाद योजक/अवग्रह जैसा चिह्न है; "सो विततनुः" (स + विततनुः) पढ़ा गया।
Manuscript Note (EN)AI-assisted transcription
Metre: Drutavilambita. At the end of pāda 3, after "सो", there is a mark like a hyphen or avagraha; it is read as "सो विततनुः" (saḥ + vitatanuḥ).
English Translation (of the Sanskrit)AI-assisted translation
Then, on Dākṣi's instruction, those five excellent brahmins [anointed him] with moist turmeric on his feet, knees, fine shoulders and head; and he, his body thus covered, shone with the fine turmeric. (17)
Translationamanuensis's hand
उसके बाद दाक्षी के आदेश को पाकर उन पाँच द्विजवरों ने गीली हल्दी का लेप किया जिससे चरण-जानु-कन्धे तथा सिर पर लगे हल्दी से शरीर सुशोभित हो गया ॥ १७ ॥
English Translation (of the Hindi)AI-assisted translation
Then, on receiving Dākṣi's instruction, those five excellent brahmins applied a paste of moist turmeric, so that his body shone with the turmeric on his feet, knees, shoulders and head. (17)

English NotesAI-assisted translation
After this, on receiving Sage Dākṣi's instruction, five excellent, learned brahmins took moist turmeric and anointed the bridegroom's feet, thighs, broad shoulders and head with it, making his body golden-yellow, so that his radiant body became more charming still. (17)
Verse 18
Shlokamanuensis's hand
अथ च षोडशमातृसुपूजनं विहितवाञ्जनकोऽस्य सभार्यकः । सतृणतोरणवारिजमातृका- स्थलजमातृसुपूजनपूर्वकम् ॥ १८ ॥
Manuscript NoteAI-assisted transcription
वृत्त: द्रुतविलम्बित। "वारिज" = कमल (हिन्दी अनुवाद: "कमल"; टिप्पणी: "जल मातृका")।
Manuscript Note (EN)AI-assisted transcription
Metre: Drutavilambita. "वारिज" = lotus (the Hindi translation has "lotus"; the notes have "water mātṛkā").
English Translation (of the Sanskrit)AI-assisted translation
And then his father, with his wife, performed the worship of the sixteen Mothers, preceded by the worship of the Mothers of grass and the toraṇa, of the water-born (lotus), and of the land. (18)
Translationamanuensis's hand
इसके बाद तृण-तोरण-कमल-मातृका स्थल मातृका पूजनपूर्वक वर के पिता ने पत्नी के साथ षोडश मातृका पूजन किया ॥ १८ ॥
English Translation (of the Hindi)AI-assisted translation
After this, after worshipping the grass, toraṇa and lotus mātṛkās and the land mātṛkā, the bridegroom's father with his wife performed the worship of the sixteen mātṛkās. (18)
Notesgrandfather's hand
इसके बाद मुनि मृकण्डु के पिता मुनि धाता ने अपनी पत्नी के साथ षोडश मातृकाओं का पूजन किया, साथ ही सुन्दर तोरण मातृका, जल मातृका, स्थल मातृका की पूजा भी उसके पूर्व सम्पादित किया। १८
English NotesAI-assisted translation
After this, Sage Dhātā, father of Sage Mṛkaṇḍu, together with his wife, worshipped the sixteen mātṛkās. Before that he had also performed the worship of the beautiful toraṇa mātṛkā, the water mātṛkā and the land mātṛkā. (18)
