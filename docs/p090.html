English NotesAI-assisted translation
For the building work, Sage Mṛkaṇḍa first called Lord Bhāskara to mind. The Lord Sun, contemplated in his meditation, appeared in person with his intense circle of rays. At once he showed him the land fit for building (told him of it), and vanished again to his daily worldly work (running the creation). (7)
