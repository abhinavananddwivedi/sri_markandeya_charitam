दाक्षिणा समुपदिष्टमाश्रमं निश्चितं समयकारि गन्तुकः । तिर्यगग्निदिशि [unclear] गङ्गया समनुमोदितो ययौ ॥ ३ ॥
Manuscript NoteAI-assisted transcription
वृत्त: रथोद्धता। पाद १ "दाक्षिणा" (= दक्षपुत्र दाक्षि द्वारा; टिप्पणी: "दक्षपुत्र (दधि)") और "समुपदिष्टम्" छन्द से; पूर्व पाठ "दाक्षिण्या समुद्दिष्ट मार्गं" (९ अक्षर) भ्रान्त था। पाद ३ का उत्तरार्ध अस्पष्ट है ("…वाहिनी…" जैसा); पूर्व पाठ "वह्निसंज्ञकायाम्" छन्द में नहीं बैठता। पाद ४ "गङ्गया" (पूर्व "गङ्गाया")।
Manuscript Note (EN)AI-assisted transcription
Metre: Rathoddhatā. Pāda 1 reads "दाक्षिणा" (by Dākṣi, Dakṣa's son; the note says "दक्षपुत्र") and "समुपदिष्टम्", as the metre requires; the earlier "दाक्षिण्या समुद्दिष्ट मार्गं" (nine syllables) was wrong. The second half of pāda 3 is unclear (it looks like "…वाहिनी…"); the earlier "वह्निसंज्ञकायाम्" does not scan. Pāda 4 reads "गङ्गया" (earlier "गङ्गाया").
English Translation (of the Sanskrit)AI-assisted translation
Having fixed on the hermitage pointed out by Dākṣi, the traveller, keeping to his time, set out in the oblique, south-eastern direction [unclear], with the Gaṅgā's assent. (3)

English Translation (of the Hindi)AI-assisted translation
That travelling great soul, having settled on the hermitage pointed out by Dakṣa [the Hindi glosses "(चातुर)", "clever one"; the Sanskrit "दाक्षिण्या" suggests Dākṣi], set out on the road slanting toward the south-east, with the Gaṅgā's approval. (3)

English NotesAI-assisted translation
Although the decision was to go only by the road shown to Mṛkaṇḍa by Dakṣa's son [the notes as transcribed add "(दधि)"; probably Dākṣi] for making his dwelling, he nonetheless decided to go toward his goal along the Gaṅgā's stream, which runs slanting toward the south-east, and set out in that direction. Manuscript NoteAI-assisted transcription
वृत्त: रथोद्धता (चारों पाद शुद्ध)। पाद ४ का आरम्भ पाण्डुलिपि में अधिलिखित है; छन्द और सन्धि ("कस्यचिद् आश्रमे") से "दाश्रमे" पढ़ा गया (पूर्व पाठ "त्तस्याश्रमे")।
Manuscript Note (EN)AI-assisted transcription
Metre: Rathoddhatā (all four pādas scan). The opening of pāda 4 is overwritten in the manuscript; "दाश्रमे" is read from the metre and the sandhi ("कस्यचिद् आश्रमे"); the earlier reading was "त्तस्याश्रमे".
English Translation (of the Sanskrit)AI-assisted translation
Leaving the hilly land on his left and going quickly by a straight road, he reached Pītabhitti and lodged as a guest in someone's hermitage there. (4)
