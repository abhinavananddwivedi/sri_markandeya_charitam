English Translation (of the Sanskrit)AI-assisted translation
At the request of the auspicious wedding rite, for the auspicious marriage ceremony that had been [unclear: agreed], the sage Dākṣi too, devoted to performing every rite, was invited here. (15)

At the request of the auspicious wedding rite, for the settled auspicious wedding ceremony, Dākṣi too, intent on every rite, is invited along with the sage. (15)

English NotesAI-assisted translation
The sage Dākṣi, a constant helper in auspicious wedding work, who at the request of the wedding always took the lead in it (an engaged helper), and who gave careful help in all this work, was also invited with honour. (15)

Shlokamanuensis's hand
नियतलग्नतिथेः किल पूर्वतः शरमिते दिवसे कृतमङ्गलम् । कलशपूर्वमुमागणनाथयोः सविधिपूजनमाशु वरोऽकरोत् ॥ १६ ॥
Manuscript NoteAI-assisted transcription
वृत्त: द्रुतविलम्बित। "शरमिते" = पाँच (शर = ५)।
Manuscript Note (EN)AI-assisted transcription
Metre: Drutavilambita. "शरमिते" = five (śara, "arrow", stands for 5).
English Translation (of the Sanskrit)AI-assisted translation
Five days before the appointed lagna and tithi, the auspicious rites were performed; and the bridegroom promptly performed, with due rite, the worship of Umā and Gaṇanātha (Gaṇeśa), preceded by [the installing of] the kalaśa. (16)
Translationamanuensis's hand
नियत लग्न तिथि के पाँच दिन पूर्व ही माङ्गलिक कार्य सम्पन्न किया गया। कलश स्थापन के पूर्व वर ने गौरी गणेश की पूजा की ॥ १६ ॥
English Translation (of the Hindi)AI-assisted translation
Five days before the appointed lagna and tithi the auspicious rites were performed. Before the installing of the kalaśa, the bridegroom worshipped Gaurī and Gaṇeśa. (16)
Notesgrandfather's hand
विवाह के लिए निर्धारित लग्न और तिथि के पाँच दिन पूर्व ही मङ्गल कार्य प्रारम्भ कर दिया गया, जिसमें वर ने स्वयं विधिपूर्वक [unclear: गुरुओं] के द्वारा माङ्गलिक सम्पन्न सामग्रियों द्वारा भगवती गौरी एवं भगवान गणपति की पूजा कलश-स्थापन पूर्वक स्वयं सम्पन्न किया। १६
English NotesAI-assisted translation
Five days before the lagna and tithi fixed for the wedding, the auspicious rites were begun. In them the bridegroom himself, duly, through [unclear: the gurus], with the auspicious materials provided, performed in person the worship of the goddess Gaurī and Lord Gaṇapati, beginning with the installing of the kalaśa. (16)
