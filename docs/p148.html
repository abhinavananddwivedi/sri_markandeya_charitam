Manuscript Note (EN)AI-assisted transcription
Metre: Drutavilambita. Pādas 1, 2 and 4 scan as written. Pāda 3: "रथाङ्गयो-" (of the cakravāka pair) is clear; what precedes it is written like "प्रबलया(?)श्रुवदस्रु" ("प्र", then "ल"/"ब", "ल", "या"; a light stroke over "श्रु"). Its eight syllables scan (।।।ऽ।।ऽ।), but the reading and sense ("shedding unceasing tears from their eyes") are uncertain. Pāda 4: "विलोकयत" = look! (plural imperative).

महति विप्लवदर्शनकारणा- न्निजकुलायनिलायनतत्पराः । अतिविपद्भरदुर्मनसो द्रुतं खगवरा अपि वासद्रुमं श्रिताः ॥ ६० ॥
Manuscript NoteAI-assisted transcription
वृत्त: द्रुतविलम्बित। पाद १–३ लिखित रूप में छन्द में बैठते हैं। पाद ४: "वासद्रुमं" (लिखित "बासदुमं" जैसा) में "स" संयुक्ताक्षर "द्रु" से पहले गुरु हो जाता है, जबकि छन्द को वहाँ लघु चाहिए; "वासदुमं" लिखा मानें तो छन्द बैठता है, पर शब्द "द्रुम" ही होना चाहिए — कवि की छूट या लेखन-भेद।
Manuscript Note (EN)AI-assisted transcription
Metre: Drutavilambita. Pādas 1–3 scan as written. Pāda 4: in "वासद्रुमं" (written like "बासदुमं") the "स" becomes heavy before the conjunct "द्रु", where the metre wants a light syllable; taken as written ("वासदुमं") it scans, but the word must be "द्रुम" (tree), so this is either the poet's licence or a scribal variant.
English Translation (of the Sanskrit)AI-assisted translation
Because they saw a great upheaval [coming], intent on hiding in their own nests, the fine birds too, very downcast at the weight of the danger, quickly took refuge in the trees where they dwell. (60)
