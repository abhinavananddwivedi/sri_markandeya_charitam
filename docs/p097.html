English NotesAI-assisted translation
Learning of Sage Mṛkaṇḍa's many kinds of direct knowledge and of his intense (firm), matchless spiritual attainment, the people who came had all their questions answered and went back fully calm and at ease. Those who did not yet know him, and who hoped for success in their various undertakings, were also encouraged by these people to go to Sage Mṛkaṇḍa. (21)
Verse 22
Shlokamanuensis's hand
एवमेव बहुधा श्रुतकीर्ति मुद्गलो मुनिवरः कुरुवासी । दाक्षिबोधितसुश्रुतचणः स स्वात्मजापरिणयाय समुत्कः ॥ २२ ॥
Manuscript NoteAI-assisted transcription
वृत्त: स्वागता। पाद २ में "मुद्गलो" का पूर्व रूप काटकर संशोधित है। पाद ३ "दाक्षिबोधितसुश्रुतचणः स" (दाक्षि द्वारा अवगत कराये गये, सुश्रुत से प्रसिद्ध) — पूर्व निष्कासन का "दाक्षिणात्यचरितं सुश्रुत-वृत्तः" (१२ वर्ण) भ्रान्त था।
Manuscript Note (EN)AI-assisted transcription
Metre: Svāgatā. In pāda 2, an earlier form of "मुद्गलो" is corrected. Pāda 3 reads "दाक्षिबोधितसुश्रुतचणः स" ("informed by Dākṣi, known for what he had well heard"); the earlier export's "दाक्षिणात्यचरितं सुश्रुत-वृत्तः" (12 syllables) was a misreading.
English Translation (of the Sanskrit)AI-assisted translation
In just this way, having often heard of his fame, Mudgala, the excellent sage dwelling among the Kurus, informed by Dākṣi and well acquainted with what he had heard, became eager to give his own daughter in marriage. (22)

English Translation (of the Hindi)AI-assisted translation
Having thus heard of his fame many times, and having been introduced [to him] by Dākṣi and heard good report, the excellent sage Mudgala, who lived among the Kurus, became eager for his daughter's marriage. (22)

English NotesAI-assisted translation
Thus the banner of his fame spread in all directions. At that time the great soul Mudgala, a resident of Kurukṣetra, whom Maharṣi Dākṣi had made fully acquainted with him by telling him everything about him, and who was a man of good conduct, was also eager for his daughter's marriage. (22)
