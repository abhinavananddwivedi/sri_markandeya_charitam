Verse 15
Shlokamanuensis's hand
प्रातरेव प्रविकल्प्य चेतसा निर्मलेन मुकुरे यथाऽग्रतः । नैर्ऋतौ च स्थल त्रियोजनात् गोमतीतटगतं पराशरम् ॥ १५ ॥
English Translation (of the Sanskrit)AI-assisted translation
That very morning, having discerned with a clear mind, as [in] a mirror before him, [he knew of] Parāśara, on the bank of the Gomatī, three yojanas away to the south-west. (15)
Translationamanuensis's hand
मुनिवर ने प्रातः काल ही निर्मल चित्त से ही, ध्यान कर जान लिया कि नैर्ऋत्य कोण में तीन योजन पर गोमती के तट पर महामुनि पराशर विद्यमान हैं ॥ १५ ॥
English Translation (of the Hindi)AI-assisted translation
That very morning, meditating with a clear mind, the excellent sage came to know that the great sage Parāśara was living on the bank of the Gomatī, three yojanas away to the south-west. (15)
Notesgrandfather's hand
मुनि मृकण्ड ने प्रातः नित्यक्रिया के अनन्तर अपने चित्तवृत्ति को एकाग्र कर स्वस्थ चित्त हो यह जान लिया कि यहाँ से नैर्ऋत्य कोण में ३ योजन (१२ कोस) की दूरी पर गोमती नदी के सुरम्य तट पर मुनीश्वरों में अत्यन्त श्रेष्ठ महामुनि पराशर जी का पुण्यप्रद आश्रम है । १५
English NotesAI-assisted translation
After his morning daily rites, the sage Mṛkaṇḍa focused his mind and, with a settled mind, came to know that at a distance of 3 yojanas (12 kos) to the south-west from here, on the lovely bank of the river Gomatī, was the meritorious hermitage of the great sage Parāśara, most excellent among the lords of sages. (15)
Verse 16
Shlokamanuensis's hand
संविधास्यति स मे मनोरथान्- नन्वदारहृदयः पराशरः । इत्थमग्रजसमं महामुनिं द्रष्टुमेव हृदये व्यचिन्तयत् ॥ १६ ॥
English Translation (of the Sanskrit)AI-assisted translation
"Surely Parāśara, generous of heart, will fulfil my wishes": so he thought in his heart, [resolving] only to see that great sage, who was like an elder brother. (16)
Translationamanuensis's hand
महामुनि पराशर अति उदार चेता हैं, वे ही मेरे मनोरथों को पूर्ण करेंगे, इस प्रकार बड़े भैया के समान महामुनि के दर्शनार्थ उन्होंने हृदय में निश्चय किया ॥ १६ ॥
English Translation (of the Hindi)AI-assisted translation
"The great sage Parāśara is very generous-minded; he will fulfil my wishes." Thus he resolved in his heart to see the great sage, who was like an elder brother. (16)
Notesgrandfather's hand
सुना है महामुनि पराशर अत्यन्त उदार सहृदय एवं मुनियों में अतिशय श्रेष्ठ हैं, वे मेरा सब प्रकार का कल्याण करेंगे वे मेरी सभी कामनायें अवश्य पूरी करेंगे, वे मेरे बड़े भाई के समान हैं। उनके दर्शन की उत्कट अभिलाषा से वहाँ जाने का निश्चय किया । १६
English NotesAI-assisted translation
"I have heard that the great sage Parāśara is very generous, warm-hearted and most excellent among sages. He will do me every kind of good; he will surely fulfil all my wishes; he is like my elder brother." With an intense longing to see him, he decided to go there. (16)
