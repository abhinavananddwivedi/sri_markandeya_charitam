Paleographic / Textual Note: The verse extols the traditional spiritual power of exalted brāhmaṇas whose mere mental resolve (saṅkalpa) prompts the righteous subjects of Bhāratavarṣa to supply whatever is needed. Meter: Rathoddhatā [– ⏑ – | ⏑ ⏑ ⏑ | – ⏑ – | ⏑ –]
English Translation (of the Sanskrit)AI-assisted translation
"Excellent brahmins, here, whatever they desire in their minds attains fulfilment; by this the people of Bhārata here eagerly do it, to bring it to you." (42)

"There is power of fulfilment in the speech of brahmins; whatever you have wished in your mind, all the people of Bhārata will bring before you." (42)

English NotesAI-assisted translation
"The speech of generous, high-minded, excellent brahmins is fulfilled of itself; whatever things they imagine in their minds, the people living in this land provide them of their own accord. It has been the special quality of the people of this land of Bhāratavarṣa that, at the mere imagining of brahmins, all the work they hold dear is completed of itself." (42)
