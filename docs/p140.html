English Translation (of the Sanskrit)AI-assisted translation

Manuscript NoteAI-assisted transcription
वृत्त: द्रुतविलम्बित। पाद २ लिखा है "न्नमल…" (पिछले पाद के अन्त का न् + अमल)। पाद ४ का पहला अक्षर "न"-जैसा, पर हिन्दी ("सूर्य के समान") के अनुसार "रविनिभान्"।
Manuscript Note (EN)AI-assisted transcription
Metre: Drutavilambita. Pāda 2 is written "न्नमल…" (the final न् of pāda 1 + अमल). The first letter of pāda 4 looks like "न", but the Hindi ("like the sun") gives "रविनिभान्".
English Translation (of the Sanskrit)AI-assisted translation
[Look at] the dwellers of the Sun's world, like the sun: adorned with golden earrings and necklaces, filled with pure divine gems, resplendent with bright crowns and four hands. (44)

English NotesAI-assisted translation
Now do look at those who live in the Sun's world. Look also at these dwellers of the Sun's world: always splendid with abundant golden earrings and various golden necklaces, set with various pure and costly gems, wearing many holy gem-studded crowns, four-handed, shining with a radiance like that of the blessed Sun himself. (44)
