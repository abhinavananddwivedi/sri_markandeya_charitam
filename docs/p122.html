English NotesAI-assisted translation
After this he himself went, full of eagerness, to the house of the maker of mauli (crowns). Having seen a beautiful, charming crown, he paid its proper price. Then, to obtain the special wedding article made of bamboo called a 'ḍāl', in which a platter for the parched grain (lājā) and other useful things are carried in good order, he went in person to the house of a 'Ḍom', a caste held to be unclean [unclear: (a bamboo-working caste)].
