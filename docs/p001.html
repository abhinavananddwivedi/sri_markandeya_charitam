English Translation (of the Sanskrit)AI-assisted translation
When the Creator's mind awakens to show its own skill in the treasury of works, it quietly makes an extraordinary work, an excellent one that at once puts the gods to shame. (1)

English Translation (of the Hindi)AI-assisted translation
To show skill in the work of making a poem, the apt intellect of a young maker comes forth, and only then is a renowned, extraordinary work accomplished; even so, it is fitting that he should suddenly feel abashed at completing that very work. (1)

English NotesAI-assisted translation
The Creator, maker and leader of the endless universe, being engaged in the work of creation, sometimes wishes to present an example of his finest art. At that time, to show the fullness of his highest skill, he gives his all and creates a wonderful new creation, by which, putting to shame with his highest art even the gods, the best form of creation, full of divine wealth, he creates a [unclear: being] more wonderful, matchless and complete than they. (1)

English Translation (of the Sanskrit)AI-assisted translation
Where is life, made very short by the force of the Kali age, and where the sage whose life lasts for seven kalpas? Yet, to write the story of Mṛkaṇḍu's son, I take up an iron pen and rely on it. (2)

English Translation (of the Hindi)AI-assisted translation
Where is my short life, under the influence of the Kali age, and where the sage Mārkaṇḍeya, who lives for seven kalpas? To write the life of such a one, Mārkaṇḍeya, son of Mṛkaṇḍu, I take the support of my iron pen. (2)

आज मैं उसी उदात्ततम संरचना के एक कड़ी की संस्मृति में अपनी इस कलयुगी स्थाणु लौह निर्मित लेखनी द्वारा उनका यशोवर्णन करना चाहता हूँ, यद्यपि हम सांसारिक प्राणियों का जीवन स्वतः क्षणिक एवं नगण्य है उसमें भी इस घोर कलिकाल का दुष्प्रभाव भी! फिर भी आज मैं अपने इस क्षणिक जीवन में सप्तकल्पान्त की लम्बी जीवन यात्रा करने वाले (सप्त कल्प तक चिरंजीवी) महामुनि मार्कण्डेय जी के उदात्ततम जीवन की कथा का वर्णन जो सर्वथा दुष्कर है, फिर भी उसकी चर्चा करना चाहता हूँ।
English NotesAI-assisted translation
Today, in remembrance of one link of that most exalted creation, I wish to describe his glory with this iron pen of the Kali age, rigid as a post. The life of us worldly beings is fleeting and insignificant in itself, and on top of that there is the influence of this terrible Kali age! Even so, today, in this fleeting life of mine, I wish to tell the most exalted life story of the great sage Mārkaṇḍeya, who makes the long journey of life to the end of seven kalpas (living for seven kalpas). Although this is altogether difficult, I still wish to speak of it. (2)
