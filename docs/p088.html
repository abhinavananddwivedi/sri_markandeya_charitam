एत्य पूर्वकृतवासवटं सः स्वासनादि विदधे रुचिरं यत् । यत्तु नोद्विजत एकलकोऽपि किं पुनः सहित आङ्गिरसेन ॥ ३ ॥

वृत्त: स्वागता। पाद ३ में "एकलकोऽपि" (अकेला होने पर भी) पठनीय है।
Manuscript Note (EN)AI-assisted transcription
Metre: Svāgatā. Pāda 3 reads "एकलकोऽपि" ("even when alone").
English Translation (of the Sanskrit)AI-assisted translation
Reaching the banyan of his former dwelling, he arranged a pleasant seat and the like. He who was not troubled even when alone, how much less now, accompanied by Āṅgirasa! (3)

English Translation (of the Hindi)AI-assisted translation
Coming to the banyan tree at his former dwelling place, he set up his fine seat and other things. He was not troubled even when he was alone; now that he is with Āṅgirasa, what need is there to say more? (3)

English NotesAI-assisted translation
Leaving that place, Sage Mṛkaṇḍa came to the banyan tree at his old dwelling and, as before, arranged his fine, comfortable and very pleasing seat and the like. Earlier, when he used to live there alone, he felt no agitation or worry; how much less now, when he was with one more companion, Āṅgirasa. (3)

Manuscript NoteAI-assisted transcription
वृत्त: स्वागता। पाद ३ "विधिन्त्वनुकूलं" (= विधिं तु अनुकूलं), पाद ४ "व्ययतिष्ट" (वि + अयतिष्ट, प्रयत्न किया)।
Manuscript Note (EN)AI-assisted transcription
Metre: Svāgatā. Pāda 3 is written "विधिन्त्वनुकूलं" (= विधिं तु अनुकूलं); pāda 4 has "व्ययतिष्ट" (vi + ayatiṣṭa, "he strove").
English Translation (of the Sanskrit)AI-assisted translation
Reaching the land of Dardara, whose quarters are purified by the waters of the Gaṅgā, Mṛkaṇḍu deemed fate favourable and set about building a dwelling. (4)
Translationamanuensis's hand

English Translation (of the Hindi)AI-assisted translation
Coming to the land of Dardara, whose quarters in every direction are made holy by the water of the Gaṅgā, Maharṣi Mṛkaṇḍu accepted the favourable course of fate and set about making a hut to live in. (4)

वहाँ से चलकर मुनि मृकण्ड भगवती गङ्गा की अविरल पवित्र धार से चारों ओर से घिरी हुई (दिगन्त-व्यापिनी भगवती भागीरथी से परिवेष्टित) उस अनुपम पवित्र भूमि को देखकर उन्होंने अपने विधि (दैव) को अत्यन्त अनुकूल माना और वहाँ अपने निवास करने के लिए उचित विधि (सहकार) के लिए भी प्रयत्न करना शुरू किया। ४
English NotesAI-assisted translation
Proceeding from there, Sage Mṛkaṇḍa saw that matchless holy land, surrounded on all sides by the unbroken, sacred stream of the goddess Gaṅgā (encircled by the goddess Bhāgīrathī, who spreads to the horizon). He considered his fate (daiva) to be very favourable, and began to make efforts toward the proper means (cooperation) for settling there. (4)
