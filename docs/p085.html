Verse 50
Shlokamanuensis's hand
मादृशेऽप्यविदिते त्वयीदृशी भावना यदि न काचनाभवत् । पूर्त्तिमेष्यति कथं मतिः सादरं पादपङ्कमधुना समाश्रये ॥ ५० ॥
Manuscript NoteAI-assisted transcription
वृत्त: रथोद्धता। पाद ४ "पादपङ्कमधुना" (पूर्व "पादपङ्कजमधुना", एक अक्षर अधिक)। पाद ३ छन्द में नहीं बैठता; पाण्डुलिपि में "…मनोऽर्थ सादरं" जैसा भी पढ़ा जा सकता है; अनिश्चित।
Manuscript Note (EN)AI-assisted transcription
Metre: Rathoddhatā. Pāda 4 reads "पादपङ्कमधुना" (the earlier "पादपङ्कजमधुना" has one syllable too many). Pāda 3 does not scan; the manuscript may read "…मनोऽर्थ सादरं"; uncertain.
English Translation (of the Sanskrit)AI-assisted translation
"If you had had no such feeling for one like me, unknown to you, how would [my] intention reach fulfilment? Now I respectfully take refuge at your lotus feet." (50)

English NotesAI-assisted translation
The sage Mṛkaṇḍu began to say humbly: "Lord! If you had not had this generous, compassionate feeling toward an unknown, poor person like me, how could my wishes have been fulfilled? I am wholly grateful; now, [taking refuge at] your lotus feet with my whole self, I consider myself fulfilled." (50)

योऽपि मे सहचरोऽर्पितस्त्वया त्वाङ्गिरःकुलसमुद्भवो मुनिः। कल्पवृक्ष इव पूरयिष्यति त्वन्निदेशमधिगत्य मेऽर्थिनाम्॥ ५१॥

Manuscript Note (EN)AI-assisted transcription
Paleographic / Textual Note: In line 2, the scribal text reads `वाङ्गिरः कुलसमुद्भवो मुनिः` (the sage born of the Āṅgirasa lineage). Lines 3–4 compare the sage’s cooperative assistance to the wish-fulfilling tree (kalpavṛkṣa). Meter: Rathoddhatā [– ⏑ – | ⏑ ⏑ ⏑ | – ⏑ – | ⏑ –]
English Translation (of the Sanskrit)AI-assisted translation
"And the sage born of the Āṅgirasa line whom you have given me as companion will, like a wish-granting tree, having received your direction, fulfil what I seek." (51)
