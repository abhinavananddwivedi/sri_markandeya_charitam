Verse 30
Shlokamanuensis's hand
गां विसृज्य मधुपर्कसंयुतं हृद्यमन्नमथ मिष्टतर्पणम् । स्वासनेऽभिसमुपाविशद् द्रुतं तं मृकण्डुमवदत् पराशरः ॥ ३० ॥
English Translation (of the Sanskrit)AI-assisted translation
Having given the cow with the madhuparka, and pleasing food, sweet and satisfying, [and] when [Mṛkaṇḍu] had quickly sat down on his seat, Parāśara spoke to Mṛkaṇḍu. (30)
Translationamanuensis's hand
मधुपर्कार्थ गौ अर्पित कर तृप्त मधुर एवं स्वादिष्ट भोजन से, सुहृद आसन पर विराजमान मृकण्डु से मुनिवर पराशर ने सुन्दर वाणी से अपना हार्दिक भाव अभिव्यक्त किया ॥ ३० ॥
English Translation (of the Hindi)AI-assisted translation
Having offered a cow for the madhuparka, and [served] satisfying, sweet and tasty food, the excellent sage Parāśara expressed his heartfelt feelings in fine words to Mṛkaṇḍu, who was seated on a comfortable seat. (30)

मुनि पराशर ने सद्भावनापूर्ण उत्तम वाणी से उनका सत्कार करते हुए अत्यन्त सुन्दर सुखद भोज्य पदार्थों द्वारा सविधि आतिथ्य पूर्ण करके तृप्त-हृष्ट होकर आसन पर बैठ जाने के बाद अभी ज्यों ही मुनि मृकण्ड अपनी बात कहने की चेष्टा कर रहे हैं तब तक महर्षि पराशर ने अपनी सुमधुर वाणी से उनसे कहना प्रारम्भ किया । ३०
English NotesAI-assisted translation
The sage Parāśara honoured him with excellent words full of goodwill and duly completed the hospitality with very fine, pleasing foods. After [Mṛkaṇḍa], satisfied and glad, had sat down on his seat, and just as the sage Mṛkaṇḍa was trying to say what he had to say, Maharṣi Parāśara began to speak to him in his very sweet voice. (30)
Verse 31
Shlokamanuensis's hand
वत्स वेद्मि भृगुवंशभूषणं धातृपुत्रमखिलाहृतदूषणम् । अंशुमद्विदितवेदपूरुषं त्वां सदा क्रतुषु सोमभूषणम् ॥ ३१ ॥
English Translation (of the Sanskrit)AI-assisted translation
"Child, I know you: ornament of the Bhṛgu line, son of Dhātā, who has removed every fault, radiant, knower of the Vedas and of the Person, always the ornament of the soma at sacrifices." (31)
Translationamanuensis's hand
हे वत्स! भृगुवंश के भूषण स्वरूप धाता के पुत्र समस्त दोषों से शून्य तुम्हें मैं जानता हूँ । अपने प्रभाव से समस्त वेदों को जानने वाले तुम सूर्य के समान तथा यज्ञों में सोमपायी हो ॥ ३१ ॥
English Translation (of the Hindi)AI-assisted translation
"Child! I know you, the ornament of the Bhṛgu line, son of Dhātā, free of all faults. By your power you know all the Vedas; you are like the sun, and a drinker of soma at sacrifices." (31)
Notesgrandfather's hand
प्रिय वत्स! मैं जानता हूँ, आप भृगुवंश के कुलभूषण हैं, आप महर्षि धाता के सुपुत्र एवं सर्वथा निर्दोष दोषों से सर्वथा दूर रहने वाले हो, आप अपनी प्रखर ज्योतिर्पुञ्ज से भगवान् भास्कर की प्रभा को भी सुपुष्ट करने वाले हो, आप सदा सोमपायी व्रतों के आचरण शील व्यक्तियों में भी श्रेष्ठ हो। अर्थात् उत्तम सोमपायियों में आपका उच्चतम स्थान है। ३१
English NotesAI-assisted translation
"Dear child! I know that you are the ornament of the Bhṛgu line; you are the good son of Maharṣi Dhātā, wholly faultless and always far from faults. With your intense mass of light you strengthen even the radiance of Lord Bhāskara, and you are always the best even among those who keep the vows of soma-drinking; that is, you have the highest place among excellent soma-drinkers." (31)
