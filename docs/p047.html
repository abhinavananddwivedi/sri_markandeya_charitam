Verse 29
Shlokamanuensis's hand
मृकण्डुनामा पितुराज्ञया चिरं निवस्तुमिच्छन् कुटजं सदुपदम् । विलोकयंस्तल्लोकगणं समन्ततः नरञ्च नारायणमभ्युपागतः ॥ २९ ॥
English Translation (of the Sanskrit)AI-assisted translation
"[I am] named Mṛkaṇḍu; by my father's command, wishing to settle for good, [looking for] a good place for a hut, seeing the regions of the world all around, I came to Nara and Nārāyaṇa." (29)
Translationamanuensis's hand
मेरा नाम मृकण्डु है मैं पिता की आज्ञा से चिरकाल से निवास करने की इच्छा करता हुआ कुटिया के लिए अच्छी भूमि देखता हुआ उन लोकों में भ्रमण करता हुआ मैं नर नारायण के पास पहुँचा ॥ २९ ॥
English Translation (of the Hindi)AI-assisted translation
"My name is Mṛkaṇḍu. By my father's command, wishing to settle for a long time, looking for good land for a hut and travelling through those regions, I reached Nara and Nārāyaṇa." (29)

English NotesAI-assisted translation
"Sage! My name is Mṛkaṇḍa. By my father's command, wishing for many days to obtain land fit for my dwelling, searching for land fit to build a hut and live on, and travelling through various regions, I suddenly came near the place of Nara-Nārāyaṇa." (29)
Verse 30
Shlokamanuensis's hand
परञ्च ताभ्यामयि मे निदर्शिता गृहस्थितिर्भुक्तिविमुक्तिदायिनी । ततश्च गङ्गामनुयाहि यत्र ते रुचिर्यतस्तत्र निवत्स्यसि स्वयम् ॥ ३० ॥
English Translation (of the Sanskrit)AI-assisted translation
"But those two showed me the householder's state, which gives both enjoyment and liberation: 'Then follow the Gaṅgā; where your liking is, there you yourself will dwell.'" (30)
Translationamanuensis's hand
अब उन दोनों मुनियों ने भुक्ति मुक्ति दायिनी गृहस्थाश्रम के लिये मुझे आदेश दिया और कहा कि गङ्गा के किनारे किनारे जाओ जहाँ तुम्हारी रुचि हो वहाँ निवास करना आदि ॥ ३० ॥
English Translation (of the Hindi)AI-assisted translation
"Then those two sages directed me to the householder's stage, which gives enjoyment and liberation, and said: 'Go along the bank of the Gaṅgā; live wherever you like', and so on." (30)
Notesgrandfather's hand
किन्तु उन दोनों ऋषियों ने भी मुझे आदेश दिया कि भोग और मोक्ष प्रदायिनी उभयविध कल्याणकारिणी भगवती गङ्गा के किनारे किनारे चले जाओ, तुम्हे गृहस्थोचित रुचिकर जहां भूमि मिल जाये वहीं निवास करने के लिए घर बना लेना। ३०
English NotesAI-assisted translation
"But those two sages also directed me: 'Go along the bank of the goddess Gaṅgā, giver of enjoyment and liberation, who brings welfare of both kinds; wherever you find land that pleases you and suits a householder, make a house there and live.'" (30)
