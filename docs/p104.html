वृत्त: स्वागता। पाद १ "दातुमभीप्से-/त्पुङ्गवस्य" (अभीप्सेत्), पाद ४ "सदृशा जनकेन" (सागर-सदृश पिता) — अनुवाद "सागर तुल्य पिता" से पुष्ट; पूर्व निष्कासन का "सहसा" भ्रान्त था।
Manuscript Note (EN)AI-assisted transcription
Metre: Svāgatā. Pāda 1 reads "दातुमभीप्से-/त्पुङ्गवस्य" (abhīpset), and pāda 4 "सदृशा जनकेन" ("by a father like the ocean"), confirmed by the Hindi "सागर तुल्य पिता"; the earlier export's "सहसा" was a misreading.
English Translation (of the Sanskrit)AI-assisted translation
"What fee should one wish to give here to a groom who is the foremost of men? The maiden is to be given by a father like the ocean, just as Ramā (Lakṣmī) was." (35)

English Translation (of the Hindi)AI-assisted translation
"Why should one wish to give a fee (dakṣiṇā) for a groom who is the best of men? A daughter should be given away by a father like the ocean, as Ramā (Lakṣmī) was." (35)

महर्षे! अत्यन्त योग्य उत्तम वर के लिए दक्षिणा देने की क्या बातें की जायँ, वह तो सदा अदेय ही है। जैसे उदात्त प्रकृति पिता सागर ने अपनी कन्या भगवती लक्ष्मी का जैसे उदारतापूर्वक दान किया था, वैसे ही पिता को कन्यादान करना चाहिए। ३५
English NotesAI-assisted translation
"Great sage! What talk can there be of giving a fee for a most worthy, excellent groom? That is something that can never be given. Just as the ocean, a father of noble nature, generously gave away his daughter, the goddess Lakṣmī, so should a father give away his daughter in marriage (kanyādān)." (35)

"Even if the people on the bride's side, father, mother and others, give everything for the daughter's sake, it is still little. But the good people on the groom's side should be happy on seeing just that much." (36)

कन्या पक्ष के रहने वाले माता पिता भाई बन्धुओं द्वारा कन्या के लिए उदारता पूर्वक जितना भी दिया जाता है वह सब कुछ कम ही है क्योंकि देने की कोई सीमा नहीं होती, यों ही वर पक्ष के लोगों को भी सम्मान पूर्वक उदारता से जो कुछ मिल जाय उसी से परम सन्तुष्ट एवं प्रसन्न रहना चाहिए। ३६
English NotesAI-assisted translation
"However much the mother, father, brothers and relatives on the bride's side generously give for the daughter, all of it is too little, for there is no limit to giving. In the same way, the people on the groom's side should be fully content and happy with whatever they receive, given respectfully and generously." (36)
