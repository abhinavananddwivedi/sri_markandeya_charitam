Verse 55
Shlokamanuensis's hand
उत्तरेण परितोष्य जनांस्तान् हर्षयद्भिरखिलान् समुत्कैः । मार्गमध्यकृतवासनिकेतैः प्राप्यते स्म [unclear: उचिता] स्वनुरूपा ॥ ५५ ॥
Manuscript NoteAI-assisted transcription
वृत्त: स्वागता। पाद २ में १० वर्ण हैं (हस्तलेख के अनुसार)। पाद ३ "मार्गमध्यकृतवासनिकेतैः" (अनुवाद "मध्यमार्ग में डेरा डालने के लिए") और पाद ४ "प्राप्यते स्म ... स्वनुरूपा" ("अपने अनुरूप ... निवास स्थान प्राप्त किया"); पाद ४ का मध्य अस्पष्ट है। पूर्व निष्कासन का "मार्गसत्कृतसमाननिकेतैः / प्रापितैः स्थितिरवापि सुसत्त्वा" हस्तलेख में नहीं है।
Manuscript Note (EN)AI-assisted transcription
Metre: Svāgatā. Pāda 2 has 10 syllables (as written). Pāda 3 reads "मार्गमध्यकृतवासनिकेतैः" (Hindi: "to camp midway along the road") and pāda 4 "प्राप्यते स्म ... स्वनुरूपा" (Hindi: "obtained lodging suited to them"); the middle of pāda 4 is unclear. The earlier export's "मार्गसत्कृतसमाननिकेतैः / प्रापितैः स्थितिरवापि सुसत्त्वा" is not in the manuscript.
English Translation (of the Sanskrit)AI-assisted translation
Pleasing those people with their answers, the eager [members of the party], gladdening everyone and making their camp midway along the road, found a [unclear: fitting] lodging well suited to them. (55)

English Translation (of the Hindi)AI-assisted translation

यों मार्ग में चलते हुए वर वरण करने वालों ने आराम के लिए आधे रास्ते में डेरा (स्थान) बनाया उस समय उत्सुक मार्ग में रहने वालों ने इस उक्त सम्बन्ध में जो प्रश्न किया था किसको है? कहाँ से है? इत्यादि का सम्यक् उत्तर देते हुए उन सबको अत्यन्त हर्षित और आनन्दित किया, साथ ही उन लोगों द्वारा समुचित सत्कारादि से भी तृप्त हुए। ५५
English NotesAI-assisted translation
Thus, as they travelled, the betrothal party made a camp (a halting place) halfway along the road to rest. At that time, the curious people who lived along the road asked about the matter: "Whose is it? Where is it from?" and so on. Giving them proper answers, the party made them all very happy and glad, and were themselves also satisfied by the fitting hospitality those people gave them. (55)

वृत्त: स्वागता। पाद २ "परितृप्तमनोभिः" (पूर्व निष्कासन का "परितुष्टमनोभिः" भ्रान्त था)।
Manuscript Note (EN)AI-assisted transcription
Metre: Svāgatā. Pāda 2 reads "परितृप्तमनोभिः"; the earlier export's "परितुष्टमनोभिः" was a misreading.
English Translation (of the Sanskrit)AI-assisted translation
In just this way, their minds fully satisfied by the welcome of the many country people, the groom's party, led by Dākṣi, went on along the road they had to travel. (56)

Thus the members of the procession (bārātī), led by Dākṣi, their minds content with the welcome given by the country people, set out in the direction they were bound for. (56)

English NotesAI-assisted translation
As they travelled, the people of the various districts along the road received and honoured them well and with due rites. So, very glad, joyful and fully content in mind, the members of the procession, under the protection of Sage Dākṣi, the leader of the party, followed the road to their destination. (56)
