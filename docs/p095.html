English NotesAI-assisted translation
In the house, on one side he set the hand-mill for grinding flour, and on another the grinding slab and roller (sil-loṛhā) for grinding spices, chutney and the like. There were boxes made of good bamboo, in one place a storeroom for keeping grain, and in another fine beds made of good planks, beyond imagination. All of them looked handsome, each in its proper place. (17)
