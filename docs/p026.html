English Translation (of the Hindi)AI-assisted translation
At the remembrance of the glorious Gaṅgā's virtues, the great sage's voice choked; from excess of joy, trembling and thrilling of the hair spread through his body for a long time. Joining his flower-filled hands at his heart, he began to praise the goddess Gaṅgā. (51)

English NotesAI-assisted translation
At the mere remembrance of the endless virtues of the goddess Gaṅgā, who purifies the whole world, the great sage Mṛkaṇḍu's voice suddenly choked; from the excess of joy all the hair of his trembling body bristled (all his hair suddenly stood on end). With faith, taking heaps of flowers in both hands and holding them to his heart, he began to praise the most worshipful goddess, the divine river Gaṅgā. (51)

English Translation (of the Sanskrit)AI-assisted translation
At the longing of the folk who had gone to Goloka, the Lord beyond the highest, wishing to raise them up, desired the unearthly rāsa, so as to show plainly his own compassionate form. (52)

English Translation (of the Hindi)AI-assisted translation
At the wish of those who had gone to Goloka, the Lord beyond the highest, wishing to deliver them, desired to perform the unearthly rāsa play; and he desired the rāsa also in order to show his own compassionate body plainly. (52)
