English NotesAI-assisted translation
After this, following the ancient tradition (it was the custom to give brahmins a fee [unclear: in the form of] the price of barley and ginger; they were content with that much and were well pleased), once the worship was over the great sage Dhātā respectfully offered the learned men a generous fee as the redemption price of the barley and ginger, and then he, wishing for welfare, also duly dismissed his revered hosts of Fathers.
