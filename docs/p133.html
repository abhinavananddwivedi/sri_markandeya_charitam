Manuscript Note (EN)AI-assisted transcription
Metre: Drutavilambita. The last word of pāda 3 is read as "सुचारवो" (the Hindi: "beautiful sound"). Pāda 4 is written "परिचय प्रचलद्भिरदर्शयत्"; the verb is singular with a plural subject and is kept as written.
English Translation (of the Sanskrit)AI-assisted translation
By men with bands of various instruments, each apart, intent on fine dress, moving along and making them known, the beautiful sounds of the instruments each loved were displayed. (29)
