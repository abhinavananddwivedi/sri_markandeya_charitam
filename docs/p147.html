English NotesAI-assisted translation
Evening was coming on, and everyone was busy with work suited to their own nature. In the evening the lotus-cups were slowly closing one by one, so that several bees were shut inside those closing petals. Seeing them, the onlookers, judging it a fault of their own sight, looked at the bees shut in those lotus-cups and were sad, blaming their own sight (their eye).

चन्द्रमा का उदयास्त अनियत है किन्तु सूर्य का उदय अस्त सर्वदा अपने समय पर [unclear: समयबद्ध] निर्धारित है इसलिए क्रोध से अपने विधिपति चन्द्रमा का परित्याग कर, ये वारुणी दिशा रूपी पश्चिम दिग्वधू — अपने पति के प्रतिकूल सूर्य नियत समय पर उस वारुणी दिशा में उपस्थित हो जाता है, इसलिए पश्चिम दिशा रूपी नायिका ने कुलटा (व्यभिचारिणी) के समान उपपति सूर्य का आलिङ्गन नहीं किया क्या ? अर्थात् सूर्य अस्ताचल में डूब गये । ५८
English NotesAI-assisted translation
The moon's rising and setting are irregular, but the sun's rising and setting are always fixed to their own time. So, in anger, this bride of the western quarter, the direction of Varuṇa, cast off her lawful husband the moon; and the sun, unlike her husband, arrives in that western quarter at the fixed time. So did not the heroine, the western quarter, like an unchaste woman (an adulteress), embrace her paramour the sun? That is, the sun sank behind the western mountain. (58)
