Shlokamanuensis's hand
तमाचमय्यास्य सुताः सुतल्पकेऽ- ध्यशीशयन् कर्मकरा इव प्रभुम् । शिरःप्रदेशे जलसम्प्लुतं घटं निधाय याता गृहकर्मतत्पराः ॥ ४३ ॥
Manuscript NoteAI-assisted transcription
वृत्त: वंशस्थ (चारों पाद शुद्ध)। पाद ४ "निधाय याता" (पूर्व "निधाय गता", एक अक्षर कम)।
Manuscript Note (EN)AI-assisted transcription
Metre: Vaṃśastha (all four pādas scan). Pāda 4 reads "निधाय याता" (the earlier "निधाय गता" is one syllable short).
English Translation (of the Sanskrit)AI-assisted translation
His sons, having had him rinse his mouth, laid him on a good bed, as servants [would] their master; placing a pot filled with water at his head, they went off, intent on the work of the house. (43)

गृहस्वामी के पुत्रगण महात्मा के हाथ धुलाकर उन्हें अच्छे बिस्तर पर शयन कराये। उनके शिरः प्रदेश में जलपूर्ण कलश रखकर गृह के अन्य कार्य में जाकर लग गये ॥ ४३ ॥
English Translation (of the Hindi)AI-assisted translation
The master of the house's sons washed the great soul's hands and laid him on a good bed. Placing a pot full of water at his head, they went and busied themselves with other work of the house. (43)

English NotesAI-assisted translation
The householder's obedient sons, after the meal, washed his hands and feet and laid him on a good, soft, clean bed; placing a covered pot full of water at his head, and completing the task humbly, as a servant serves his master in full obedience, they busied themselves with their daily household work. (43)
Verse 44
Shlokamanuensis's hand
मुनिश्च तल्पे स्वमभीष्टदं प्रभुं तथा विषघ्नांश्च तथैव गारुडान् । मनून्स्मरन् सत्सुखशायितो मुहु- र्द्वितीययामे शयितुं प्रचक्रमे ॥ ४४ ॥
English Translation (of the Sanskrit)AI-assisted translation
And the sage on the bed, remembering his Lord, giver of what is desired, and the Gāruḍa mantras that destroy poison, lay down at ease and, in the second watch, began to sleep. (44)
Translationamanuensis's hand
महात्मा मृकण्डु बिस्तर पर अपने अभीष्टपूरक प्रभु को तथा विष से त्राण करने वाले गारुड मन्त्रों को स्मरण करते हुए द्वितीय याम में सुख पूर्वक शयनार्थ तत्पर हुए ॥ ४४ ॥
English Translation (of the Hindi)AI-assisted translation
The great soul Mṛkaṇḍu, on the bed, remembering his Lord who fulfils his wishes, and the Gāruḍa mantras that protect from poison, got ready to sleep at ease in the second watch. (44)
Notesgrandfather's hand
आगन्तुक ऋषि मृकण्ड ने भी अपने आस्तरण पर सुख पूर्वक सोते हुए, अपने अभीष्ट पूर्ण करने वाले परात्पर प्रभु का स्मरण करते हुए, रात्रि के विषादि बाधाओं से सुरक्षा की कामना से गरुड़ देव सम्बन्धी विविध 'गारुड' मन्त्रों का स्मरण करते हुए, रात्रि के द्वितीय प्रहर में सुख पूर्वक शयन किया। ४४
English NotesAI-assisted translation
The visiting sage Mṛkaṇḍa too, sleeping at ease on his bedding, remembering the Lord beyond the highest who fulfils his wishes, and remembering various Gāruḍa mantras relating to the god Garuḍa, wishing for protection from poison and other dangers of the night, slept at ease in the second watch of the night. (44)
