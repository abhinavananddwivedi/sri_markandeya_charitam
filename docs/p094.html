He also gathered the praṇītā and prokṣaṇī vessels made of vāruṇa wood (udumbara) with two sruva ladles, and, adorned with a fine juhū ladle, the araṇi churning sticks made of śamī and pīpal wood. (16)

English NotesAI-assisted translation
He arranged, for performing the sacrificial rites, the praṇītā and prokṣaṇī vessels made from the varuṇa tree approved by the Vedic scriptures, which in Hindi is called "varnā" (some also regard it as a synonym of udumbara, the gūlar); two sruva ladles (juhū); and araṇi sticks made of śamī and pīpal for kindling fire by friction. (16)
