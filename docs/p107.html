English NotesAI-assisted translation
"So now, at once, please bring turmeric, curd, dūb grass, betel nuts, akṣata (grains of rice), grain and fragrant flowers of various kinds. Let these (and the assembly of brahmin sages) begin the svastivācana, and let the phaladān (vāgdān, betrothal) of the snātaka sage Mṛkaṇḍa be completed." (41)

Manuscript NoteAI-assisted transcription
वृत्त: स्वागता (पाद १, २, ४ छन्द में पूर्णतः ठीक नहीं बैठते; पाठ हस्तलेख के अनुसार)। पाद १ "स्वजनगुणेभ्यः" (अनुवाद "स्वजनगुणों से युक्त" से पुष्ट), पाद २ "...जनार्दनशाश्वतात्" (अनुवाद "जनार्दन शाश्वत से भी अधिक पूजित"), पाद ४ "पूर्णमस्ति किमतोऽस्ति विचार्यम्" (अनुवाद "सब कुछ पूर्ण ही है, अब इसके आगे विचार क्या है")। पूर्व निष्कासन के "स्वजनयुतैश्च", "पूर्यते किमु विनिश्चयोऽत्र" हस्तलेख में नहीं हैं। इस श्लोक के नीचे टिप्पणी के लिए रिक्त स्थान छोड़ा गया है।
Manuscript Note (EN)AI-assisted transcription
Metre: Svāgatā (pādas 1, 2 and 4 do not scan perfectly; the text follows the manuscript). Pāda 1 reads "स्वजनगुणेभ्यः" (confirmed by the Hindi "स्वजनगुणों से युक्त"), pāda 2 "...जनार्दनशाश्वतात्" (Hindi "more honoured even than the eternal Janārdana"), and pāda 4 "पूर्णमस्ति किमतोऽस्ति विचार्यम्" (Hindi "everything is complete; what is there to consider beyond this?"). The earlier export's "स्वजनयुतैश्च" and "पूर्यते किमु विनिश्चयोऽत्र" are not in the manuscript. Space for commentary is left blank below this verse.
English Translation (of the Sanskrit)AI-assisted translation
"Even a foremost brahmin is honoured for the virtues of his own people, [more] than the eternal Janārdana; so, with you all present here, everything is complete. What more is there to consider?" (42)
