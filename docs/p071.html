Verse 21
Shlokamanuensis's hand
वामहस्तकुशकाण्डच दक्षिणे सत्पवित्रसहितं कुशत्रयम् । तर्जनीं जपवटोर्बहिस्कृतां वेदमातृजपतत्परान्मुनीन् ॥ २१ ॥
English Translation (of the Sanskrit)AI-assisted translation
[He saw] sages intent on the japa of the Mother of the Vedas (Gāyatrī), with a stalk of kuśa in the left hand and in the right three kuśa blades with the pavitra, the forefinger kept outside the japa bag. (21)
Translationamanuensis's hand
कहीं पर उन्होंने बायें हाथ में कुशों को दाहिने हाथ में पवित्री (पैंती) के साथ तीन कुशों को धारण किये हुए, जपकाल में ब्रह्मचारी की तर्जनी अंगुली को गोमुखी से बाहर करते हुए वेदमाता गायत्री मन्त्र के जप के तत्पर मुनियों को देखा ॥ २१ ॥
English Translation (of the Hindi)AI-assisted translation
He observed sages holding a bundle of kuśa grass in the left hand, and three sacred blades of kuśa grass alongside the ritual ring (pavitrī) in the right hand; keeping the index finger extended outside the rosary pouch (japa-vaṭī / gomukhī), they were wholly absorbed in repeating the sacred hymn of Mother Gāyatrī (the Mother of the Vedas).

English NotesAI-assisted translation
In one place he saw ever-attentive sages, holding a handful of kuśa in the left hand and in the right hand a pavitrī made of three kuśa roots, putting the forefinger out through the hole at the end of the japa bag, and engaged in the japa of the goddess Gāyatrī, Mother of the Vedas. (21)
