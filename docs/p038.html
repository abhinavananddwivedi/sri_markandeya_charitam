English NotesAI-assisted translation
Somewhere he heard rows of bees humming softly and sweetly; somewhere he also saw a row of blooming lotus plants, covered with soft green moss leaves, adorned in green garments, made to dance in various forms by the free stream of water, bending a little under the great weight of their flowers. (11)
Verse 12

ददर्श पीठानिव कच्छपान् क्वचित् परञ्च मीनान् मकरान् विसर्पतः । प्रवाह मध्ये शिशुमारमद्भूतं तथा परावृत्य निमज्जतं मुहुः ॥ १२ ॥
English Translation (of the Sanskrit)AI-assisted translation
Somewhere he saw turtles like wooden seats, elsewhere fish and makaras gliding, and in the middle of the stream a wonderful dolphin (śiśumāra) turning over and diving again and again. (12)

कहीं पर उन्होंने कछुओं को पीढ़े (पाटों) के समान, अन्यत्र मछलियों एवं मगरों को तैरते हुए, तथा धारा के बीच ऊपर उलट कर गोता लगाते हुए शिशुमारों (सोंसों) को देखा ॥ १२ ॥
English Translation (of the Hindi)AI-assisted translation
Somewhere he saw turtles like wooden seats (low stools), elsewhere fish and makaras swimming, and in the middle of the stream dolphins (śiśumāra, the sõs) turning over and diving. (12)

English NotesAI-assisted translation
Somewhere he saw turtles moving about like wooden seats (low stools); on the other side, large fish and large makaras swimming; and in the middle current of the great mass of water, wonderful creatures of the śiśumāra (sõs) kind, turning over and sinking and rising. (12)
