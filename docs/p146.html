English NotesAI-assisted translation
So, walking along the road, the wedding party saw a charming well with a very beautiful, lovely paved floor (whose floor [unclear: …] was splendidly built by very fine craftsmen), and seeing also the nipāna (that is, a pool next to the well, with a paved floor of the same kind, where animals drink water), they rested; and bringing laddus from the sweet-shops of the market, they ate them, drank water and were fully satisfied.
