English Translation (of the Sanskrit)AI-assisted translation
"Seeing the melted state of him whom Śambhu now humbly bore on his head, you, bearing his form and again bestowing his form, are seen as its melting form." (55)

English Translation (of the Hindi)AI-assisted translation
"Because Lord Śiva humbly holds you on his head, seeing now your melted state, you have again taken a form that, as befits, bestows the form of Śiva and the form of Viṣṇu; therefore you appear as the form melting with compassion." (55)

English NotesAI-assisted translation
"There Lord Śiva, with great humility, held you on his head, so that your form became invisible. But again, becoming the bringer of welfare to the world, wishing to grant worldly creatures the form of Śiva and Viṣṇu, you, made of water and tender with compassion, give direct sight of yourself in the form of this Gaṅgā." (55)
Verse 56
Shlokamanuensis's hand
अतिद्रवी यज्जगतो हिताय शिवस्य विष्णोरपि शक्तिरूर्जिता । शिरस्तनुभ्यामनुलालिता चिरं तदार्द्रमूर्तिर्भवती प्रदृश्यते ॥ ५६ ॥
Manuscript NoteAI-assisted transcription
वृत्त: वंशस्थ। पाद १ में ११ ही अक्षर हैं (एक अक्षर छूटा प्रतीत होता है); पाण्डुलिपि यथावत्। पाद २ "शक्तिरूर्जिता" (हिन्दी: "उर्जित (वर्द्धित)")। पूर्व पाठ "अतिक्षमी / शिवात्म विष्णोररीय शक्तिदर्जिता / धृता स्तनु भ्यामधुनाऽनुकम्पिता" भ्रान्त था।
Manuscript Note (EN)AI-assisted transcription
Metre: Vaṃśastha. Pāda 1 has only eleven syllables (one seems to have dropped out); it is kept as written. Pāda 2 reads "शक्तिरूर्जिता" (the Hindi has "उर्जित (वर्द्धित)", "strengthened (increased)"). The earlier reading ("अतिक्षमी / शिवात्म विष्णोररीय शक्तिदर्जिता / धृता स्तनु भ्यामधुनाऽनुकम्पिता") was wrong.
English Translation (of the Sanskrit)AI-assisted translation
"The exceedingly fluid power of Śiva and of Viṣṇu, strengthened for the good of the world and long cherished by [Śiva's] head and [Viṣṇu's] body: you are seen as its melting form." (56)

संसार-हितार्थ शिव-विष्णु की अतिद्रवी शक्ति उर्जित (वर्द्धित) हुई। भगवान् शिव ने शिर पर धारण कर चिरकाल तक लालित किया और भगवान् विष्णु ने चरणों में चिरकाल तक धारण कर लालित किया अत एव आप दयार्द्रमूर्ति आप दिखाई देती हैं ॥ ५६ ॥
English Translation (of the Hindi)AI-assisted translation
"For the good of the world, the exceedingly fluid power of Śiva and Viṣṇu was strengthened (increased). Lord Śiva held it on his head and cherished it for a long time, and Lord Viṣṇu held it at his feet for a long time and cherished it; therefore you appear as the form melting with compassion." (56)

English NotesAI-assisted translation
"To bring about the welfare of the world, Lord Śiva [unclear] held on his head, and Lord Viṣṇu held in his body, that goddess, the divine river, endowed with an exceedingly fluid power (which no one else in the world has). You, goddess Gaṅgā, in fluid form, embodying the energy and mass of radiance of those two, are yourself plainly seen as the very form of compassion." (56)
