Verse 31

Manuscript NoteAI-assisted transcription
वृत्त: स्वागता। पाद ३ का अन्तिम शब्द ("भवन्तं"/"भवन्तौ") अधिलिखित है। पाद ४ का आरम्भ ("प्रार्थनां") किसी पूर्व पाठ पर लिखा गया है, और अन्तिम शब्द अस्पष्ट है।
Manuscript Note (EN)AI-assisted transcription

English Translation (of the Sanskrit)AI-assisted translation
"In plain speech there is no deceit, so all people speak plainly. Therefore, noble one, I say it to you: may your honours make my request fruitful." (31)

Verse 32
Shlokamanuensis's hand
दाक्षिणा श्रुतमपूर्वगुणौघं स्नातकं किल मृकण्डुमहर्षिम् । आगतोऽस्मि तत एव चिकीर्षुः पाणिपीडनमहं तनयायाः ॥ ३२ ॥
Manuscript NoteAI-assisted transcription
वृत्त: स्वागता। पाद १ "दाक्षिणा श्रुतम्" — "दाक्षिणा" दाक्षि का तृतीया एकवचन है ("दाक्षि द्वारा सुना गया"), जो अनुवाद के "दाक्षी ने ... सुना है" से पुष्ट होता है। पूर्व निष्कासन का "दाक्षिणाच्छ्रुतम्" (पञ्चमी) व्याकरण-सम्मत नहीं था (दाक्षि की पञ्चमी "दाक्षेः" होती)।
Manuscript Note (EN)AI-assisted transcription
Metre: Svāgatā. Pāda 1 reads "दाक्षिणा श्रुतम्": "दाक्षिणा" is the instrumental singular of Dākṣi ("heard by Dākṣi"), confirmed by the Hindi "दाक्षी ने ... सुना है". The earlier export's "दाक्षिणाच्छ्रुतम्" (ablative) was ungrammatical; the ablative of Dākṣi would be "दाक्षेः".
English Translation (of the Sanskrit)AI-assisted translation
"The great sage Mṛkaṇḍu, a snātaka, whose flood of unrivalled virtues Dākṣi has heard of: for that very reason I have come, wishing to perform my daughter's marriage." (32)
Translationamanuensis's hand

English Translation (of the Hindi)AI-assisted translation
"Dākṣi has heard of the virtues of the snātaka Maharṣi Mṛkaṇḍu; to carry out that very purpose, the marriage of my daughter, I have come here." (32)

English NotesAI-assisted translation
"From the excellent sage Dākṣi I have constantly heard of the wonderful store of virtues of Sage Mṛkaṇḍu, and that this great sage is still a snātaka. That is why I have come here, wishing to celebrate my daughter's marriage with this great sage." (32)
