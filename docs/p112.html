English NotesAI-assisted translation
The people who saw these betrothal goods being carried along the road were very curious. They wanted to know where such a huge royal betrothal consignment was coming from and where it was going; so, out of eagerness, people asked along the road. In wonder, everyone kept asking where, and to whom, this matchless royal consignment was going. (52)
