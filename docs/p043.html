English NotesAI-assisted translation
Meanwhile, seeing that brahmacārī coming toward his house from quite far off, the master of the house bowed humbly; he also had all the members of his household and the children bow respectfully to him, and he respectfully worshipped that unknown brahmacārī guest who had come to his house at sunset. (21)
Verse 22
Shlokamanuensis's hand
उपाजहाराथ स यज्ञियां वृषीं सुविष्टरं पाद्यमथार्घ्यमुत्तमम् । ततः समाचान्तममुं सुबोधितं सवत्सधेन्वा मधुपर्कमाहरत् ॥ २२ ॥
English Translation (of the Sanskrit)AI-assisted translation
Then he brought him a sacrificial seat, a fine mat, water for the feet and an excellent arghya; then, when he had sipped water and was at ease, he brought the madhuparka, with a cow and calf. (22)
Translationamanuensis's hand
उन्होंने यज्ञिय आसन, बैठने के आसन, पाद्य, उत्तम अर्घ्य ब्रह्मचारी को प्रदान किया। पाद्यार्घ्य लेने के बाद सुखपूर्वक बैठे हुए ब्रह्मचारी को सवत्सा धेनु के माध्यम से मधुपर्क समर्पित किया ॥ २२ ॥
English Translation (of the Hindi)AI-assisted translation
He gave the brahmacārī a sacrificial seat, a seat to sit on, water for the feet and an excellent arghya. After he had taken the water and arghya and was seated at ease, he offered him the madhuparka by means of a cow with her calf. (22)
Notesgrandfather's hand
उस अतिथि ब्रह्मचारी के बैठने के लिए यज्ञीय आसन तथा पादप्रक्षालनार्थ जल, एवं अर्घ्य आदि उचित सत्कार सम्पादित कर उनके हाथ पैर धोकर आचमन कर सुखपूर्वक बैठ जाने के बाद अपनी सवत्सा गो देवी द्वारा सम्पादित मधुमिश्रित मधुपर्क भी समर्पित किया । २२
English NotesAI-assisted translation
For that brahmacārī guest he provided a sacrificial seat to sit on, water for washing the feet, arghya and other proper honours; after the guest had washed his hands and feet, sipped water and sat down at ease, he also offered madhuparka mixed with honey, prepared [with milk] from his cow-goddess with her calf. (22)
