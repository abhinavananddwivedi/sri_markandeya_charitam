English Translation (of the Sanskrit)AI-assisted translation
Then, placing on his head two good handfuls of the water that overpowers all sin, purified outwardly and cleansing himself within as well, he sipped [the water] three times from the kāya-tīrtha [of his palm]. (50)

English NotesAI-assisted translation
Sprinkling his head with two handfuls of the holy water of the goddess Gaṅgā, who uproots the whole mass of sin, and fully content with his outer purity, he sipped Gaṅgā water three times through the kāya-tīrtha (that is, the middle part of the right hand) for inner purity, and considered himself wholly purified. (50)
