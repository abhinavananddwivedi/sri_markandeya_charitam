English NotesAI-assisted translation
The most humble Sage Mudgala was coming with his companions, wishing to make a humble request for his daughter's marriage. On the way he had to pass through various districts, and everywhere he heard talk of Sage Mṛkaṇḍa's spiritual powers and greatness. Reaching his hermitage and finding Sage Mṛkaṇḍa, he began to speak to him thus. (23)

स्नातकं समुपश्रुत्य भवन्तं पाणिपीडनमहं तनयायाः । कर्तुमागतमवेहि मुने मां स्वीकृतिर्हि भवता करणीया ॥ २४ ॥
Manuscript NoteAI-assisted transcription
वृत्त: स्वागता। पाद ३ "कर्तुमागतमवेहि मुने मां" तथा पाद ४ "स्वीकृतिर्हि भवता करणीया" — दोनों अनुवाद ("विवाहार्थ आये हुए मुझे जानकर आप स्वीकृति प्रदान करें") से पुष्ट; पूर्व निष्कासन का "कर्तुमागत इहास्मि मुनीन्द्र / स्वीकुरुष्व हि मया क्रियमाणाम्" हस्तलेख में नहीं है। पाद १ में "पश्रु" संयुक्ताक्षर से पूर्व-वर्ण गुरु हो जाता है; कवि ने यहाँ "श्र" को स्थिति-कारक नहीं माना है।
Manuscript Note (EN)AI-assisted transcription
Metre: Svāgatā. Pāda 3 "कर्तुमागतमवेहि मुने मां" and pāda 4 "स्वीकृतिर्हि भवता करणीया" are both confirmed by the Hindi ("knowing me to have come for the marriage, please grant your consent"); the earlier export's "कर्तुमागत इहास्मि मुनीन्द्र / स्वीकुरुष्व हि मया क्रियमाणाम्" is not in the manuscript. In pāda 1 the conjunct "श्रु" would make the preceding syllable heavy; the poet does not count "श्र" as making position here.
English Translation (of the Sanskrit)AI-assisted translation
"Having heard that you are a snātaka (unmarried graduate), know me, O sage, to have come to perform my daughter's marriage; your consent must be given." (24)
