Verse 59
Shlokamanuensis's hand
वृक्षानुन्मूलयन्ती परमपदकृते कोटिशो जन्मजात- क्लेशानाकृष्य मातर्न भवति महिमा तावकः कस्य दृश्यः । किञ्चाहंकारमूढान्[unclear: विषयि]भवतो मार्गरोधप्रवृत्ता- नुत्तुङ्गानुत्तमाङ्गान्यणुरिव शकलान्याशु चक्रे गिरीणाम् ॥ ५९ ॥
English Translation (of the Sanskrit)AI-assisted translation
Uprooting the trees, and drawing away, for the sake of the highest abode, the afflictions born of millions of births: O Mother, to whom is your greatness not visible? Moreover, the lofty heads of the mountains, deluded by pride and set on blocking your path, you swiftly made into fragments, like atoms. (59)

English Translation (of the Hindi)AI-assisted translation
O Mother Gaṅgā! Uprooting trees, you draw away (destroy) the afflictions of their millions of births so that they may reach the highest abode (you show matchless compassion); who has not seen this greatness (distinction) in you? Not only that: the lofty peaks of the mountains, deluded by pride (like men), that block the middle path, you turn into particles, like small fragments. (59)

English NotesAI-assisted translation
Mother Gaṅgā! Uprooting the many firm, sturdy rows of trees that stand as obstacles in your path, you destroy the whole mass of afflictions of creatures who remain bound in the net of the bonds of karma through millions upon millions of births, so that they may constantly reach the highest abode; you deliver them. Who in this world has not seen this wonderful greatness of yours? Not only that, Mother: [beings] overcome by great pride, raising their heads high to display their superiority and → ego, standing as obstacles in the life of ordinary people: living beings, what of them? Even the lifeless: the mountain ranges, high-peaked with pride, with their loftiest summits blocking your path as obstacles, you scatter into particles, destroy their existence, give them the form of ordinary specks, and so keep them always mindful of what their existence really is. (59)

गर्जन्ती वैरिवृन्दे सपदि निजरवैः पापिनस्तर्जयन्ती मोक्षार्थं भूरिभाग्यानवनितलजुषः सज्जनांस्तारयन्ती । भक्त्युद्रेकात्कुतश्चिच्छरणमुपगतान् पावयन्ती तरङ्गै- र्मातर्गङ्गे निजाङ्के कलयसि सततं संसृतेर्गर्तमध्यात् ॥ ६० ॥
English Translation (of the Sanskrit)AI-assisted translation
Roaring at the host of enemies, at once rebuking sinners with your own sounds, carrying across to liberation the most fortunate good people who dwell on the earth, purifying with your waves those who have come to you for refuge from anywhere out of an overflow of devotion: O Mother Gaṅgā, you always take them into your own lap, out of the pit of worldly existence. (60)

English Translation (of the Hindi)AI-assisted translation
O Mother Gaṅgā! You roar so that enemies may take heed; with your murmuring sound you [unclear: rebuke] sinners; you carry across the fortunate good people living on this earth, for their swift liberation. Purifying with your waves the devotees who in places come to you for refuge out of an abundance of devotion, you lift them out of the pit of worldly life and take them into your lap. (60)
