Arriving there, in the middle of the pure square, rich in the carpets spread by the son of Śakti, honoured by the excellent sages with water for their feet, they sat down, endowed with great splendour. (57)

There, in the middle of the holy crossroads, honoured by the sages with water for the feet and the like, they all rested with great honour on the bedding spread beforehand by Parāśara, the son of Śakti. (57)

English NotesAI-assisted translation
As soon as these people reached Sage Mṛkaṇḍa's hermitage there, all the guests washed their hands, feet and so on in due form and, very happily, freed from their great weariness, settled down to rest on the fine carpets that Sage Parāśara, the son of Śakti, had duly spread in the courtyard. (57)
Verse 58
Shlokamanuensis's hand
स्थाप्य मङ्गलघटं च वरस्य स्थापिते सुकलशे कनकस्य । पूजिते गणपतौ सहगौर्या सुग्रहैश्च वरुणोऽर्चित देवैः ॥ ५८ ॥
Manuscript NoteAI-assisted transcription
वृत्त: स्वागता। पाद ३ "गणपतौ सहगौर्या" (पूर्व निष्कासन का "गणपतिः सगौर्या", १० वर्ण, भ्रान्त था)। पाद ४ "सुग्रहैश्च वरुणोऽर्चित देवैः" (पूर्व निष्कासन का "वरुणैर्वरवन्द्यैः" हस्तलेख में नहीं है)। पाद ४ की व्याकरणिक संरचना कुछ शिथिल है; पाठ हस्तलेख के अनुसार।
Manuscript Note (EN)AI-assisted transcription
Metre: Svāgatā. Pāda 3 reads "गणपतौ सहगौर्या"; the earlier export's "गणपतिः सगौर्या" (10 syllables) was a misreading. Pāda 4 reads "सुग्रहैश्च वरुणोऽर्चित देवैः"; the earlier export's "वरुणैर्वरवन्द्यैः" is not in the manuscript. The grammar of pāda 4 is somewhat loose; the text follows the manuscript.
English Translation (of the Sanskrit)AI-assisted translation
Having set up the auspicious pot of the groom, and a fine golden kalaśa being installed, Gaṇapati with Gaurī was worshipped, and Varuṇa was worshipped together with the good planets and the gods. (58)

Wishing for good fortune, the groom installed a golden kalaśa and worshipped Gaṇapati with Gaurī, the planets, and Varuṇa together with all the gods. (58)

English NotesAI-assisted translation
Knowing the auspicious rite of his betrothal, the groom Mṛkaṇḍu himself installed a golden kalaśa. On it he duly worshipped Lord Gaṇapati, the remover of obstacles, together with the goddess Gaurī; then he also duly worshipped the god Varuṇa together with all the gods and the nine planets beginning with the Sun. (58)
