English Translation (of the Sanskrit)AI-assisted translation
[Partly unclear.] Before him, falling from a cleft in the snowy mountain, issuing like [water through] a gateway at Gomukha, he saw the stream that has long dwelt on Śiva's head. (25)

हिमालय की गुहा से निर्गत गोमुख से नीचे की ओर गिरती हुई। अनादिकाल से अधिष्टित शिव के मस्तक पर चिरकाल से विद्यमान धारा को उन्होंने देखा ॥ २५ ॥
English Translation (of the Hindi)AI-assisted translation
[The stream] falling downward from the Gomukha that issues from a cave of the Himālaya: he saw the stream that has been present for a long time on the head of Śiva, established since time without beginning. (25)

English NotesAI-assisted translation
Here he saw the clear stream of the goddess Bhāgīrathī, which comes out of a cave of the Himālaya mountain and falls downward to Gomukh, murmuring, and which has been present with great force since time without beginning on the head of Lord Sadāśiva. (25)

English Translation (of the Sanskrit)AI-assisted translation
[Partly unclear.] Gazing long and unblinking in that direction and finding contentment like nectar, he, wishing to live there, followed [the river]. (26)
Translationamanuensis's hand
उस दिशा की ओर निर्निमेष दृष्टि गड़ा कर अमृतप्राप्ति तुल्य आनन्द का अवलोकन कर। मनुश्रेष्ठ के पालन तत्पर निवास करने की इच्छा करते हुए वे ऊपर चल पड़े ॥ २६ ॥
English Translation (of the Hindi)AI-assisted translation
Fixing his unblinking gaze in that direction, he experienced joy like obtaining nectar. Wishing to live [there], devoted to keeping [the ways] of the best of Manu's line, he set off upstream. (26)
Notesgrandfather's hand
मृकण्डु ने इस प्रवाहमयी अतिशय प्रभाशोषित भगवती भगीरथी के कलकल निनाद सुन अपलक नयनों से अनन्त काल तक देखते हुए अमृत प्राप्ति के समान परम शान्ति प्राप्त किया, आनन्द समुद्र में गोता लगाते रहे, सम्प्रति उस पावनी भगवती गङ्गा के तट पर निवास करने के लिए उसकी धारा का अनुसरण करते हुए उसके पुण्यप्रद तटों के किनारे २ चल पड़े ॥ २६ ॥
English NotesAI-assisted translation
Hearing the murmuring of this flowing, very radiant goddess Bhāgīrathī and gazing at it with unblinking eyes for endless time, Mṛkaṇḍu obtained the highest peace, like obtaining nectar, and kept diving into an ocean of joy. Now, to live on the bank of that holy goddess Gaṅgā, he set off following her stream along her bank, which gives merit. (26)
