Manuscript Note (EN)AI-assisted transcription
Metre: Drutavilambita. Pāda 1: "गतीडिता-" = gati + īḍita (the Hindi has "praised"). Pāda 2: "धडधडायित" (onomatopoeia; the Hindi has "rattling sound"). The verse has no verb "पश्यत"; it carries over from the context.
