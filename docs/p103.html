Manuscript NoteAI-assisted transcription
वृत्त: स्वागता। पाद २ "सारगुणतोऽस्त्यनुरूपा" और पाद ३ "भवतैव" हस्तलेख के अनुसार (पूर्व निष्कासन का "साऽत्र गुणतो" तथा "भवता वै" भ्रान्त थे)।
Manuscript Note (EN)AI-assisted transcription
Metre: Svāgatā. Pāda 2 "सारगुणतोऽस्त्यनुरूपा" and pāda 3 "भवतैव" follow the manuscript; the earlier export's "साऽत्र गुणतो" and "भवता वै" were misreadings.
English Translation (of the Sanskrit)AI-assisted translation
"I have nothing at all to give here; but in excellence of virtues she is a fit match. In the marriage of these two, every rite is to be carried out by you alone." (33)

English NotesAI-assisted translation
"Great sage! I have nothing to give as dowry (yautuka); I cannot give anything. But yes, my daughter is fully worthy of him in virtue. [unclear: Revered one]! In the matter of these two being married, all the rites and all the tasks are for you alone to perform." (33)
Verse 34
Shlokamanuensis's hand
वल्कलानि परिधाय तरूणा- माश्रमे हिमजलादि सहित्वा । कन्दमूलफलभोजनशीला जङ्गलेषु मुनयो निवसामः ॥ ३४ ॥
Manuscript NoteAI-assisted transcription
वृत्त: स्वागता। पाद २ "माश्रमे" (तरूणाम् आश्रमे) — पूर्व निष्कासन का "मातपे" हस्तलेख में नहीं है (हिन्दी अनुवाद में "आतप" शब्द काटा गया है)।
Manuscript Note (EN)AI-assisted transcription
Metre: Svāgatā. Pāda 2 reads "माश्रमे" (tarūṇām āśrame); the earlier export's "मातपे" is not in the manuscript. In the Hindi translation the word "आतप" is struck out.
English Translation (of the Sanskrit)AI-assisted translation
"Wearing the bark of trees, enduring snow, water and the like in the hermitage, living on roots, tubers and fruits, we sages dwell in the forests." (34)
Translationamanuensis's hand
वृक्षों के छाल पहन कर ठण्डी गर्मी को सहन कर कन्द-मूल-फलादि भक्षण करने वाले हम मुनिगण इस वन्यभूमि में रहते हैं ॥ ३४ ॥
English Translation (of the Hindi)AI-assisted translation
"Wearing the bark of trees, enduring cold and heat, we sages, who live on roots, tubers, fruits and the like, dwell in this forest land." (34)
Notesgrandfather's hand
अहो मुनीन्द्र! हम लोग सदा वृक्षों की छाल पहनकर, वहाँ की भीषण सर्दी गर्मी (शीत उष्णतादि) अत्यन्त शीतल बर्फीली नदियों का जल आदि का सहन करते हुए जंगली फल मूल कन्दादि का भोजन कर जङ्गलों में रहते हुए मुनिवृत्ति से अपना जीवन निर्वाह करते हुए रह रहे हैं। ३४
English NotesAI-assisted translation
"O lord of sages! We always wear the bark of trees and endure the fierce cold and heat there (cold, heat and the like) and the water of icy, snow-fed rivers; we eat wild fruits, roots and tubers, live in the forests, and sustain our life by the way of life of sages." (34)
