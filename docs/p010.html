English Translation (of the Sanskrit)AI-assisted translation
And from those two was born, by his own radiance, a son, the sage named Mṛkaṇḍu, of mighty splendour; having completed his learning and satisfied the twice-born, he took leave of his teachers and completed his student life (samāvartana). (19)

From Dhātā and Āyatī was born the sage named Mṛkaṇḍu, a great ascetic by his radiance and endowed with growing splendour. He studied, practised good vows, pleased his teachers and completed the samāvartana rite. (19)

English NotesAI-assisted translation
From the very radiant Dhātā and Āyatī a son named Mṛkaṇḍa was born, very splendid and a great ascetic, of great lustre. He studied all the Vedas and Vedāṅgas thoroughly, practised good conduct properly, worshipped his teachers with his unequalled devotion as a disciple, and, with their permission, after the samāvartana rite completed the rite marking the end of study; and, with their permission, ready to follow the householder's dharma, he left the teacher's household. (19)

When that broad-minded great soul Mṛkaṇḍu made efforts toward the householder's stage, many wishes arose in his mind. Then, on his father's advice, he began to travel the earth to search for land fit to live on. (20)

English NotesAI-assisted translation
When the very generous-hearted, high-minded sage Mṛkaṇḍa began to think about the householder's stage, then, at his revered father's command, many thoughts kept rising and falling in his mind about obtaining land, suitable for himself (fit for a brahmin), on which to build a dwelling. He began to search the best places in all the dvīpas, going to various places and inspecting them. (20)
