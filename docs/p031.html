English Translation (of the Sanskrit)AI-assisted translation
"Unceasing in your course even in the wheel of spreading teaching, [arriving] before any obstructer could obstruct, you came down to earth of yourself and, from your gravity, bring the multitudes the message of steadfastness. English Translation (of the Hindi)AI-assisted translation
"Though your motion does not stop in the wheel of spreading teaching, before the obstructer could obstruct you came with gravity to the surface of the earth, giving the multitudes of people the message of steadfastness. When one counted lowly in the world's eyes comes to you for refuge, you make his heart pure; thus you reveal an unprecedented mystery in the world. Therefore I take refuge at your lotus feet." (61)

English NotesAI-assisted translation
"To establish in the whole world the importance of the [unclear: surge of devotion], and to teach how to attain devotion unhindered despite many obstacles, you came to this earth, ever moving, teaching supreme steadfastness and giving creatures the message to go forward. जगदाधार माँ गङ्गे! उपर्युक्त तथ्यों तथा आपकी निष्काम निर्बाध संसार सागर से सन्तारण करने की अपूर्व क्षमता और शक्ति जानकर तुम्हें ही अपना सर्वस्व समर्पित कर देना उचित एवं स्वाभाविक है। अन्य स्थानों (देवों तथा मानवों) में आत्मनिवेदन समर्पण करना तो एकमात्र औपचारिकता (प्रदर्शन) मात्र है। माँ प्रत्यक्ष स्वरूपवाली तुम्हें छोड़कर अन्य अदृश्य ज्योति निर्गुण निराकार (प्रकाशपुञ्ज ब्रह्म) का ध्यान कैसे किया जा सकता है? जो लोग निर्गुण निराकार परब्रह्म ज्योतिःस्वरूप के दर्शन का व्यर्थ में दम्भ भरते हैं वे मात्र केवल ढोंगी होते हैं। क्योंकि मन वाणी को अगम अगोचर (सो जानै जो पावै)। न तत्र वाग्गच्छति, न मनः, नेन्द्रियाणि। अतः सगुण स्नेहमयी प्रत्यक्ष मूर्ति मातृस्वरूपा भगवती गङ्गा का हम सदा आश्रयण करते हैं। ६२
English NotesAI-assisted translation
"Mother Gaṅgā, support of the world! Knowing the facts above, and your matchless, selfless and unhindered power and ability to carry us across the ocean of worldly existence, it is right and natural to surrender our all to you alone. Self-offering and surrender in other places (to gods and to men) is a mere formality (a show). Mother, leaving you, whose form is before our eyes, how can one meditate on some other invisible light, without qualities and without form (brahman, a mass of light)? Those who vainly boast of seeing the formless supreme brahman, without qualities, whose nature is light, are mere pretenders. For it is beyond the reach of mind and speech ('he knows it who attains it'): 'na tatra vāg gacchati, na manaḥ, nendriyāṇi' (speech does not go there, nor the mind, nor the senses). Therefore we always take refuge in the goddess Gaṅgā, whose form is a mother's, loving, endowed with qualities, visible before us." (62)
