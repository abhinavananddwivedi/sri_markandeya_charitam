Manuscript Note (EN)AI-assisted transcription
Metre: Vaṃśastha. The second half of pāda 1 ("…मपश्यदसौ दिदृक्षौ") does not scan and is unclear in the manuscript; kept as it was.
English Translation (of the Sanskrit)AI-assisted translation
At the end of the night he, wishing to see [it], saw in a dream his own household life, beginning with his marriage; seized by fear of ill, he got up and long meditated on Kāśī and Kukkuṭa. (45)

English Translation (of the Hindi)AI-assisted translation
At the end of the night, that is, at dawn, he saw a dream: he saw (in the dream) his household life before his marriage. Filled with fear of something bad, as soon as he rose he meditated for a long time on Kāśī and Kukkuṭa. (45)

English NotesAI-assisted translation
In the last watch of the night the sage Mṛkaṇḍa saw a wonderful dream of his state after his marriage was complete; it seemed that he had already had his wedding. At this his heart shuddered with fear of something bad; he sat up at once and for a long time kept remembering the holy city of Kāśī and Kukkuṭeśvara Mahādeva. (45)

गृहाधिपं नित्यविधौ समाहृतं प्रयातुमापृच्छुमथानुपागतः । प्रणम्य गोत्राद्यनुकीर्त्य सादरं कृताञ्जलिर्वाक्यमुवाच धीरवान् ॥ ४६ ॥
English Translation (of the Sanskrit)AI-assisted translation
He went to the master of the house, who was engaged in his daily rites, to ask leave to depart; bowing and respectfully reciting his gotra and so on, with joined palms, the steady one spoke these words. (46)

He came to the master of the house, who was engaged in his daily rites, to ask leave to set out. Bowing respectfully, naming his gotra and so on, with joined hands, that steady one said this. (46)

English NotesAI-assisted translation
To the householder sage, who was engaged in his daily morning rites, the noble-minded sage Mṛkaṇḍa came to ask permission to leave; reciting his gotra, he bowed humbly, joined both hands, and very steadily and humbly said these things. (46)
