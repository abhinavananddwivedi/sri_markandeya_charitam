Further along, he observed hungry cranes (sārasas) screeching flatteringly on both banks of the Gaṅgā, resembling sycophants and gluttons who persistently linger and fawn at the threshold of wealthy magnates, ignoring every rebuff solely to satisfy their belly.

English NotesAI-assisted translation
Beholding hunger-stricken cranes screeching plaintively along both shores of the sacred river, it seemed as though they were singing flattering hymns to Mother Gaṅgā solely to fill their stomachs. The sage compared them to shameless worldly flatterers who persistently haunt the doorways of wealthy grandees, enduring repeated humiliations and rebuffs just to gratify their appetite.

Translationamanuensis's hand
पुनः उन्होंने असमय में ताण्डव-नृत्य निरत कमल की विशेषता से अनभिज्ञ बगुलों द्वारा तिरस्कृत पद्मसमूहों को कुमान्त्र्य जनों से बार-बार प्रताड़ित, राजाओं के सन्मान्त्रियों के समान देखा ॥ ६ ॥
English TranslationAI-assisted translation
He then watched insensitive herons ruthlessly trampling and tearing apart delicate lotus clusters through unseasonable and erratic movements—a scene evoking the plight of wise and noble counselors in a royal court repeatedly undermined and harassed by deceitful, uncultured sycophants.

फिर मुनि मृकण्डु ने उन अनजाने गुणदोष का विचार न कर सकने वालों मूर्ख बगुलों (बकपङ्क्ति) को भी देखा, जिन्हें अत्यन्त सुभग मनोरम मृदु हास विलास करती कमलिनी के सौगन्ध्य माधुर्य सौन्दर्य की परिचय हीनता के कारण उनका विविध रूपों में तिरस्कार पूर्वक बार २ अपमान किया जा रहा है, जैसे किसी राजा की राज्य सभा में उत्तम उदात्त कोटि के उच्च विचारशील मन्त्रियों पर भी कुटिल कलुषित अविवेकी मन्त्रियों का दुष्प्रभाव बार २ पड़ता या डाला जाता है ।
English NotesAI-assisted translation
Sage Mṛkaṇḍu observed foolish cranes, utterly oblivious to the subtle fragrance, beauty, and nobility of blossoming lotuses, repeatedly crushing and trampling the delicate blooms. This poignant imagery brought to his mind the tragic dysfunction of royal assemblies where virtuous, far-sighted ministers of the highest integrity are continually marginalized, silenced, and tormented by cunning, corrupt courtiers.
