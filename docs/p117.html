Verse 61
Shlokamanuensis's hand
स्वस्तिवाचकवरैः प्रतितान्सो- ऽथाक्षतान् स्वशिरसा [unclear: बिभ्रदभात्] । देवमण्डलविसर्जनपूर्वं प्रागसौ स्वकुलदेवमुपास्त ॥ ६१ ॥
Manuscript NoteAI-assisted transcription
वृत्त: स्वागता। पाद १ "स्वस्तिवाचकवरैः प्रतितान्सोऽ-" (प्रतितान् = प्रदत्तान्), पाद २ "थाक्षतान् स्वशिरसा बिभ्र..." — पाद २ का अन्त अस्पष्ट है। पाद ४ "प्रागसौ स्वकुलदेवमुपास्त" (पूर्व निष्कासन का "प्रागतो स्वकुलदेवमुपास्त" भ्रान्त था)।
Manuscript Note (EN)AI-assisted transcription
Metre: Svāgatā. Pāda 1 reads "स्वस्तिवाचकवरैः प्रतितान्सोऽ-" (pratitān = given) and pāda 2 "थाक्षतान् स्वशिरसा बिभ्र..."; the end of pāda 2 is unclear. Pāda 4 reads "प्रागसौ स्वकुलदेवमुपास्त"; the earlier export's "प्रागतो स्वकुलदेवमुपास्त" was a misreading.
English Translation (of the Sanskrit)AI-assisted translation
He, bearing on his head the akṣata given by the excellent reciters of blessings, worshipped his family deity before the dismissal of the assembly of gods. (61)

English NotesAI-assisted translation
Sage Mṛkaṇḍu humbly placed on his head the heap of holy akṣata given by the most learned assembly reciting the svastivācana. And before the dismissal of the gods installed before him, Gaurī, Gaṇapati and the others, he also offered prayers to his revered family deities. (61)
Verse 62
Shlokamanuensis's hand
सम्भ्रमेण परया किल भक्त्या विप्रवृन्दमपि दाक्षिसनाथम् । आशिषा किल विवर्धिततेजा- स्तं पराशरमुनिं प्रणनाम ॥ ६२ ॥
Manuscript NoteAI-assisted transcription
वृत्त: स्वागता। श्लोक के ऊपर बाएँ हाशिये में "सम्भ्रमेण" (पाद १ का पहला शब्द) अलग से लिखा है। पाद २ "दाक्षिसनाथम्" (दाक्षि के साथ; अनुवाद "दाक्षि से सनाथित" से पुष्ट); पूर्व निष्कासन का "दक्षिणनाथम्" भ्रान्त था। पाद ४ "स्तं" (पूर्व निष्कासन का "स्ते" भ्रान्त)।
Manuscript Note (EN)AI-assisted transcription
Metre: Svāgatā. Above the verse, in the left margin, "सम्भ्रमेण" (the first word of pāda 1) is written separately. Pāda 2 reads "दाक्षिसनाथम्" ("accompanied by Dākṣi", confirmed by the Hindi "दाक्षि से सनाथित"); the earlier export's "दक्षिणनाथम्" was a misreading. Pāda 4 begins "स्तं"; the earlier export's "स्ते" was wrong.
English Translation (of the Sanskrit)AI-assisted translation
With eager reverence and supreme devotion he bowed to the assembly of brahmins, together with Dākṣi, and then, his splendour increased by their blessing, he bowed to the sage Parāśara. (62)

With full devotion he bowed to the assembly of brahmins accompanied by Dākṣi, and, his splendour increased by their blessing, he also bowed to the sage Parāśara. (62)

English NotesAI-assisted translation
After the dismissal of the kalaśa, Sage Mṛkaṇḍu respectfully bowed to all the sages and learned men headed by Dākṣi. Having received the blessing of all the sages and brahmins, Mṛkaṇḍu, radiant with his own inner splendour, also bowed to Maharṣi Parāśara with faith and humility. (62)
