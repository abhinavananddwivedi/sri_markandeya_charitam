English NotesAI-assisted translation
Although Sage Mṛkaṇḍu had come here only a very few days before, his noble virtues, his conduct and his noble dealings left a remarkable impression of his worth on the local people. As a result he came to be regarded as the best among all the excellent people of the Dardara region, and his fame spread in every direction. (19)
