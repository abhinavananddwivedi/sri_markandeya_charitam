He who is master of all that moves, the four-faced one, created the flowering [plants]; and the sky, the earth, the path of the wind and heaven: the fourfold ground for embodied beings. (9)

English Translation (of the Hindi)AI-assisted translation
The four-faced Prajāpati Brahmā, the self-controlled one, having mastered himself, created among all moving beings those with flowers (that is, trees); then he created the fourfold creation that supports embodied beings: sky, earth, the path of the wind, heaven and the rest. (9)

अतिशय नियतेन्द्रिय आत्मसंयमी उस हिरण्यगर्भ (जिसने अपनी प्राकृत स्थिति में सर्वदृक् होने के कारण सभी दिशाओं में समान रूप से समदर्शन करने के कारण चतुर्मुख नाम से प्रख्यात हुए) ने सम्पूर्ण ध्यान किया, इच्छा से विविध चेष्टाओं के माध्यम अनेक पुष्पावलियों से सुरभित अनेक विविध पादपों की उत्पत्ति की, तदनन्तर अपने प्राकृत प्रक्रिया से विविध शरीरधारियों के जीवनाधार स्वरूप द्युलोक (देवलोक), पृथ्वीलोक, वायुलोक (आकाश), अन्तरिक्ष, स्वर्गलोक, इन चार प्रकार के प्राणियों के अनेक गुणों के आधार पर रहने के लिए विविध लोकों का निर्माण किया। ९
English NotesAI-assisted translation
That Hiraṇyagarbha, with senses wholly restrained and self-controlled (who, seeing all in his natural state and looking equally in all directions, became famous by the name "four-faced"), meditated fully, and by his will, through various efforts, produced many kinds of plants fragrant with many rows of flowers. Then, by his natural process, he made various worlds as the basis of life for various embodied beings: the bright world (world of the gods), the earth, the world of wind (the sky), the mid-region and heaven, for these four kinds of beings to live in according to their many qualities. (9)

Again, that very wise Prajāpati thought about mental creation, with qualities that are the support and the supported of creation. After thinking, he created the gods, the sages, the hosts of lights and the planets. (10)

English NotesAI-assisted translation
After this, the four-faced Prajāpati, self-born, powerful, very wise and of complete majesty, thought deeply within himself about making a mental creation, the support of creation and the basis of much, rich in various qualities.
