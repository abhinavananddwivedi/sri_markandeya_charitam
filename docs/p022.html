Shlokamanuensis's hand
क्रियाश्च कुर्वन् परमेश्वरेऽर्पये- त्सनातनी तस्य गतिः सुनिश्चिता । क्रियात्मकस्यास्य हि दायरूपकः [unclear] विना च तां कः क्षणमत्र तिष्ठति ॥ ४३ ॥
Manuscript NoteAI-assisted transcription
वृत्त: वंशस्थ (चारों पाद शुद्ध)। पाद ३ का उत्तरार्ध ("हि दायरूपकः") अधिलिखित और अनिश्चित है; हिन्दी ("इस क्रियात्मक का यही अधिकार है") और टिप्पणी ("दायित्व") के आधार पर पढ़ा गया। पूर्व पाठ "क्रियास्वेव कुर्वन्त्येवमेव तद्वत् / त्सन्नाम्नि… / क्रियात्मक स्वाभावो ह्यस्य देशस्य / विना किञ्चित्कोऽपि तिष्ठेद्विधाव" भ्रान्त था।
Manuscript Note (EN)AI-assisted transcription
Metre: Vaṃśastha (all four pādas scan). The second half of pāda 3 ("हि दायरूपकः") is overwritten and uncertain; it is read with the help of the Hindi ("this is the right of what is of the nature of action") and the note ("दायित्व", duty). The earlier reading ("क्रियास्वेव कुर्वन्त्येवमेव तद्वत् / त्सन्नाम्नि… / क्रियात्मक स्वाभावो ह्यस्य देशस्य / विना किञ्चित्कोऽपि तिष्ठेद्विधाव") was wrong.
English Translation (of the Sanskrit)AI-assisted translation
"Doing your works, offer them to the Supreme Lord; the eternal goal of one who does so is assured. This is the [unclear: birthright] of what is of the nature of action; for without it, who here stays even for a moment?" (43)

English NotesAI-assisted translation
"Dear child! Constantly doing good deeds in life, always keep offering them to God, for this is a natural law for all time, laid down by rule. This kind of constant activity is indeed the very basis (a duty); for can anyone remain even for a moment without some action?" (43)

Shlokamanuensis's hand
निवृत्तिमार्गस्य दुरूहता तथा सुदुर्गतिः किं विदिताऽस्ति नावयोः । समादरस्तत्र च पूर्णकामयो- रसक्तयोः कर्मसु पश्य कौतुकम् ॥ ४४ ॥
Manuscript NoteAI-assisted transcription
वृत्त: वंशस्थ (चारों पाद शुद्ध)। "आवयोः" (हम दोनों को) — नर-नारायण स्वयं के विषय में बोल रहे हैं। पूर्व पाठ "निवृत्तिमार्गीय गुरुता तथा सुदुर्गतिः / किं विदिताहित समादरस्तथा / च पूर्णकामानां- / र्माऽसक्तेः…" भ्रान्त था।
Manuscript Note (EN)AI-assisted transcription
Metre: Vaṃśastha (all four pādas scan). "आवयोः" ("to us two"): Nara and Nārāyaṇa are speaking of themselves. The earlier reading ("निवृत्तिमार्गीय गुरुता तथा सुदुर्गतिः / किं विदिताहित समादरस्तथा / च पूर्णकामानां- / र्माऽसक्तेः…") was wrong.
English Translation (of the Sanskrit)AI-assisted translation
"Are the hardness of the path of renunciation and its grave misfortune unknown to us two? And yet see the wonder: the honour there of us two, whose desires are fulfilled and who are unattached in our actions." (44)

English Translation (of the Hindi)AI-assisted translation
"Do we not know the hardness and the grave misfortune of the path of renunciation? We know them. But see this wonder: those who are unattached in action and whose desires are fulfilled are honoured there." (44)
Notesgrandfather's hand
चिरञ्जीवक! अब तुम संसार से विरक्त होकर निवृत्ति मार्ग का अनुसरण करने वालों की विविध कठिनाइयों तथा उस कारण होने वाली अनेकविध दुर्दशाओं, दुर्गतियों की ओर ध्यान दो, क्या उन शारीरिक यंत्रणाओं द्वारा आत्मा का उत्पीडन नहीं किया जाता? यह बात सही है? प्रवृत्ति मार्ग में निरन्तर रहने वाले किन्तु असक्त भावना से सांसारिक कार्यों में संलग्न होते हुए भी पूर्णकाम (कर्मफल त्यागने वाली प्रवृत्ति) सद्गृहस्थों का समाज में अतिशय समादर नहीं होता? यह लौकिक आश्चर्यपूर्ण कौतुक (उत्कण्ठा) क्या स्पष्ट नहीं दीखती? ४४
English NotesAI-assisted translation
"Long-lived one! Now consider the many hardships of those who turn away from the world and follow the path of renunciation, and the manifold sufferings and misfortunes that come of it: is the soul not tormented by those bodily torments? Is this not so? Are not good householders, who remain always on the path of active life (pravṛtti) and, though engaged in worldly work, do it with unattached feeling and are fulfilled in desire (disposed to give up the fruits of action), greatly honoured in society? Is this wonderful worldly marvel (eagerness) not plain to see?" (44)
