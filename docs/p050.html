English Translation (of the Sanskrit)AI-assisted translation
As he was talking, his sons, carrying water, called them to the meal; then, inside the house, he who knew the householder's duties first offered the vaiśvadeva bali. (35)

As they were talking thus, boys with water in their hands called them to the meal. Then the householder, inside the house, offered the vaiśvadeva bali in accordance with the householder's dharma. (35)

English NotesAI-assisted translation
These things were still being discussed when the householder sage's son came with a pot of water and respectfully invited them to eat. Then the householder sage went inside the house and completed the first duty, the vaiśvadeva bali, in keeping with the conduct of the householder's dharma. (35)

ततोऽतिथिं सत्कृतमभ्युपाविश- न्नेकशाकैः सहसूपसंयुतम् । पयोविधानैः स किलाटकूर्चिकैः समानिनायौदनमिन्दुसन्निभम् ॥ ३६ ॥
Manuscript NoteAI-assisted transcription
वृत्त: वंशस्थ। पाद २ पाण्डुलिपि में "न्नेकशाकैः" लिखा है, जो एक अक्षर कम है; अभिप्रेत "(उपाविश)न्ननेकशाकैः" प्रतीत होता है; यथावत्।
Manuscript Note (EN)AI-assisted transcription
Metre: Vaṃśastha. Pāda 2 is written "न्नेकशाकैः", one syllable short; "(उपाविश)न्ननेकशाकैः" seems intended; kept as written.
English Translation (of the Sanskrit)AI-assisted translation
Then he sat by the honoured guest and brought rice like the moon, with many vegetables and soups, and with milk dishes, kilāṭa (thickened milk) and kūrcikā (curds). (36)
Translationamanuensis's hand

English Translation (of the Hindi)AI-assisted translation
Then, sitting near the honoured guest, rice bright as the moon was served, with many kinds of vegetables and dishes, kūrcika with dal, and milk dishes. (36)
Notesgrandfather's hand
इसके बाद पूर्व सत्कृत किए गये अतिथि के सन्निकट गृही मुनि ने बैठकर विविध प्रकार के सामयिक शाकों के साथ दाल और विविध प्रकार के दही, घी, पनीर, रबड़ी आदि स्निग्ध भोज्य पदार्थों के साथ चन्द्रमा के कान्ति के सदृश अत्यन्त धवल चावल का भात भी परसा । ३६
English NotesAI-assisted translation
After this the householder sage sat near the guest, who had already been honoured, and served rice very white like the moon's light, with various seasonal vegetables and dal, and various rich foods such as curd, ghee, paneer and rabṛī. (36)
