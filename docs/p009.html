English NotesAI-assisted translation
For the increase of offspring, the great sage Manu married his younger daughter Devahūti to the excellent sage Kardama. From them a daughter named Khyāti was born, who was married to the excellent maharṣi Bhṛgu, born of Hiraṇyagarbha; to produce various kinds of creatures, he began with Khyāti, [grand]daughter of Manu, the varied process of developing the order of creation. (17)
