English Translation (of the Sanskrit)AI-assisted translation
"What is there in living on the land of Śāka?" [So thinking,] he passed over fruitful Plakṣa and the rest, and saw the land shining with gold (Jambūdvīpa), well frequented and surrounded on every side by the ocean. (23)

English Translation (of the Hindi)AI-assisted translation
What will come of serving Śākadvīpa? That is, it is useless. He passed over fruitful Plakṣadvīpa. [He saw the land that is] joined with the Jambū river, and so shining, and surrounded on all sides by the sea. (23)

English NotesAI-assisted translation
"What can be done by living in this ordinary Śākadvīpa?" Thinking of its pointlessness, he passed over Plakṣadvīpa, full of fruit, while looking at it. At last he saw Jambūdvīpa, fully protected and surrounded on all sides by the moat of the sea, jāmbūnada (full of mines of gleaming gold), and very radiant. (23)

English Translation (of the Sanskrit)AI-assisted translation
Between the Himālaya and the Vindhya ranges is [the land] named Ārya[varta], reaching from the eastern to the western sea; thinking "it gives happiness and gives the good the fruit of their deeds", he came to the Himālaya as if drawn by longing. (24)

English Translation (of the Hindi)AI-assisted translation
The middle region between the Himālaya and the Vindhya mountains is famous by the name Āryāvarta; its boundaries are the eastern and western seas. This part of the earth gives the good the fruit of their deeds; drawn by this, he came near the Himālaya. (24)

जो हिमालय और विन्ध्यमालाओं के मध्यभाग में आर्यावर्त नाम से प्रख्यात है, जो पूर्व और पश्चिम दोनों ओर सागरों की लहरों से सदा संश्लिष्ट रहता है, जिसकी सीमा का विभाजन पूर्व पश्चिम सागर किया करते हैं, यह भूभाग कर्मभूमि के नाम से प्रख्यात है, जहाँ सज्जनों को अपने उदात्तादि कर्मानुसार फल प्राप्ति हुआ करती है, इन विचारों से अभिभूत होकर अनायास ही खिंचते हुए हिमवान् पर्वत की उपत्यका में आ पहुँचे। २४
English NotesAI-assisted translation
The middle region between the Himālaya and the Vindhya ranges is famous by the name Āryāvarta. It is always touched by the waves of the seas on both east and west, and the eastern and western seas mark its boundaries. This land is famous by the name Karmabhūmi (land of action), where the good obtain fruit according to their noble deeds. Moved by these thoughts, and drawn without effort, he arrived in the valley of the Himavān mountain. (24)
