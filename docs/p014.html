English NotesAI-assisted translation
Following the goddess Gaṅgā of holy waters, the sage Mṛkaṇḍa saw that this Gaṅgā stream in places flowed forward with very great speed, and in places spread fearsome whirlpools and turned the mass of water into eddies; seeing that river of the gods, Gaṅgā, resounding in places with a murmuring sound, he felt peace of mind and considered his life blessed for ever and ever. (27)

English NotesAI-assisted translation
Going downstream along the bank, four yojanas (that is, 16 kos) from the lovely Gomukh of that river of the gods, the sage Mṛkaṇḍa saw the primal persons Nara and Nārāyaṇa, who roam in the Badarī forest, densely covered with badarī trees. With faith he worshipped them with due rites, and, with great faith and trust, was struck with wonder and overwhelmed. (28)
