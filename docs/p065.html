Verse 9
Shlokamanuensis's hand
कोऽधिपोऽस्य विषयस्य साम्प्रतं कश्च वा मुनिवरोऽत्र जागृतः । यद्दयालवलभाम्यथापि त- त्सम्मितेन सहसैव भूयताम् ॥ ९ ॥
English Translation (of the Sanskrit)AI-assisted translation
"Who is the ruler of this land now? And which excellent sage is awake (eminent) here? [Gaining] whose kindness I may at once come to be at one with him." (9)

सम्प्रति इस देश का राजा कौन है ? अ यहाँ पर कौन मुनिवर प्रभावशाली है ? जिनकी दयाकृपा से मैं भी उनकी सम्मति वाला बनूं ॥ ९ ॥
English Translation (of the Hindi)AI-assisted translation
"Who is the king of this country now? Which excellent sage is influential here? By whose kindness and favour I too may come to share his views." (9)

English NotesAI-assisted translation
"Now let me see who is the lord (king) of this region, and who is the best among the very awake, influential sages of this area, in whose company I may obtain even a particle of his favour and become settled as his associate." (9)
Verse 10
Shlokamanuensis's hand
एवमादि सुबहून्मनोरथाँ श्चेतसा परिसमीक्ष्य निर्वृतः । श्रान्तिमात्मनि पथा निवेदिता- न्निद्रया समुपनेतुमुद्यतः ॥ १० ॥
English Translation (of the Sanskrit)AI-assisted translation
Having surveyed in his mind these and very many other wishes, he was content, and set about removing through sleep the weariness the road had laid on him. (10)
Translationamanuensis's hand
इस प्रकार अनेक मनोरथों को मन से देखकर सुखी हुए। मार्गगमन जनित अपने में जो थकावट थी उसे निद्रा के माध्यम से दूर करने के लिये तत्पर हुए ॥ १० ॥
English Translation (of the Hindi)AI-assisted translation
Having thus looked over many wishes in his mind, he was happy. He got ready to remove through sleep the tiredness in him caused by walking the road. (10)
Notesgrandfather's hand
इस प्रकार के विविध मानस कल्पनाओं एवं मनोरथों का विचार करते हुए मन ही मन अत्यन्त प्रसन्नता का अनुभव करते हुए सुखी हो उठे शान्ति पायी। मार्ग में गाय के साथ निरन्तर चलते रहने के कारण अत्यन्त थकान का अनुभव करते हुए गाढ़ी निद्रा से थकान दूर करने के लिए शान्त सो गये। १०
English NotesAI-assisted translation
Thinking in this way about various imaginings and wishes in his mind, he felt very glad inwardly, became happy and found peace. Feeling very tired from walking constantly with the cow along the road, he fell into a calm sleep to remove his tiredness through deep sleep. (10)
