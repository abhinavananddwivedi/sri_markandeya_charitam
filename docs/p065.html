Translationamanuensis's hand
सम्प्रति इस देश का राजा कौन है ? अ यहाँ पर कौन मुनिवर प्रभावशाली है ? जिनकी दयाकृपा से मैं भी उनकी सम्मति वाला बनूं ॥ ९ ॥
English TranslationAI-assisted translation
"Who is the ruler of this territory at present? And which revered sage here is awakened in spiritual radiance? By obtaining even a small particle of whose compassionate favour might I straightway become harmonious in fellowship with him?"

English NotesAI-assisted translation
"Now I shall ascertain who is the king of this region, and who among the eminent, spiritually awakened ascetics stands preeminent here—so that through proximity to him and receiving even a fraction of his grace, I may establish myself as his noble associate."

Translationamanuensis's hand
इस प्रकार अनेक मनोरथों को मन से देखकर सुखी हुए। मार्गगमन जनित अपने में जो थकावट थी उसे निद्रा के माध्यम से दूर करने के लिये तत्पर हुए ॥ १० ॥
English TranslationAI-assisted translation
Having surveyed in his mind very many such heartfelt aspirations, he felt deeply serene; and to dispel the weariness wrought upon his limbs by the long road, he prepared to welcome sleep.

English NotesAI-assisted translation
Reflecting upon these manifold inner visions and ideals, the sage experienced immense inner joy and tranquility in his heart. Having journeyed continuously alongside his sacred cow across great distances, he felt deep bodily fatigue and serenely yielded to sound sleep to dissolve all tiredness.
