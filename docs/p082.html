English TranslationAI-assisted translation
"I myself stand as the authoritative officiating preceptor (vācaka) for your sacred matrimony, knowing well the deeply joyous and auspicious planetary alignment (lagna). My profound respect goes with you, for you conduct your life in alignment with the righteous path of worldly duties (pravṛtti-mārga)." (Verse 45)

English NotesAI-assisted translation
"Beloved child! Having comprehended the entire astrological timing and destiny of this sacred wedding hour, I assure you that I myself stand as the spiritual officiant and coordinator (saṁyojaka / vaivāhika paṇḍita) of your forthcoming marriage. I hold your upcoming matrimony in highest esteem, because you too have chosen to live righteously in full conformity with the path of sacred domestic duties (pravṛtti-mārga)." (45)
