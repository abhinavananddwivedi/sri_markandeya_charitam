English Translation (of the Sanskrit)AI-assisted translation
"Go quickly, child, to the land of Dardura; all you desire will come to be. Mudgala too will soon come to you, to give his own daughter." (44)

Verse 45
Shlokamanuensis's hand
वाचकोऽस्मि तव दारसंग्रहे वेद्मि लग्नमतिहर्षनिर्भरम् । भावि ते त्वयि ममाप्यथादरो यत्प्रवृत्तिमभिरुह्य वर्तसे ॥ ४५ ॥
Manuscript NoteAI-assisted transcription
वृत्त: रथोद्धता। पाद १ "वाचकोऽस्मि" (पूर्व "वाचकोऽस्म्यहमिह", तीन अक्षर अधिक)। पाद २–४ यथावत्।
Manuscript Note (EN)AI-assisted transcription
Metre: Rathoddhatā. Pāda 1 reads "वाचकोऽस्मि" (the earlier "वाचकोऽस्म्यहमिह" has three syllables too many). Pādas 2–4 are kept as before.
English Translation (of the Sanskrit)AI-assisted translation
"I am the one here to speak [the rites] in your taking of a wife; I know the lagna (auspicious moment), full of great joy. I too have regard for you as you will be, since you go on by taking up [the path of] action." (45)
Translationamanuensis's hand
तुम्हारे विवाह में विवाह वाचक मैं ही हूँ क्योंकि इस विवाह के समस्त लग्न को मैं जानता हूँ। तुम प्रवृत्ति मार्ग के अनुयायी हो अतः तुम्हारे प्रति मेरा भी आदर है॥ ४५॥
English Translation (of the Hindi)AI-assisted translation
"In your marriage I myself am the one who speaks the marriage rite, for I know the whole lagna (auspicious timing) of this marriage. You are a follower of the path of action, so I too have regard for you." (45)

प्रियवत्स! मैं अपने इस काल के लग्न की वेला से सारी बातें समझ कर के तुम्हें बतलाये देता हूँ कि तुम्हारे भावी विवाह के वाचक (संयोजक / वैवाहिक पण्डित) के स्वरूप में मैं स्वतः हूँ मैं तुम्हारे होने वाले विवाह का भी समादर करता हूँ क्योंकि तुम भी प्रवृत्ति मार्ग के अनुरूप आचरण करना चाहते हो। ४५
English NotesAI-assisted translation
"Dear child! Having understood everything from the lagna of this present time, I tell you that I myself am the officiant (the arranger, the wedding paṇḍit) of your coming marriage. I also respect your coming marriage, because you too wish to conduct yourself according to the path of action." (45)
