English NotesAI-assisted translation
"Sage! It seems that, apart from the world of heaven, without the Kāmadhenu, where else could such tasty, sweet milk be had? The dish you fed me by the name of rice: should it be called rice, or is it a very sweet fruit? I have never eaten such excellent food (rice) anywhere." (39)

अहो स्नुषाणां रचना सुचातुरी तुरीयकक्षामतिशाय्य राजते । पृथक् पृथक् षड्रसता रुचिप्रदा विभाव्यते यासु विमलभावना ॥ ४० ॥
Manuscript NoteAI-assisted transcription
वृत्त: वंशस्थ। पाद ४ "विमलभावना" पाण्डुलिपि में ऐसा ही है; छन्द में एक लघु अधिक है; यथावत्।
Manuscript Note (EN)AI-assisted transcription
Metre: Vaṃśastha. Pāda 4, "विमलभावना", is so written in the manuscript and has one light syllable too many; kept as written.
English Translation (of the Sanskrit)AI-assisted translation
"Ah, the skill of your daughters-in-law's cooking shines, surpassing [even] the fourth rank; in it the six flavours, each distinct and pleasing, are perceived, [made] with a pure mind." (40)
Translationamanuensis's hand
वधुओं की भोजन बनाने की चातुरी आश्चर्यकारी है, यह तो चतुर्थ कक्षा को पार कर सुशोभित हो रही है। भिन्न-भिन्न रुचिप्रद छह रसों की विभावित भावना प्रतीत होती है ॥ ४० ॥
English Translation (of the Hindi)AI-assisted translation
"The daughters-in-law's skill in cooking is astonishing; it shines, having passed beyond the fourth class. One can feel in it the distinct, pleasing six flavours brought out." (40)

महर्षिप्रवर! आपके बहुओं की भोजन कला की अत्यन्त समग्र रचना कौशल की क्या बात कही जाय, यह तो प्रथम मध्यमोत्तम रचना कौशल को अतिक्रमण कर उससे भी ऊपर उच्च कोटि की चतुर्थ श्रेणी का स्थान प्राप्त कर रही है। इन बहुओं की भोजन संरचना की अद्भुत कलाभिजता है। जिससे (मधुर, अम्ल, लवण, कटु, कषाय, तिक्त) इन छह रसों की निरन्तर साथ-साथ अनुभूति और स्वाद मिल रहा है, यह रचना कौशल तो अन्यत्र सुदुर्लभ है। ४०
English NotesAI-assisted translation
"Best of great sages! What can be said of the very complete skill of your daughters-in-law in the art of cooking? It goes beyond the first, middle and best levels of skill and reaches the fourth, still higher class. These daughters-in-law have a wonderful mastery of the art of preparing food, by which one constantly feels and tastes these six flavours (sweet, sour, salty, pungent, astringent, bitter) together; such skill is very rare elsewhere." (40)
