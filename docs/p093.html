English NotesAI-assisted translation
At his door Sage Mṛkaṇḍa had a fine caraṇī built (a manger, where cows and other animals are fed and watered) as approved by the science of astrology. There, constantly engaged in the service of cows, he began his daily discipline, thinking that through it his prosperity in this region would become very great in future. (13)

English NotesAI-assisted translation
The excellent Sage Mṛkaṇḍu was a very alert person. Following the rules of the path of worldly life (pravṛtti-mārga), he was constantly mindful of his coming marriage and of the duties it would bring. So he set about gathering all the things that would be needed in future for household work, and collected them. (14)
