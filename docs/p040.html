Verse 15
Shlokamanuensis's hand
ततः परावृत्य निमज्ज्य जाह्नवी- जलेऽमले भुक्ति-विमुक्तिदायिनि । विधाय सन्ध्यां विधिसर्जितप्रभः प्रदोषकाले शिवमर्चयत्ततः ॥ १५ ॥
English Translation (of the Sanskrit)AI-assisted translation
Then, turning back and bathing in the pure water of the Jāhnavī, giver of enjoyment and liberation, and performing the sandhyā, he, radiant by [Vedic] rule, worshipped Śiva at the time of pradoṣa. (15)

शौचादि से निवृत्त हो भुक्ति मुक्ति दायिनी गङ्गा के निर्मल जल में स्नान कर सन्ध्यावन्दनादि कार्य सम्पन्न कर प्रभावशालि महर्षि मृकण्ड ने प्रदोष काल में आशुतोष भगवान् शंकर को प्रसन्न किया ॥ १५ ॥
English Translation (of the Hindi)AI-assisted translation
Having finished cleansing, he bathed in the clear water of the Gaṅgā, giver of enjoyment and liberation, and performed the sandhyā worship and other rites; then the powerful Maharṣi Mṛkaṇḍu pleased Lord Śaṅkara, the easily pleased, at the time of pradoṣa. (15)

English NotesAI-assisted translation
Having thus finished the cleansing rite, he bathed duly in the clear water of the goddess, daughter of Jahnu, giver of liberation and enjoyment, and completed the sandhyā worship and other daily rites; after that the radiant, splendid great sage Mṛkaṇḍu, at the time of pradoṣa, also worshipped Lord Śaṅkara, husband of Bhavānī, sustainer of beings, and fully pleased him. (15)
Verse 16
Shlokamanuensis's hand
भवाम्बुधेरुत्तरणाय जन्मिनाम् भवन्तमेवाऽऽश्रयते ऽखिलो नरः । निरुध्यते जन्मशतैःकृतैरपि विना भवं तस्य कुतः पुनर्भवः ॥ १६ ॥
English Translation (of the Sanskrit)AI-assisted translation
"To cross the ocean of worldly life, every man among the born takes refuge in you alone; [rebirth] is held back [even] by deeds done over hundreds of births; without Bhava (Śiva), how can there be [freedom from] rebirth?" (16)
Translationamanuensis's hand
संसार-सागर से पार पाने के लिए सभी लोग आप के ही आश्रय ग्रहण करते हैं। क्यों कि सैकड़ों जन्मों के किये गये कर्मों से व्यक्ति का पुनर्जन्म बिना भगवान् शिव की आराधना किये कैसे रोका जा सकता है? ॥ १६ ॥
English Translation (of the Hindi)AI-assisted translation
"To cross the ocean of worldly life, all people take refuge in you alone. For how can a person's rebirth, [caused] by deeds done in hundreds of births, be stopped without worshipping Lord Śiva?" (16)
Notesgrandfather's hand
संसार में जन्म धारण करने वाले सभी प्राणी इस अपार भवसागर को पार करने के लिए हे प्रभो! मात्र आपका ही आश्रयण करते हैं, क्योंकि सैकड़ों जन्मों के किए गये कर्मों के द्वारा प्राप्त पुनर्जन्म भगवान् सदाशिव की आराधना किए बिना कैसे रोका जा सकता है? १६
English NotesAI-assisted translation
"Lord! All beings who take birth in the world take refuge in you alone to cross this boundless ocean of worldly life. For how can the rebirth that comes from deeds done in hundreds of births be stopped without worshipping Lord Sadāśiva?" (16)
