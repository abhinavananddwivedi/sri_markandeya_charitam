Verse 28
Shlokamanuensis's hand
तं ततः समुपेत्य सम्मुखं दण्डवत्प्रणिपपात सादरम् । इङ्गितेन परिसान्त्व्य सोऽचिरात् सन्मुनिस्तमपि साध्वयासदत् ॥ २८ ॥
English Translation (of the Sanskrit)AI-assisted translation
Then he went before him and respectfully fell prostrate like a staff; [Parāśara], comforting him by a gesture, soon, as a good sage, duly received him too. (28)

महर्षि मृकण्डु ने उनके पास जाकर उन्हें आदरपूर्वक दण्डवत् प्रणाम किया । इशारे से उन्हें सान्त्वना प्रदान कर सज्जनमुनि पराशर ने भी शीघ्र उनकी पूजा सत्कार की इच्छा की ॥ २८ ॥
English Translation (of the Hindi)AI-assisted translation
Maharṣi Mṛkaṇḍu went to him and bowed respectfully, prostrate like a staff. Comforting him with a gesture, the good sage Parāśara too quickly wished to worship and honour him. (28)

English NotesAI-assisted translation
As soon as he saw Maharṣi Parāśara, the sage Mṛkaṇḍa went near him and respectfully prostrated himself like a staff at his feet; the generous Maharṣi Parāśara at once reassured him with a kind gesture and comforted him at once in a soothing way. (28)
Verse 29
Shlokamanuensis's hand
किञ्च शिष्यसहकारतो मुनिः पाद्यमर्घ्यं मधुपर्क इत्यदम् । सम्मुखेन कतिधोच्चरञ्जवा- त्तेन मृकण्डुरधुना समार्च्यत ॥ २९ ॥
English Translation (of the Sanskrit)AI-assisted translation
And the sage, with his disciples' help, calling out several times in haste, "water for the feet, arghya, madhuparka", now had Mṛkaṇḍu honoured by them. [Partly unclear.] (29)
Translationamanuensis's hand
और शिष्यों के माध्यम से मुनि पराशर जी पाद्य अर्घ्य मधुपर्क यह सब सम्मानपूर्वक कई बार उच्चारण करते हुए सम्प्रति महर्षि मृकण्डु की अर्चना करायी ॥ २९ ॥
English Translation (of the Hindi)AI-assisted translation
And through his disciples, the sage Parāśara, respectfully calling out several times "water for the feet, arghya, madhuparka", now had Maharṣi Mṛkaṇḍu worshipped. (29)
Notesgrandfather's hand
साथ ही अपने प्रिय शिष्य को पाद्य, अर्घ्य, मधुपर्क इत्यादि के लिए शीघ्रता में कई बार उच्चारण करते हुए मुनि मृकण्ड की मधुपर्कादि विविध उपचारों से ससम्मान विधिवत् परिचर्या की । २९
English NotesAI-assisted translation
At the same time, calling out several times in haste to his dear disciple for water for the feet, arghya, madhuparka and the like, he duly and respectfully attended to the sage Mṛkaṇḍa with madhuparka and various other offerings. (29)
