Manuscript NoteAI-assisted transcription
वृत्त: रथोद्धता। पाद २ "सहायकतया" (पूर्व "सहायकस्तथा")। पाद ४ अधिलिखित और अस्पष्ट है; पूर्व पाठ (१४ अक्षर) छन्द में नहीं बैठता, यथावत् रखा है।
Manuscript Note (EN)AI-assisted transcription
Metre: Rathoddhatā. Pāda 2 reads "सहायकतया" (earlier "सहायकस्तथा"). Pāda 4 is overwritten and unclear; the earlier reading (fourteen syllables) does not scan and is kept as it was.
English Translation (of the Sanskrit)AI-assisted translation
"This one, born in the Āṅgirasa gotra, will be your helper at every step; skilled in all work, he will now carry out [the building of] the hermitage, following your wishes." (46)

English NotesAI-assisted translation
"I am giving you, for your support, this distinguished person born in the Āṅgirasa gotra, because he is fully able to give you proper help. This man is skilled in his work and fully hard-working; he will have the hermitage built as you conceive it, just as you wish." (46)
