English NotesAI-assisted translation
After his praise of the goddess, the river of the gods, the sage Mṛkaṇḍu crossed with great difficulty the forest near the mountain ranges, decked with masses of very white snow, very white like autumn, clear like rows of clouds. Gradually reaching the level land, he saw its charming beauty, and his mind filled with joy. (1)
Verse 2
Shlokamanuensis's hand
स सैकते देवनदीतटे मुनि श्चिरं चरत्सामिथुनं रथाङ्गयोः । ददर्श युक्तीकृत मानवन्दनं व्यभावयत्प्रेम तदप्यलौकिकम् ॥ २ ॥
English Translation (of the Sanskrit)AI-assisted translation
On the sandy bank of the river of the gods, the sage saw a pair of cakravāka birds long moving together, bound in union, and he pondered their love, which was itself unearthly. (2)
Translationamanuensis's hand
महात्मा शुकण्ड ने बालुकामय गङ्गातट पर चिर काल से विचरण करते हुए चक्रवाक मिथुन को देखा साथ ही मिथुनीकृत भाव-बन्धन युक्त उनके अलौकिक प्रेम की भावना की ॥ २ ॥
English Translation (of the Hindi)AI-assisted translation
The great soul Mṛkaṇḍu saw on the sandy bank of the Gaṅgā a pair of cakravāka birds that had long been wandering there, and he contemplated their unearthly love, bound in the feeling of being a pair. (2)

भूतल पर पहुँचते ही महात्मा शुकण्ड ने अत्यन्त स्वच्छ धवल बालुकामय भगवती गङ्गा के पवित्र तट पर चक्रवाक मिथुन को देखा, जो निरन्तर बहुत दिनों से अपनी प्रेयसी के साथ प्रेमासक्त हो आत्म विस्मृति की स्थिति में विचरण कर रहा था, को देखा और उनके युगल स्नेह की उत्कटता का अनुभव किया जिससे उनके मन में अलौकिक दिव्य प्रेम की अद्भुत उद्भावना हुई । २
English NotesAI-assisted translation
As soon as he reached the level land, the great soul Mṛkaṇḍu saw, on the holy bank of the goddess Gaṅgā with its very clean white sand, a pair of cakravākas: the male, attached in love to his beloved, had been wandering with her for many days in a state of self-forgetfulness. He felt the intensity of their love as a pair, and a wonderful idea of unearthly, divine love arose in his mind. (2)
