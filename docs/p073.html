Verse 24

एवमादि हठयोगतत्परान् राजयोगगतमानसानथ । स्वान्तरे परतरं समाधिभि- र्ज्योतिरुज्ज्वलतरं व्यलोकयत् ॥ २४ ॥
English Translation (of the Sanskrit)AI-assisted translation
[He saw] these and other [sages] intent on haṭha yoga, and others whose minds were in rāja yoga, beholding within themselves, through samādhi, the highest, very bright light. (24)

Thus he beheld various ascetics deeply devoted to Haṭha-yoga, others whose minds were absorbed in Rāja-yoga, and many beholding the transcendent, supremely effulgent Light within their inner self through states of deep meditative absorption (samādhi).

English Translation (of the Sanskrit)AI-assisted translation
(25) In the middle of the altar, of bright splendour, his body golden in hue, tall in form, with golden hair and a long beard, his eyes closed in meditation, rich in austerity; (26) his forehead adorned with the upright mark (ūrdhvapuṇḍra), with fine tulasī garlands at his ears, high-nosed, [unclear] on his face, bearing the light of a gentle smile; (27) his mind sunk in the dense joy of consciousness, intent on restraining outward activity, worshipping with fitting offerings: Parāśara he recognised before him. English Translation (of the Hindi)AI-assisted translation
Seated in the midst of the sacrificial altar, shining with dazzling radiance, possessing a tall frame of golden complexion, with golden hair and a long flowing beard, his eyes gently closed in meditation, rich in the wealth of penance; His forehead adorned with the auspicious vertical mark (ūrdhvapuṇḍra), his ears graced with delicate strands of sacred tulasī, endowed with a noble aquiline nose, bearing on his face a soft, enchanting smile; His consciousness deeply immersed in the dense nectar of spiritual bliss (cit-sukha), wholly restraint of all outward sensory modifications, and offering divine worship with fitting ceremonial rites—perceiving all these sacred marks, Mṛkaṇḍu recognized before him the venerable Sage Parāśara!

उसके बाद यज्ञवेदी के मध्य में विद्यमान दिव्य प्रभा भासित स्वर्ण की पीताभता को भी अपनी लम्बी सुदीर्घ शरीर व्यूह रचना कान्ति से तिरस्कृत करने वाले पीली कान्ति से संयुक्त केशपाश वाले लम्बी दाढ़ी से सुशोभित प्रत्यक्ष में समाधि के कारण मुद्रित नेत्र वाले उस तपस्वी प्रवर को १. ऊर्ध्वपुण्ड्र (वैष्णव दीक्षा सम्पन्न) से सुशोभित भाल वाले, दोनों कानों में सुन्दर तुलसी की लड़ियाँ धारण किए हुए, समुन्नत नासिका वाले, सुदीर्घ नेत्र कोनों से युक्त, मृदु मधुर मुस्कान युक्त शरीर कान्ति वाले, २. अन्तर्हृदय में परात्तत्व के ध्यानी, अनुभूतियों के कारण सभी सांसारिक बाह्यवृत्तियों के निरोध करने में समर्थ एवं देवाराधन की विविध विधियों द्वारा परब्रह्म की आराधना करते हुए देख यही महर्षि पराशर जी हैं, ऐसा मुनि मृकण्ड ने अपने हृदय में यह विश्वास किया । (२५, २६, २७)
English NotesAI-assisted translation
After this, [he saw] that excellent ascetic in the middle of the sacrificial altar, shining with divine splendour, whose tall body and grace of form outshone even the yellow glow of gold, with hair of yellow lustre, adorned with a long beard, his eyes closed because of samādhi: (1) his forehead adorned with the ūrdhvapuṇḍra (having received Vaiṣṇava initiation), wearing lovely strands of tulasī in both ears, with a high nose and long corners to his eyes, his bodily lustre joined with a soft, sweet smile; (2) meditating within his heart on the highest reality, able, through his experiences, to restrain all outward worldly activity, and worshipping the Supreme Brahman by various methods of worship of the gods. Seeing him, the sage Mṛkaṇḍa was convinced in his heart: "This is Maharṣi Parāśara." (25, 26, 27)
