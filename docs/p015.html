Verse 29
Shlokamanuensis's hand
अदृष्टपूर्वावपि तौ तपस्विना प्रभावितेन प्रतिनन्दितौ मुहुः । सतां हि सम्भाषण-दर्शनादिना मनोगतं लक्ष्यत एव सत्वरम् ॥ २९ ॥

वृत्त: वंशस्थ (चारों पाद शुद्ध)। पूर्व पाठ "प्रमाणिलेन … पुनः / सतो हि … / … मत्परम्" भ्रान्त था।
Manuscript Note (EN)AI-assisted transcription
Metre: Vaṃśastha (all four pādas scan). The earlier reading ("प्रमाणिलेन … पुनः / सतो हि … / … मत्परम्") was wrong.
English Translation (of the Sanskrit)AI-assisted translation
Though they had never been seen before, the two were greeted again and again by the ascetic, who was deeply impressed; for through conversing with, seeing and so on, the good, what lies in the mind is quickly perceived. (29)

English Translation (of the Hindi)AI-assisted translation
Although that ascetic, deeply impressed, had not seen the two before, he greeted them. For through conversing with and seeing the good, the feelings in the mind quickly become apparent. (29)

English NotesAI-assisted translation
Although Mṛkaṇḍu had never seen them before, the very radiant figures of the two impressed him greatly, and he kept greeting them again and again; for through conversing with saints and good people and seeing them, one's own feelings soon show of themselves. (29)

तयोश्च सम्प्रेक्ष्य मुदं स्वदर्शनात् स्तुतिं विधातुं मनसा प्रचक्रमे । स्वतश्च जिज्ञासुतया प्रवृद्धधी- र्मनोऽभिलाषं गदितुं मनो दधे ॥ ३० ॥
Manuscript NoteAI-assisted transcription
वृत्त: वंशस्थ। पाद ३–४ की सन्धि में "र्" (प्रवृद्धधीर्मनो…) पाण्डुलिपि में नहीं लिखा है; यहाँ जोड़ा गया। पाद २ में "मनसा" के स्थान पर "मनसः" भी पढ़ा जा सकता है।
Manuscript Note (EN)AI-assisted transcription
Metre: Vaṃśastha. The sandhi "r" across pādas 3–4 (प्रवृद्धधीर्मनो…) is not written in the manuscript and has been supplied. In pāda 2, "मनसा" could also be read "मनसः".
English Translation (of the Sanskrit)AI-assisted translation
Seeing their joy at the sight of him, he began to praise them in his mind; and of his own accord, his understanding grown through the wish to know, he set his mind on speaking his heart's desire. (30)

उन दोनों आदि पुरुषों (नरनारायण) को अपने दर्शनकाल से ही देखकर मुनि के अन्तःकरण में अतिशय आनन्दानुभूति हुई, उन्होंने उन आदि पुरुषों की समाहितचित्त होकर मन ही मन स्तुति करना प्रारम्भ किया, उनकी अलौकिक उपस्थिति तथा उनकी अनुपम उदाराशयता देखकर मुनि मृकण्डु ने जिज्ञासावश अपनी मनोभिलषित भावनाओं को व्यक्त करने के लिए दृढ़ निश्चय कर लिया। ३०
English NotesAI-assisted translation
Seeing the two primal persons (Nara and Nārāyaṇa) from the very moment he beheld them, the sage felt great joy in his heart. With a collected mind he began inwardly to praise those primal persons; and seeing their unearthly presence and their matchless generosity of heart, the sage Mṛkaṇḍu, out of the wish to know, firmly resolved to express the feelings his mind desired. (30)
