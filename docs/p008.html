Knowing the earth to be fit to live on, Brahmā quickly ordered Manu to dwell on it. Receiving the order, Manu, honouring Brahmā, ruled his creatures on that earth according to dharma. (15)

English NotesAI-assisted translation
At that time, when the Creator had brought out, through the primal boar, the land sunk in the bottomless waters of the dissolution and set it in place, so that the happy life of creatures could go on upon it, he at once ordered Manu to dwell on it. Manu too, honouring his maker the Creator, began to produce, according to dharma, the human creatures he brought forth and to govern them. (15)

Metre: Vaṃśastha. The earlier reading ("…अध्यायता / प्रसूतिदेकान्तरजा … किल यत्परागा") was wrong; pāda 4 reads "भर्तृतत्परा" ("devoted to her husband", confirmed by the Hindi).
English Translation (of the Sanskrit)AI-assisted translation
His son Priyavrata, a teacher among the good, and likewise the younger, Uttānapāda, were born; then Prasūti, born between, slender-waisted; and Devahūti, devoted to her husband. (16)

English Translation (of the Hindi)AI-assisted translation
His elder son was Priyavrata, best among the good; the younger son was named Uttānapāda. After that came Prasūti, and then a daughter named Devahūti, devoted to her husband. (16)

इस प्रकार महात्मा मनु ने अपने मैथुनी सृष्टि के सुचारु रूप से सञ्चालन के लिए प्रथम पत्नी द्वारा सुप्रथम श्रेष्ठ प्रियव्रत नामक ज्येष्ठ पुत्र तथा उत्तानपाद नामक कनिष्ठ पुत्रों को उत्पन्न किया। साथ ही प्रसूति नामक ज्येष्ठ कन्या एवं पतिपरायणा कनीयसी देवहूति को भी जन्म दिया ॥ १६ ॥
English NotesAI-assisted translation
Thus, for the proper running of creation through union, the great soul Manu, through his first wife, produced an excellent eldest son named Priyavrata and a younger son named Uttānapāda. He also begot an elder daughter named Prasūti and a younger one, devoted to her husband, named Devahūti. (16)
