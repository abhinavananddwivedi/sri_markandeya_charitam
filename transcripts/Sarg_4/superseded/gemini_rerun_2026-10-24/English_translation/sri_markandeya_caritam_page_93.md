# Śrī Mārkaṇḍeya Caritam — Canto 4 (चतुर्थः सर्गः)
**Page 93 (पृष्ठ ९३)**

---

### Verse 13 (श्लोक १३)

#### I. Sanskrit Text
```sanskrit
द्वारि चारु चरणिं विरचय्य
गोसुसेवनपरो मुनिरासीत् ।
येन भावि विषयाय विभूतिं
ह्यागमम्यत सुसिद्धितरैव ॥ १३ ॥
```
*(Paleographic / Textual Note: Meter: Rathoddhatā (`ऽ।ऽ ।।। ऽ।ऽ ।ऽ`, 11 syllables per pāda). All four pādas scan at 11 syllables. The grandfather's note clearly reads `चर्या` ("conduct/routine"), describing the sage constructing an auspicious cattle feeding manger (*caraṇī*) at his doorway and engaging in daily bovine service.)*

#### II. Hindi Translation (Amanuensis)
द्वार पर पृथिवी को सुन्दर बनाकर महर्षि मृकण्डु गो-सेवा में संलग्न हो गये। जिस गो-सेवा से उन्होंने भावी विषय की समृद्धि को निकटतर ही माना ॥ १३ ॥

#### III. Grandfather's Commentary / Hindi Notes
मुनि मृकण्ड ने अपने दरवाजे पर ज्योतिष शास्त्रानुमोदित सुन्दर चरणि (चरनी जहाँ गाय आदि पशुओं के खिलाने पिलाने की व्यवस्था होती है) का निर्माण कराया, वहीं पर गोसेवा में नित्य निरत होते हुए अपनी चर्या प्रारम्भ की, जिससे भविष्य में भी इस क्षेत्र में इनकी विभूति अत्यन्त समृद्धियुक्त होगी ऐसा इन्होंने सोचा। १३

#### IV. English Translation: Verse & Literal Meaning
Beautifying the ground at the entrance, Maharṣi Mṛkaṇḍu engaged in the service of cows; through which cow-service he regarded the prosperity of his future life to have drawn nearer. (Verse 13)

#### V. English Translation: Grandfather's Commentary & Context
At his entrance, Sage Mṛkaṇḍu had a handsome manger (*caraṇī*, an arrangement for feeding and watering cattle) constructed according to astrological treatises; constantly devoted to the care of cows right there, he commenced his daily spiritual discipline (*caryā*), thinking that through this his spiritual and temporal glory would flourish abundantly in this realm in the future. (13)

### Verse 14 (श्लोक १४)

#### I. Sanskrit Text
```sanskrit
भाविनेह परिणीति विधौ यत्
सत्सम्भावितमभूत् किल वस्तु ।
गृह्यकर्मनिपुणः स महर्षि-
-रुत्सुको मुनिवरोऽपि हि तत्तत् ॥ १४ ॥
```
*(Paleographic / Textual Note: Meter: Rathoddhatā (11 syllables per pāda). Line 2 reads `सत्सम्भावितमभूत् किल वस्तु` (11 syllables), fully harmonizing the main shlok text with the manuscript notes.)*

#### II. Hindi Translation (Amanuensis)
भावी वैवाहिक कार्य में जो-जो वस्तु आवश्यक है, उसे गृह्यकर्म में चतुर मुनिवर ने भी एकत्रित कर लिया ॥ १४ ॥

#### III. Grandfather's Commentary / Hindi Notes
मुनिवर मृकण्डु अतिशय सचेतन व्यक्ति थे, प्रवृत्ति मार्ग के नियमानुसार अपने होने वाले वैवाहिक कार्य तथा उसके द्वारा सम्पादित होने वाले कार्यों के प्रति भी सतत जागरूक होने के कारण भविष्य में जिन गृहकृत्य के लिए जिन २ वस्तुओं की आवश्यकता थी उन सभी वस्तुओं को एकत्र करने के लिए समुद्यत होकर एक-एक इकट्ठा किया। १४

#### IV. English Translation: Verse & Literal Meaning
Whatever articles were requisite for the forthcoming wedding rites, the noble sage, adept in domestic rituals, collected them all eagerly. (Verse 14)

#### V. English Translation: Grandfather's Commentary & Context
Sage Mṛkaṇḍu was an exceptionally alert individual; following the regulations of the worldly path of action (*pravṛtti-mārga*), he was continuously aware of his impending marriage ceremony and the duties to be performed through it. Consequently, whatever articles were necessary for future household rites, he zealously prepared to assemble and gathered them one by one. (14)
