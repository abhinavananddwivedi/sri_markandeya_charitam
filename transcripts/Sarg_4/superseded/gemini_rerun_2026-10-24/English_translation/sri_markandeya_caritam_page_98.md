# Śrī Mārkaṇḍeya Caritam — Canto 4 (चतुर्थः सर्गः)
**Page 98 (पृष्ठ ९८)**

---

### Verse 23 (श्लोक २३)

#### I. Sanskrit Text
```sanskrit
तेन सार्धमभिगम्य विनीतः
प्रार्थनातिशयमत्र चिकीर्षुः ।
मार्गजानपदवर्धितचेताः
प्रह्वतं मुनिमृकण्डुमुवाच ॥ २३ ॥
```
*(Paleographic / Textual Note: Meter: Rathoddhatā (`ऽ।ऽ ।।। ऽ।ऽ ।ऽ`, 11 syllables per pāda). All four pādas scan at 11 syllables. In line 2, the manuscript clearly has `प्रार्थनातिशयमत्र चिकीर्षुः` (earlier misread as `मन्त्र`). In line 4, `प्रह्लुतं` in the manuscript represents scribal corruption for `प्रह्वस्तं मुनिमृकण्डुमुवाच` (Mudgala bowed humbly and spoke to Sage Mṛkaṇḍu).)*

#### II. Hindi Translation (Amanuensis)
अतिशय प्रार्थना करने की इच्छा वाले उनके साथ जाकर जनपद के मार्ग में प्रफुल्लित चित्त वाले मुद्गल मुनि ने मुनिवर मृकण्डु से कहा ॥ २३ ॥

#### III. Grandfather's Commentary / Hindi Notes
अत्यन्त विनयशील मुनि मुद्गल अपने सहचरों के साथ चलते हुए अपनी कन्या के विवाह के लिए विनीत भाव से अनुनय विनय करने की अभिलाषा से आ रहे थे, उन्हें रास्ते में विविध जनपदों से होते हुए जाना पड़ रहा था सर्वत्र मुनि मृकण्डु की सिद्धियों विभूतियों की कथा सुनते हुए उनके आश्रम में पहुँच कर मुनि मृकण्डु को पाकर उनके प्रति कृतज्ञता ज्ञापन किया। २३

#### IV. English Translation: Verse & Literal Meaning
Approaching in his company, humble and earnest in his desire to make a fervent supplication, his spirit uplifted by what he heard across the realms along his path, he bowed low and addressed Sage Mṛkaṇḍu. (Verse 23)

#### V. English Translation: Grandfather's Commentary & Context
The supremely courteous Sage Mudgala, journeying alongside his companions with the humble aspiration of proposing the marriage of his daughter, had to traverse various realms along the way; hearing everywhere the accounts of Sage Mṛkaṇḍu's spiritual accomplishments and divine splendour, upon arriving at his hermitage and meeting Sage Mṛkaṇḍu, he expressed his deep reverence and gratitude. (23)

### Verse 24 (श्लोक २४)

#### I. Sanskrit Text
```sanskrit
स्नातकं समुपसृत्य भवन्तं
पाणिपीडनमहं तनयायाः ।
कर्तुमागत इहास्मि मुनीन्द्र
स्वीकुरुष्व हि मया क्रियमाणाम् ॥ २४ ॥
```
*(Paleographic / Textual Note: Meter: Rathoddhatā (`ऽ।ऽ ।।। ऽ।ऽ ।ऽ`, 11 syllables per pāda). All four pādas scan at 11 syllables. The manuscript scribal error `कर्तुमागत भवान्हि` (which was grammatically distorted) and corrupt earlier readings like `योजियाऽहमभिनन्दनयायाः` have been resolved. Mudgala is addressing Mṛkaṇḍu, who is a *snātaka* (celibate scholar who has completed studies but not yet married), proposing that Mṛkaṇḍu accept the hand of his daughter in marriage.)*

#### II. Hindi Translation (Amanuensis)
आपको स्नातक सुनकर मैं अपनी कन्या के विवाहार्थ यहाँ आया हूँ। मुनिवर! विवाहार्थ आये हुए मुझे जानकर आप स्वीकृति प्रदान करें ॥ २४ ॥

#### III. Grandfather's Commentary / Hindi Notes
हे मुनिपुङ्गव! मैंने सुना है अभी आप स्नातक (अविवाहित) हैं, मैं अपनी कन्या का विवाह करने की अभिलाषा से आया हुआ हूँ, ऐसा आप समझें, मैं आपसे विवाह करने की स्वीकृति चाहता हूँ, विश्वास है आप अवश्य स्वीकार करेंगे। २४

#### IV. English Translation: Verse & Literal Meaning
"Having approached you, knowing you to be a celibate scholar (*snātaka*), I have come here, O foremost of sages, to perform the marriage (*pāṇipīḍana*) of my daughter; please grant your acceptance to this proposal made by me." (Verse 24)

#### V. English Translation: Grandfather's Commentary & Context
"O exalted Sage! I have heard that you are currently a *snātaka* (an unmarried scholar who has completed Vedic vows); know that I have arrived with the heartfelt desire to perform the marriage of my daughter. I seek your gracious consent for this union, confident that you will surely accept." (24)
