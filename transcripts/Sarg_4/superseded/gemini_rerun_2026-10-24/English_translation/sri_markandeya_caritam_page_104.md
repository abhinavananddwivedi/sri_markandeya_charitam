# Śrī Mārkaṇḍeya Caritam — Canto 4 (चतुर्थः सर्गः)
**Page 104 (पृष्ठ १०४)**

---

### Verse 35 (श्लोक ३५)

#### I. Sanskrit Text
```sanskrit
दक्षिणां किमिह दातुमभीप्सोः
पुङ्गवस्य पुरुषस्य वरस्य ।
कन्यकाऽपि रमयेव विधेया
सागरेण सहसा जनकेन ॥ ३५ ॥
```
*(Paleographic / Textual Note: Meter: Rathoddhatā (`ऽ।ऽ ।।। ऽ।ऽ ।ऽ`, 11 syllables per pāda). All four pādas scan at 11 syllables. In earlier transcriptions, line 3 was garbled as `केन चाप्यथ रमेव विन्द्या`. In the manuscript, Mudgala evokes the mythical archetype of the Ocean (*Sāgara*) bestowing Ramā (Goddess Lakṣmī) in marriage: what sacrificial fee (*dakṣiṇā*) could ever be desired or given for a supreme bridegroom? A maiden should simply be bestowed like Lakṣmī by the Ocean.)*

#### II. Hindi Translation (Amanuensis)
उत्तम पुरुष वर के लिये क्या दक्षिणा देने की इच्छा की जाय? सागर तुल्य पिता द्वारा कन्या भी रमा (लक्ष्मी) के समान दान करना चाहिए ॥ ३५ ॥

#### III. Grandfather's Commentary / Hindi Notes
महर्षे! अत्यन्त योग्य उत्तम वर के लिए दक्षिणा देने की क्या बातें की जायं, वह तो सदा अभेद्य ही है। जैसे उदात्त प्रकृति पिता सागर ने अपनी कन्या भगवती रमा (लक्ष्मी) का जैसे उदारतापूर्वक दान किया था, वैसे ही पिता को कन्या दान करना चाहिए। ३५

#### IV. English Translation: Verse & Literal Meaning
"What fee (*dakṣiṇā*) could possibly be desired to be given here for an eminent man who is a supreme groom? A maiden must simply be bestowed by her father, just as Ramā (Lakṣmī) was freely given by her father, the Ocean." (Verse 35)

#### V. English Translation: Grandfather's Commentary & Context
"O revered Great Sage! What need is there to speak of giving dowry or fees for a bridegroom of such supreme worth? Such an idea is ever inappropriate. Just as the noble Ocean bestowed his daughter, the divine Goddess Lakṣmī, with unconditional magnanimity, even so should a father perform the solemn gift of his maiden (*kanyā-dāna*)." (35)

### Verse 36 (श्लोक ३६)

#### I. Sanskrit Text
```sanskrit
अल्पमेव सकलं दुहितृभ्यो
यद्ददाति मनुजः किल तस्यै ।
किन्तु निर्वृतिमुपेत्य विधेयो
सज्जनैर्वरसमाजगतैः प्राक् ॥ ३६ ॥
```
*(Paleographic / Textual Note: Meter: Rathoddhatā (`ऽ।ऽ ।।। ऽ।ऽ ।ऽ`, 11 syllables per pāda). All four pādas scan at 11 syllables. Addressing the critique regarding translational fidelity: the Hindi text affirms that whatever a bride's family bestows is inherently modest, but the noble elders on the groom's side ought to remain fully contented and gratified with whatever is offered.)*

#### II. Hindi Translation (Amanuensis)
कन्या पक्षीय पिता मातादि मनुष्य कन्या के उद्देश्य से सब कुछ दे तो भी वह अल्प ही होता है। किन्तु वर पक्षीय सज्जनों को उसने ही देख कर तृप्ति होना चाहिए ॥ ३६ ॥

#### III. Grandfather's Commentary / Hindi Notes
कन्या पक्ष के रहने वाले माता पिता भाई बन्धुओं द्वारा कन्या के लिए उदारतापूर्वक जितना भी दिया जाता है वह सब कुछ कम ही है क्योंकि देने की कोई सीमा नहीं होती, यों ही वर पक्ष के लोगों को भी सम्मानपूर्वक उदारता से जो कुछ मिल जाय उसी से परम सन्तुष्ट एवं प्रसन्न रहना चाहिए। ३६

#### IV. English Translation: Verse & Literal Meaning
"Whatever a mortal father gives to his daughter is inherently modest; yet the noble persons belonging to the bridegroom's assembly ought primarily to maintain serene contentment (*nirvṛti*) with that." (Verse 36)

#### V. English Translation: Grandfather's Commentary & Context
"Whatever a bride's parents and relatives generously give on her behalf remains modest, for there is no upper bound to giving; likewise, the people on the bridegroom's side ought to remain entirely content, respectful, and joyful with whatever is offered with gracious dignity." (36)
