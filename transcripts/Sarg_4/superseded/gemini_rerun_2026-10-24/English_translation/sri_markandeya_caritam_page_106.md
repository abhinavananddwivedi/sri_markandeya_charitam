# Śrī Mārkaṇḍeya Caritam — Canto 4 (चतुर्थः सर्गः)
**Page 106 (पृष्ठ १०६)**

---

### Verse 39 (श्लोक ३९)

#### I. Sanskrit Text
```sanskrit
इत्युदीर्य विरते किल तस्मि-
-न्नात्युदारहृदये मुनिवर्ये ।
वाचमाकलयता रुचिरार्थां
शक्तिजेन गदितं स्मितपूर्वम् ॥ ३९ ॥
```
*(Paleographic / Textual Note: Meter: Rathoddhatā (`ऽ।ऽ ।।। ऽ।ऽ ।ऽ`, 11 syllables per pāda). All four pādas scan at 11 syllables. In earlier versions, lines 3–4 were corrupted into 13–14 syllables. In the manuscript, all four pādas scan in regular Rathoddhatā. Describes the transition from Mudgala's speech to Parāśara's reply: when the generous Sage Mudgala fell silent, Parāśara (the son of Śakti), weighing the graceful import of his words, responded with a gentle smile.)*

#### II. Hindi Translation (Amanuensis)
मुनिश्रेष्ठ उदारचेता मुद्गल के ऐसे कह कर मौन हो जाने पर सुन्दर अर्थ वाले वाणी का आकलन करता हुआ शक्तिपुत्र महर्षि पराशर मुस्कुराते हुए बोल पड़े ॥ ३९ ॥

#### III. Grandfather's Commentary / Hindi Notes
अत्यन्त उदार ऋषिप्रवर मुद्गल के ऐसा कहने के बाद शान्त हो जाने पर अत्यन्त सुरुचिपूर्ण अतिशय उदार मन्द-मन्द मुस्कुराहट के साथ महर्षि शक्तिपुत्र पराशर जी ने यों कहना शुरू किया। ३९

#### IV. English Translation: Verse & Literal Meaning
When that eminent sage of exceedingly magnanimous heart had spoken thus and fallen silent, the son of Śakti (Sage Parāśara), discerning his speech of delightful meaning, spoke preceded by a smile. (Verse 39)

#### V. English Translation: Grandfather's Commentary & Context
After the deeply generous foremost sage Mudgala had finished speaking and become silent, the revered Sage Parāśara, son of Śakti, with an exceedingly gracious and gentle smile, began his reply. (39)

### Verse 40 (श्लोक ४०)

#### I. Sanskrit Text
```sanskrit
स्वीकृतं निजमनोभवमन्त्रं
यद्भवद्भिरुदितं सुविचार्या ।
यौतुके विवदतां मनुजानां
वेद्मि हन्त वरविक्रयमेव ॥ ४० ॥
```
*(Paleographic / Textual Note: Meter: Rathoddhatā (`ऽ।ऽ ।।। ऽ।ऽ ।ऽ`, 11 syllables per pāda). All four pādas scan at 11 syllables. Resolves the major textual mystery cited in earlier critiques regarding `वाहि तन्न वरोऽस्तु सुभवः` and `वञ्चिकूप`: the manuscript clearly and beautifully reads `वेद्मि हन्त वरविक्रयमेव` (*vedmi hanta vara-vikrayam eva* = "Alas, I consider it nothing but the commercial sale of the bridegroom"). Parāśara warmly accepts Mudgala's proposal without dowry, declaring that bickering over dowry is tantamount to selling the groom.)*

#### II. Hindi Translation (Amanuensis)
आपलोगों ने निश्चय कर जो कुछ कहा उसे मनोभाव को मैंने स्वीकार किया। दहेज के लिए विवाद करने वाले मनोभाव तो मुझे जान पड़ता है यह विवाह नहीं अपितु वर को बेचना ही है ॥ ४० ॥

#### III. Grandfather's Commentary / Hindi Notes
ऋषिश्रेष्ठ! आपने बड़ी सावधानीपूर्वक अपने मन में जो विचार स्थिर किया है वैवाहिक कार्य सम्पादन के सम्बन्ध में उसे मैंने सहर्ष स्वीकार किया, क्योंकि यौतुक (दहेज) के लिए जो लोग परस्पर कम बेश आदि की चर्चा कर विवाद (बहस) करते हैं, वह तो मुझे वरविक्रय सा ही लगता है। ४०

#### IV. English Translation: Verse & Literal Meaning
"I have readily accepted the heartfelt resolve that you have spoken after mature deliberation; for among mortals who wrangle over dowry (*yautuka*), alas, I consider that to be nothing but the sale of the groom (*vara-vikraya*)." (Verse 40)

#### V. English Translation: Grandfather's Commentary & Context
"O foremost of sages! Whatever thoughtful resolution you have formed in your heart concerning the performance of this marriage, I have gladly accepted it. For when people dispute and bargain over dowry (*yautuka*), haggling about more or less, it seems to me like nothing other than the commercial selling of the bridegroom." (40)
