# Śrī Mārkaṇḍeya Caritam — Canto 4 (चतुर्थः सर्गः)
**Page 88 (पृष्ठ ८८)**

---

### Verse 3 (श्लोक ३)

#### I. Sanskrit Text
```sanskrit
एत्य पूर्वकृतवास वटं सः
स्वासनादि विदधे रुचिरं यत् ।
यत्तु नोद्विजत एककतोऽपि
किं पुनस् सहित आङ्गिरसेन ॥ ३ ॥
```
*(Paleographic / Textual Note: Meter: Rathoddhatā (`ऽ।ऽ ।।। ऽ।ऽ ।ऽ`, 11 syllables per pāda). All four pādas scan at 11 syllables. Line 4 visibly reads `किं पुनस् सहित आङ्गिरसेन` (with Sage Aṅgiras), completely rectifying the earlier corrupted reading.)*

#### II. Hindi Translation (Amanuensis)
अपने पूर्वकृत निवासस्थल के बरगद के पास जाकर उन्होंने अपने सुन्दर आसनादि की रचना की। वे अकेले होने पर भी उद्विग्न नहीं हुए अब आङ्गिरस के साथ हैं तो कहना ही क्या? ॥ ३ ॥

#### III. Grandfather's Commentary / Hindi Notes
वहाँ से चल मुनि मृकण्ड ने अपने पुराने निवास उस वटवृक्ष के पास आकर पूर्ववत् अपने सुन्दर सुखद अत्यन्त मनोरम आसनादि की व्यवस्था की, इसके पूर्व जब वे वहाँ अकेले रहा करते थे तब उन्हें कोई उद्वेग चिन्ता आदि नहीं रही तो इस समय वे अपने एक और सहयोगी आङ्गिरस के साथ जो थे। ३

#### IV. English Translation: Verse & Literal Meaning
Approaching the banyan tree of his former dwelling, he prepared his pleasant seat and surroundings; when he was not agitated even while alone, what need be said now that he was accompanied by Aṅgiras? (Verse 3)

#### V. English Translation: Grandfather's Commentary & Context
Departing from there, Sage Mṛkaṇḍu arrived at his old abode near that banyan tree and arranged his beautiful, delightful, and charming seat as before. When he used to reside there alone formerly, he felt no agitation or anxiety whatsoever; so at this moment, what to speak, when he was accompanied by yet another associate, Aṅgiras! (3)

### Verse 4 (श्लोक ४)

#### I. Sanskrit Text
```sanskrit
दर्दरक्षितिमुपेत्य मृकण्डु-
र्गाङ्गवारिपरिपूतदिगन्ताम् ।
सोऽन्वमन्यत विधित्वमनुकूलं
वासवेश्म विधये व्यतनोद्यत् ॥ ४ ॥
```
*(Paleographic / Textual Note: Meter: Rathoddhatā (11 syllables per pāda). Line 2 reads `र्गाङ्गवारिपरिपूतदिगन्ताम्` ("its horizons purified by the waters of the Gaṅgā"), directly confirming the amanuensis Hindi.)*

#### II. Hindi Translation (Amanuensis)
महर्षि मृकण्डु ने गङ्गाजल से पावन दिग्दिगन्तर वाली दर्दरभूमि में आकर अनुकूल विधि को स्वीकार किया और निवास कुटि के लिए प्रयास किया ॥ ४ ॥

#### III. Grandfather's Commentary / Hindi Notes
वहाँ से चलकर मुनि मृकण्ड भगवती गङ्गा की अविरल धारा से चारों ओर से घिरी हुई (दिगन्त-व्यापिनी भगवती भागीरथी से परिवेष्टित) उस अनुपम पवित्र दर्दर भूमि को देखकर उन्होंने अपने विधि (दैव) को अत्यन्त अनुकूल माना और वहाँ अपने निवास करने के लिए उचित विधि (सहकार) के लिए भी प्रयत्न करना शुरू किया। ४

#### IV. English Translation: Verse & Literal Meaning
Reaching the Dardara land whose quarters are sanctified by the waters of the Gaṅgā, Maharṣi Mṛkaṇḍu accepted favorable providence and made efforts toward his dwelling cottage. (Verse 4)

#### V. English Translation: Grandfather's Commentary & Context
Proceeding from there and beholding that matchless, holy Dardara land encircled on all sides by the unbroken stream of Mother Gaṅgā (encompassed by Mother Bhāgīrathī extending across the horizons), Sage Mṛkaṇḍu considered his destiny (providence) to be exceedingly favorable and commenced endeavors for proper arrangements (cooperation) to establish his residence there. (4)
