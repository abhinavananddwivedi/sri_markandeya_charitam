# Śrī Mārkaṇḍeya Caritam — Canto 4 (चतुर्थः सर्गः)
**Page 89 (पृष्ठ ८९)**

---

### Verse 5 (श्लोक ५)

#### I. Sanskrit Text
```sanskrit
बाल्यतश्च ततपोऽद्भुतशक्तिः
सप्तभौम भवनं खलु कर्तुम् ।
ईश्वरोऽपि न तथाकृत लोक-
शिक्षणाय महतामनुवृत्तिः ॥ ५ ॥
```
*(Paleographic / Textual Note: Meter: Rathoddhatā (`ऽ।ऽ ।।। ऽ।ऽ ।ऽ`, 11 syllables per pāda). All four pādas scan at exactly 11 syllables. At the top of page 89, an aborted draft verse on thatch and cord construction is struck through with an X in the manuscript. Verse 5 speaks of constructing a seven-storied dwelling (*saptabhauma bhavanam*) through austere power as an exemplary model for world-instruction (*loka-śikṣaṇāya*).)*

#### II. Hindi Translation (Amanuensis)
महर्षि मृकण्डु ने सप्तमतला भवन निर्माणार्थ बाल्यकाल से ही तपश्शक्ति अर्जित की थी। ईश्वर भी उस प्रकार लोक शिक्षणार्थ परजनों का अनुवर्तन नहीं किया है ॥ ५ ॥

#### III. Grandfather's Commentary / Hindi Notes
मुनि मृकण्ड ने बाल्यकाल से तपस्या की अधिकता से शक्ति सम्पन्नता प्राप्त कर ली थी, उन्होंने सात मंजिला भवन निर्माण करने को सोचा, क्योंकि ईश्वर ने भी लोक शिक्षण के लिए अत्यन्त विशाल सृष्टि की रचना की है। ५

#### IV. English Translation: Verse & Literal Meaning
Maharṣi Mṛkaṇḍu had accumulated ascetic power since his childhood for constructing a seven-storied dwelling; even God did not follow others in that manner for the instruction of the world. (Verse 5)

#### V. English Translation: Grandfather's Commentary & Context
From childhood, Sage Mṛkaṇḍu had attained fullness of power through intense austerity; he conceived the idea of constructing a seven-storied residence, just as God created this immensely vast cosmos for the instruction and enlightenment of the world. (5)

### Verse 6 (श्लोक ६)

#### I. Sanskrit Text
```sanskrit
आजहार सकलं जनताभ्यो
वास्तुवस्तु स मुनिः ससुहायः ।
दैवशक्त्यनुरुद्धश्चिकीर्षुः
सावधानमनसा सुनिदध्यौ ॥ ६ ॥
```
*(Paleographic / Textual Note: Meter: Rathoddhatā (11 syllables per pāda). The garbled reading `निचोकुर्-` of the earlier export is resolved by the clear manuscript text `चिकीर्षुः` ("desiring to perform") and `सुनिदध्यौ` ("meditated deeply").)*

#### II. Hindi Translation (Amanuensis)
सहायक के साथ मुनिवर मृकण्डु ने जनता से समस्त वास्तु सामग्री को ले आया। दैवशक्ति को रोक कर करने वाले उन्होंने सावधान मन से ध्यान लगाया ॥ ६ ॥

#### III. Grandfather's Commentary / Hindi Notes
यों मुनि मृकण्ड ने अपने सहयोगी ऋषि आङ्गिरस के साथ वहाँ की स्थानीय जनता से सम्पूर्ण गृहनिर्माण के लिए वास्तु विधि पूर्ण करने की सारी वस्तुयें जुटायी और अत्यन्त सावधान मन से दैवी शक्ति का संस्मरण करते हुए अभिनव वृहत्तर भवन निर्माण के लिए सावधान मन से अच्छी तरह विचार किया। ६

#### IV. English Translation: Verse & Literal Meaning
Along with his assistant, the noble sage Mṛkaṇḍu gathered all architectural building materials from the populace; desiring to act while restraining divine power, he meditated with an attentive mind. (Verse 6)

#### V. English Translation: Grandfather's Commentary & Context
Thus Sage Mṛkaṇḍu, along with his associate Sage Aṅgiras, collected from the local populace all the materials necessary to fulfill the architectural (vāstu) rites for building the entire home; and deeply recollecting the divine power with an exceedingly attentive mind, he deliberated thoroughly upon constructing the grand, novel dwelling. (6)
