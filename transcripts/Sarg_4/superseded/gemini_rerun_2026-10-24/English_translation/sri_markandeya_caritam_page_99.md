# Śrī Mārkaṇḍeya Caritam — Canto 4 (चतुर्थः सर्गः)
**Page 99 (पृष्ठ ९९)**

---

### Verse 25 (श्लोक २५)

#### I. Sanskrit Text
```sanskrit
इत्युदीरितवति प्रणयेन
भार्गवः समुचितं समुवाच ।
शक्तिपुत्रमुनिना न विनाऽहं
नोत्सहे परवशः खलु वक्तुम् ॥ २५ ॥
```
*(Paleographic / Textual Note: Meter: Rathoddhatā (`ऽ।ऽ ।।। ऽ।ऽ ।ऽ`, 11 syllables per pāda). All four pādas scan at 11 syllables. In line 4, the manuscript's scribal reading `भूत्सहे` is an error for `नोत्सहे` (I cannot dare / am unable to venture). In earlier Hindi versions, the name was corrupted as `मण्डल`, here correctly restored as Sage Mudgala. Mṛkaṇḍu respectfully clarifies that he is under the guardianship of Sage Parāśara (the son of Śakti) and cannot consent to marriage without his elder's instruction.)*

#### II. Hindi Translation (Amanuensis)
प्रेमपूर्वक मुद्गल के कहने पर भृगुवंशीय मृकण्डु ने उचित कहा—मैं शक्तिपुत्र पराशर से बिना सम्मति लिए विवाह करने में समर्थ नहीं हूँ, ऐसा मुझे परवश होकर कहना पड़ रहा है ॥ २५ ॥

#### III. Grandfather's Commentary / Hindi Notes
सस्नेह ऐसा उनके कहने पर मुनि मृकण्डु ने अत्यन्त उदारतापूर्वक यह समुचित बात कही कि हे मुने! मेरे अभिभावक शक्तिपुत्र महर्षि पराशर जी ही हैं, मैं बिना उनके निर्देश के विवाह नहीं कर सकता (या कह सकता) हूँ यह मेरी विवशता है। अतः आप क्षमा करें। २५

#### IV. English Translation: Verse & Literal Meaning
When Mudgala had spoken thus with affectionate warmth, the descendant of Bhṛgu (Mṛkaṇḍu) gave an appropriate reply: "Without the son of Śakti (Sage Parāśara), I dare not speak in this matter, being dependent on his guidance." (Verse 25)

#### V. English Translation: Grandfather's Commentary & Context
Upon being addressed so affectionately by Sage Mudgala, Sage Mṛkaṇḍu replied with great magnanimity and propriety: "O revered sage! My guardian is none other than the venerable Sage Parāśara, son of Śakti; I cannot agree to marriage without his counsel. Such is my bounden constraint; therefore, please forgive me." (25)

### Verse 26 (श्लोक २६)

#### I. Sanskrit Text
```sanskrit
प्रेष्यजातकृतसत्कृतिपूर्वं
स्तान् निवेद्य रुचिरे शरणेऽऽशु ।
भोजनादिभिरभीष्टविधाभिः
स्वागतं परिचचार सुखेन ॥ २६ ॥
```
*(Paleographic / Textual Note: Meter: Rathoddhatā (`ऽ।ऽ ।।। ऽ।ऽ ।ऽ`, 11 syllables per pāda). All four pādas scan at 11 syllables. Describes the hospitable reception: first honoured by the attendants and disciples (*preṣya*), ushered into handsome lodgings (*rucire śaraṇe*), and warmly entertained with choices of delightful foods.)*

#### II. Hindi Translation (Amanuensis)
पहिले प्रेष्य (परिचारक) द्वारा सत्कृत उन्हें सुन्दर सदन में प्रवेश कराकर अभीष्ट भोजनादि से सानन्द उनका स्वागत किया गया ॥ २६ ॥

#### III. Grandfather's Commentary / Hindi Notes
अपने प्रिय शिष्यों द्वारा समुचित सत्कारपूर्वक उन्हें उत्तम आवास में टिकाया गया, वहाँ उनके उपभोग में भोजन जलपान की व्यवस्था करायी गई, इस प्रकार आये हुए अतिथि का अत्यन्त प्रहर्षपूर्वक उनकी विविध विधियों से समुचित सत्कार किया गया। २६

#### IV. English Translation: Verse & Literal Meaning
Having first had them respectfully attended to by the company of disciples, he promptly ushered them into a charming dwelling, and delightfully extended hospitality with desired preparations of food and drink. (Verse 26)

#### V. English Translation: Grandfather's Commentary & Context
Through his beloved disciples, they were received with full courtesy and accommodated in a comfortable guest cottage; elaborate arrangements were made for their meals and refreshments. In this manner, with immense joy and through diverse traditional customs, the arriving guests were given a most respectful welcome. (26)
