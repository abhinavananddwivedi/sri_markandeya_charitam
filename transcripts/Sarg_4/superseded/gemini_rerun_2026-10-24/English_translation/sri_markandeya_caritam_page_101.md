# Śrī Mārkaṇḍeya Caritam — Canto 4 (चतुर्थः सर्गः)
**Page 101 (पृष्ठ १०१)**

---

### Verse 29 (श्लोक २९)

#### I. Sanskrit Text
```sanskrit
आगते मुनिवरे ह्यपरेद्युः
सर्वे एव मुनयः समागताः ।
सोऽपि तान् समनुगृह्य निर्विक-
-ल्पासनं रुचिरमाशु सिषेवे ॥ २९ ॥
```
*(Paleographic / Textual Note: Meter: Rathoddhatā (`ऽ।ऽ ।।। ऽ।ऽ ।ऽ`, 11 syllables per pāda). All four pādas scan at 11 syllables. In earlier critiques, the finite verb was queried; the manuscript clearly provides the verb `सिषेवे` in line 4 (*siṣeve* = he occupied / resorted to), showing that after hospitably seating all the sages, Mṛkaṇḍu himself took a serene seat.)*

#### II. Hindi Translation (Amanuensis)
दूसरे दिन महर्षि पराशर के आ जाने पर सभी मुनिगण भी उपस्थित हुए। महर्षि मृकण्डु ने पवित्र आसनादि प्रदान कर उन सभी का सत्कार किया ॥ २९ ॥

#### III. Grandfather's Commentary / Hindi Notes
दूसरे दिन पुण्यश्लोक महर्षि पराशर जी के आ जाने पर उन्हें सुनकर सभी मुनिगण वहाँ उपस्थित हो गये, उस समय मुनि मृकण्डु ने सबको समुचित आसन बिछा कर उनका समुचित सत्कार किया तथा स्वयं भी एक आसन पर जाकर विराजमान हो गये। २९

#### IV. English Translation: Verse & Literal Meaning
On the following day, when the foremost sage (Parāśara) arrived, all the sages assembled together. He (Mṛkaṇḍu) also, having received them hospitably, promptly took a handsome and undisturbed seat. (Verse 29)

#### V. English Translation: Grandfather's Commentary & Context
On the next day, upon hearing of the arrival of the auspicious and revered Sage Parāśara, all the sages assembled there; at that time, Sage Mṛkaṇḍu extended proper hospitality to everyone by arranging suitable seats, and himself took a seat with dignified composure. (29)

### Verse 30 (श्लोक ३०)

#### I. Sanskrit Text
```sanskrit
प्रस्तुते स्वविषये समितौ प्राक्
मुद्गलो मुनिरुवाच वरेण्यः ।
भूतभावि सकलज्ञ! मया किं
कथ्यतामिति न मे प्रतिभाति ॥ ३० ॥
```
*(Paleographic / Textual Note: Meter: Rathoddhatā (`ऽ।ऽ ।।। ऽ।ऽ ।ऽ`, 11 syllables per pāda). All four pādas scan at 11 syllables. In earlier versions, `प्राह` and `उवाच` were duplicated resulting in 13 syllables. The manuscript reading is clean Rathoddhatā. Mudgala opens the assembly addressed to Parāśara, expressing humility before one who knows past, present, and future.)*

#### II. Hindi Translation (Amanuensis)
प्रथम सभा में विवाह का विषय प्रस्तुत किये जाने पर वरेण्य मुद्गल मुनि ने कहा—भूत-भविष्यत्-वर्तमान त्रिकालज्ञों के समक्ष मैं कुछ कहूँ, यह मुझे उचित प्रतीत नहीं होता ॥ ३० ॥

#### III. Grandfather's Commentary / Hindi Notes
महर्षि पराशर के आने के बाद सभी महर्षियों की एक सभा बैठी जिसमें महर्षि मुद्गल के कन्या परिणय की चर्चा प्रारम्भ हुई उस समय मुनिश्रेष्ठ मुद्गल ऋषि ने कहा, भगवन्! आप भूत भविष्य वर्तमान तीनों कालों के सर्वज्ञ हैं अतः मैं आपके समक्ष क्या कहूँ स्वयं नहीं समझ पाता। ३०

#### IV. English Translation: Verse & Literal Meaning
When the subject concerning him was initially introduced in the assembly, the eminent sage Mudgala spoke: "O omniscient knower of all past and future! What can possibly be said by me? This does not even occur to my mind." (Verse 30)

#### V. English Translation: Grandfather's Commentary & Context
Following Sage Parāśara's arrival, an assembly of all sages convened wherein the topic of the marriage of Sage Mudgala's daughter was broached. At that moment, the foremost sage Mudgala submitted: "O Lord! You are omniscient regarding the three divisions of time—past, present, and future; therefore, what should I state before you? I myself cannot comprehend." (30)
