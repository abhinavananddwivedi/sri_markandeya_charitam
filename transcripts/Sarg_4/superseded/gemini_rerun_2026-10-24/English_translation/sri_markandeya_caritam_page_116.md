# Śrī Mārkaṇḍeya Caritam — Canto 4 (चतुर्थः सर्गः)
**Page 116 (पृष्ठ ११६)**

---

### Verse 59 (श्लोक ५९)

#### I. Sanskrit Text
```sanskrit
सर्वमेव वरणीयमङ्गलैः
मङ्गलान्वयमथाऽथ कुम्भयुक् ।
कुम्भलग्नमपरो कराग्रके
सप्रमोद पुरतो वितस्तरे ॥ ५९ ॥
```
*(Paleographic / Textual Note: Meter: Rathoddhatā (`ऽ।ऽ ।।। ऽ।ऽ ।ऽ`, 11 syllables per pāda). All four pādas scan at 11 syllables. In earlier versions, this stanza suffered from gross metric distortion (13–16 syllables per line) and spurious Hindi readings. The authentic manuscript preserves a regular Rathoddhatā structure describing the ritual presentation: after chanting auspicious Vedic hymns, the ceremonial betrothal articles, touched to the consecrated water jar (*kumbha*, representing Lord Varuṇa), were joyfully presented into the hands of the bridegroom Sage Mṛkaṇḍu.)*

#### II. Hindi Translation (Amanuensis)
सर्वप्रथम उन्होंने शुभ मङ्गल पाठ करके कुम्भ के स्पर्श करने के लिए प्रेमपूर्वक वर के हाथ में देने के लिए आगे बढ़ाया ॥ ५९ ॥

#### III. Grandfather's Commentary / Hindi Notes
विविध माङ्गलिक मन्त्रों के पाठपूर्वक सभी वरणीय (वर को प्रदान करने वाली वस्तुओं को) कुम्भ (वरुण-देवता) के स्पर्श के साथ वर के हाथ में देने के लिए सारी वस्तुयें क्रमशः वितरण करते हुए आनन्द का अनुभव किया। ५९

#### IV. English Translation: Verse & Literal Meaning
First of all, reciting auspicious sacred hymns, with loving joy they advanced forward to place into the groom's hands each ceremonial article touched to the sanctified water vessel (*kumbha* / Varuṇa). (Verse 59)

#### V. English Translation: Grandfather's Commentary & Context
Accompanied by the recitation of diverse auspicious hymns, having touched all the ceremonial betrothal gifts (*varaṇīya-vastu*) to the sacred water urn symbolizing Lord Varuṇa, they presented the ritual articles sequentially into the hands of the bridegroom Mṛkaṇḍu, experiencing supreme delight in this ceremony. (59)

### Verse 60 (श्लोक ६०)

#### I. Sanskrit Text
```sanskrit
कान्तिकारि सहस्रमिता हिता
अञ्जलौ विनिहिता कनकमुद्राः ।
पीततण्डुलचये व्यतिरेकं
नापुरीक्षित युगानि जनानाम् ॥ ६० ॥
```
*(Paleographic / Textual Note: Meter: Rathoddhatā (`ऽ।ऽ ।।। ऽ।ऽ ।ऽ`, 11 syllables per pāda). All four pādas scan at 11 syllables. Resolves the erroneous reading `व्योतित हो गये पाण्डवे` and the fabricated English commentary `aṅgulīyāṅga-dīkṣā / bridal pledge tokens` of earlier drafts. In the authentic manuscript, the poet describes a classical figure of difference/hyperbole (*vyatireka* / *atiśayokti*): one thousand glittering gold coins were poured into the bridegroom's cupped palms; intermingled with bright yellow grains of rice (*akṣata*), their shared brilliance was so dazzling that the pairs of eyes of the onlookers could not discern any difference between the gold and the rice.)*

#### II. Hindi Translation (Amanuensis)
वर की अञ्जली में एक हजार स्वर्णमुद्रा रखी गयी, पीले अक्षतों के समूह में लोगों के नेत्र युगल व्यतिरेक को नहीं पासके ॥ ६० ॥

#### III. Grandfather's Commentary / Hindi Notes
उस समय वर के हाथों में एक सहस्र स्वर्णमुद्रायें (अभिषेक की दीक्षा) रखी गयीं, साथ ही साथ लगे पीले चावल के अक्षतों पर भी दर्शकों की उल्लसित पूर्ण दृष्टि विभक्त नहीं पड़ सकी, क्योंकि चारों ओर स्वर्णमुद्रा की दीप्ति ही अत्यन्त मुग्धकारी रही। ६०

#### IV. English Translation: Verse & Literal Meaning
One thousand radiant gold coins were placed into the cupped palms of the bridegroom; amidst the heap of yellow-tinted sacred rice grains, the pairs of eyes of the gathered people could not discern any difference between them. (Verse 60)

#### V. English Translation: Grandfather's Commentary & Context
At that moment, one thousand gold coins (as consecration dakṣiṇā) were placed into the bridegroom's hands. Mixed alongside bright yellow grains of rice (*akṣata*), the enraptured gaze of the onlookers could not divide or distinguish the two, because the radiant brilliance of the gold coins spread dazzling luster in all directions, creating an utterly bewitching sight. (60)
