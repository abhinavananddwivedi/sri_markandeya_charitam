# Śrī Mārkaṇḍeya Caritam — Canto 4 (चतुर्थः सर्गः)
**Page 100 (पृष्ठ १००)**

---

### Verse 27 (श्लोक २७)

#### I. Sanskrit Text
```sanskrit
आगतेषु वरितुं स्वमग्रतः
पूर्णजल्पशुभमन्त्रमभ्यैषम् ।
आजुहाव कलकीर्तिनिर्मुखं
तं पाराशरमभीष्टसिद्धये ॥ २७ ॥
```
*(Paleographic / Textual Note: Meter: Rathoddhatā (`ऽ।ऽ ।।। ऽ।ऽ ।ऽ`, 11 syllables per pāda). All four pādas scan at 11 syllables. In earlier drafts, line 1 was corrupted to 14 syllables and the notes were garbled. The manuscript records that when the party arrived to seek him in marriage, Sage Mṛkaṇḍu respectfully invited his elder guardian, the venerable Sage Parāśara, for the accomplishment of this auspicious proposal.)*

#### II. Hindi Translation (Amanuensis)
अपने विवाह के लिये लोगों के आने पर शीघ्रतापूर्वक महर्षि मृकण्डु बड़े भाई के गुणानुरूप कलकीर्ति नियन्त्रित अपने परम अभीष्ट पराशर मुनि को अभीष्ट कार्य की सिद्धि के लिए बुलवाये ॥ २७ ॥

#### III. Grandfather's Commentary / Hindi Notes
मुनि मृकण्डु ने अपने विवाह के लिए आने वाले लोगों के कारण अपने से बड़े शक्ति (बड़े भाई पितृतुल्य आदि के रूप में अपने अभिभावक स्वरूप) महर्षि पराशर जी को जिन की यशस्विता की आधारता लोक सम्मति सबकी फैली हुई थी, उन्हें सादर सस्नेह अपने वैवाहिक कार्य सिद्धि के लिए बुलवाया। २७

#### IV. English Translation: Verse & Literal Meaning
When guests arrived beforehand desiring to choose him for marriage, having exchanged auspicious and complete preliminary words, he summoned the celebrated Sage Parāśara for the fulfillment of the desired undertaking. (Verse 27)

#### V. English Translation: Grandfather's Commentary & Context
Owing to the arrival of delegates proposing marriage, Sage Mṛkaṇḍu respectfully and affectionately summoned the revered Sage Parāśara—his elder and fatherly guardian, whose renowned glory was universally recognized by all society—so that the matrimonial matters might be rightly accomplished. (27)

### Verse 28 (श्लोक २८)

#### I. Sanskrit Text
```sanskrit
गौरवादथ क्रिया समुदारं
कीर्तितञ्च न जगाद स किञ्चित् ।
न्यस्तवानखिलमभ्युपचारं
तं पराशरभरे कलकीर्तिः ॥ २८ ॥
```
*(Paleographic / Textual Note: Meter: Rathoddhatā (`ऽ।ऽ ।।। ऽ।ऽ ।ऽ`, 11 syllables per pāda). All four pādas scan at 11 syllables. In earlier transcriptions, `स पाराशरभरो` was garbled, and the commentary was misunderstood. Mṛkaṇḍu, out of modesty and reverence for his preceptor/elder, said nothing himself regarding the marriage, but entrusted the entire ceremonial responsibility onto the shoulders of Parāśara (*nyastavān akhilam abhyupacāraṃ taṃ parāśarabhare*).)*

#### II. Hindi Translation (Amanuensis)
गौरव के कारण उदार रूप से प्रशंसित शोभनकीर्तिशाली महर्षि मृकण्डु लज्जावश कुछ नहीं बोले, पराशर के लिए समस्त सत्कार की सामग्री एकत्रित कर लिये ॥ २८ ॥

#### III. Grandfather's Commentary / Hindi Notes
मुनि मृकण्डु ने अपनी उदारशमतो तथा संकोच वश अपनी प्रतिष्ठा के कारण भी किसी से कुछ कहा नहीं। अत्यन्त विनम्र स्वभाव यथार्थ सत्ता वाले मृकण्डु महर्षि ने अपने वैवाहिक समस्त कार्य भार का पूर्ण दायित्व महर्षि पराशर के ऊपर ही सम्पादित करने का निश्चय कर दिया। २८

#### IV. English Translation: Verse & Literal Meaning
Out of reverence and dignity, he did not utter a single word himself regarding the noble proposal; of sweet renown, he entrusted all ceremonial obligations entirely upon the care and charge of Parāśara. (Verse 28)

#### V. English Translation: Grandfather's Commentary & Context
Sage Mṛkaṇḍu, restrained by natural modesty and a sense of solemn propriety, refrained from speaking about the matter himself. Being of an extraordinarily humble and genuine disposition, Sage Mṛkaṇḍu resolved to place the complete responsibility and decision of his marriage into the hands of Sage Parāśara. (28)
