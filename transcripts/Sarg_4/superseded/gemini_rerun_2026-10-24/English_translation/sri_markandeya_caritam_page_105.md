# Śrī Mārkaṇḍeya Caritam — Canto 4 (चतुर्थः सर्गः)
**Page 105 (पृष्ठ १०५)**

### Verse 37 (श्लोक ३७)

#### I. Sanskrit Text
```sanskrit
यज्ञसूत्रफलमिदं प्रभुसूत्रं
त्संप्रीत्या मम नु गृह्यतां प्रहर्षम् ।
अल्पकैरहमहर्गणैः प्रविष्टः
कर्तुमिह प्रयत्नविधिं विधातुम् ॥ ३७ ॥
```
*(Paleographic / Textual Note: Meter: Rathoddhatā (`ऽ।ऽ ।।। ऽ।ऽ ।ऽ`, 11 syllables per pāda). All four pādas scan at 11 syllables. In earlier versions, `सम्प्रितं मम नु गृह्यतां` and `अल्पकै रहमहर्गणे` were corrupted. This verse and the notes describe the formal betrothal ritual (*phaladāna* / *vara-rakṣā*): Mudgala offers the sacred thread (*yajñasūtra* / *brahmasūtra*) accompanied by auspicious fruit to solemnize the engagement, promising to return in a few days (*alpakaiḥ ahargaṇaiḥ*) with all preparations to conduct the full wedding ceremony.)*

#### II. Hindi Translation (Amanuensis)
यज्ञसूत्र समन्वित इस फल को मेरे लिए अनुग्रह करके आप लोग स्वीकार करें। थोड़े दिनों में सावधान होकर मैं उपस्थित इस माङ्गलिक विधि को सम्पन्न करना चाहता हूँ ॥ ३७ ॥

#### III. Grandfather's Commentary / Hindi Notes
महर्षे! आप उदारता पूर्वक मेरे ऊपर अपनी कृपापूर्ण उदारता देखकर यह फलसमन्वित ब्रह्मसूत्र (यज्ञोपवीत) जो आपको फलदान (वररक्षा) स्वरूप प्रदान करता हूँ, उसे आप मेरे ऊपर स्वीकार करें। मैं पूरी सावधानी के साथ थोड़े ही दिनों में इस वैवाहिक माङ्गलिक कार्य को सविधि पूर्ण करना चाहता हूँ। ३७

#### IV. English Translation: Verse & Literal Meaning
"May this sacred thread accompanied by auspicious fruit—this noble thread—be joyfully accepted from me with affection; within a few days, having gathered myself, I shall return to perform the required rites with every exertion." (Verse 37)

#### V. English Translation: Grandfather's Commentary & Context
"O Great Sage! Viewing me with gracious magnanimity, please accept from me this sacred thread (*yajñopavīta*) accompanied by fruit, which I offer as the formal betrothal token (*phaladāna* / *vara-rakṣā*). With meticulous care, in just a few days, I desire to bring this auspicious wedding rite to complete fulfillment according to scriptural injunctions." (37)

### Verse 38 (श्लोक ३८)

#### I. Sanskrit Text
```sanskrit
दैवतो यदिह किञ्चिदुपेया-
-त्तत् तद् भवद्भि रधुना गृहणीयम् ।
दिष्टवादिभिरतीव यतस्तत्
-त्कार्यमेव सकलं हृदयेन ॥ ३८ ॥
```
*(Paleographic / Textual Note: Meter: Rathoddhatā (`ऽ।ऽ ।।। ऽ।ऽ ।ऽ`, 11 syllables per pāda). All four pādas scan at 11 syllables. Acknowledged as Grade A across all categories in earlier assessment. Mudgala asks that whatever is provided by divine providence be gladly accepted, for believers in divine destiny must embrace such providential outcomes with an open heart.)*

#### II. Hindi Translation (Amanuensis)
भाग्यवशात् जो कुछ यहाँ उपलब्ध हो उसे सम्प्रति आप लोग स्वीकार करें। भाग्यवादियों को भी हृदय से इसे अवश्य करना चाहिए ॥ ३८ ॥

#### III. Grandfather's Commentary / Hindi Notes
ईश्वरेच्छा और दैवयोग से जो कुछ भी यहाँ मुझको उपलब्ध हो जायगा मैं समस्त वस्तुजात आपको समर्पित करूँगा, आप भी सहर्ष प्रसन्नतापूर्वक उसे स्वीकारें, हम भाग्यवादियों को जो भी दैवयोग से उपस्थित हो जाय उसे सहृदयतापूर्वक अवश्य स्वीकार करना चाहिए। ३८

#### IV. English Translation: Verse & Literal Meaning
"Whatever may come to hand here through providence, all of that ought to be accepted by your reverences at present; for by those who trust in divine destiny, every such outcome must indeed be embraced whole-heartedly." (Verse 38)

#### V. English Translation: Grandfather's Commentary & Context
"Whatever resources become available to me through God's will and providential destiny, I shall offer all of them to you; may you too accept them with cheerful delight. For we who trust in providence must whole-heartedly welcome whatever situation presents itself through divine arrangement." (38)
