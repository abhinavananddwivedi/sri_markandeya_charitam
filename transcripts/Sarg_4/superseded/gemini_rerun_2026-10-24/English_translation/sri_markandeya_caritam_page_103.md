# Śrī Mārkaṇḍeya Caritam — Canto 4 (चतुर्थः सर्गः)
**Page 103 (पृष्ठ १०३)**

---

### Verse 33 (श्लोक ३३)

#### I. Sanskrit Text
```sanskrit
देयमस्ति न मया किमपीह
किन्तु साऽत्र गुणतोऽस्त्यनुरूपा ।
एतयोः परिणये भवता वै
सर्व एव विधिरस्त्युपपाद्यः ॥ ३३ ॥
```
*(Paleographic / Textual Note: Meter: Rathoddhatā (`ऽ।ऽ ।।। ऽ।ऽ ।ऽ`, 11 syllables per pāda). All four pādas scan at 11 syllables. In earlier versions, the notes erroneously addressed `मुद्गलाचार्य!`. The scan clearly reveals the vocative `महर्षे!` addressed to Sage Parāśara by Mudgala. Mudgala states that he has no worldly dowry to give, but his daughter is perfectly matched in virtue to Mṛkaṇḍu, and requests Parāśara to solemnize all the wedding rites.)*

#### II. Hindi Translation (Amanuensis)
मेरे पास देय कुछ नहीं है, किन्तु मेरी कन्या इसके गुणानुरूप ही है। इन दोनों के विवाह में सारा कार्य आप ही सम्पादित करें ॥ ३३ ॥

#### III. Grandfather's Commentary / Hindi Notes
महर्षे! मेरे पास यौतुक (दहेज) देने के लिए कुछ नहीं है, मैं कुछ दे नहीं सकता किन्तु हाँ मेरी कन्या इनके गुणानुरूप सर्वथा सुयोग्य है। अतः इन दोनों के विवाह सम्बन्ध में सारी विधियाँ सभी कार्य जात केवल आपको ही करना है। ३३

#### IV. English Translation: Verse & Literal Meaning
"There is nothing whatsoever here to be given by me (as dowry), but she is fully suited to him in virtues; in the marriage of these two, indeed every rite must be performed by your revered self." (Verse 33)

#### V. English Translation: Grandfather's Commentary & Context
"O revered Great Sage! I possess nothing to offer as dowry (*yautuka*), nor can I bestow worldly riches; yet my daughter is eminently worthy and well-matched to him in virtues. Therefore, regarding the matrimonial alliance between the two, every ritual procedure and ceremonial duty rests solely upon you to perform." (33)

### Verse 34 (श्लोक ३४)

#### I. Sanskrit Text
```sanskrit
वल्कलानि परिधाय तरूणां-
-मातपे हिमजलादि सहित्वा ।
कन्दमूलफलभोजनशीलाः
जङ्गलेषु मुनयो निवसामः ॥ ३४ ॥
```
*(Paleographic / Textual Note: Meter: Rathoddhatā (`ऽ।ऽ ।।। ऽ।ऽ ।ऽ`, 11 syllables per pāda). All four pādas scan at 11 syllables. In line 2, earlier drafts misread `सहित्वा` as the non-word `सरित्वा`. In the manuscript, the word is clearly `सहित्वा` (*sahitvā*, the regular absolutive from $\sqrt{sah}$, enduring). Mudgala describes the ascetic life of sages: wearing tree bark, enduring heat, frost, and icy waters, and subsisting on roots and wild fruits in the forest.)*

#### II. Hindi Translation (Amanuensis)
वृक्षों के छाल पहन कर आतप ठण्डी गर्मी को सहन कर कन्द-मूल-फलादि भक्षण करने वाले हम मुनिगण इस वन्य-भूमि में रहते हैं ॥ ३४ ॥

#### III. Grandfather's Commentary / Hindi Notes
अहो मुनीन्द्र! हम लोग सदा वृक्षों की छाल पहनकर, वहाँ की भीषण सरदी गर्मी (शीत उष्णतादि) अत्यन्त शीतल बर्फीली नदियों का जल आदि का सहन करते हुए जंगली फल मूल कन्दादि का भोजन कर जंगलों में रहते हुए मुनिवृत्ति से अपना जीवन निर्वाह करते हुए रह रहे हैं। ३४

#### IV. English Translation: Verse & Literal Meaning
"Wearing the bark garments of trees, enduring heat, snow-waters, and cold, subsisting on roots, tubers, and fruits, we sages dwell in the forests." (Verse 34)

#### V. English Translation: Grandfather's Commentary & Context
"O foremost of sages! We live constantly clad in the bark of trees, braving severe cold and heat, enduring the icy waters of mountain streams, subsisting on wild fruits and edible roots, living in the woods in full observance of ascetic discipline." (34)
