# Śrī Mārkaṇḍeya Caritam — Canto 4 (चतुर्थः सर्गः)
**Page 110 (पृष्ठ ११०)**

---

### Verse 47 (श्लोक ४७)

#### I. Sanskrit Text
```sanskrit
निश्चितं वरणमेव समये सोऽ-
-तीव सानन्दकरं सुविभाव्य ।
प्राप्य सद्म भगवन्तमभीष्टं
मुद्गलो हृदयतोऽनुजुहाव ॥ ४७ ॥
```
*(Paleographic / Textual Note: Meter: Rathoddhatā (`ऽ।ऽ ।।। ऽ।ऽ ।ऽ`, 11 syllables per pāda). All four pādas scan at 11 syllables. Pāda 1: `निश्चितं वरणमेव समये सोऽ-` (11; replacing the previous conjectural text `निश्चित्य वारं शुभं समयेऽथ सार्द्ध-`); Pāda 2: `-तीव सानन्दकरं सुविभाव्य` (11; replacing `-न्तीव्रं सानन्दकरं`); Pāda 3: `प्राप्य सद्म भगवन्तमभीष्टं` (11; replacing `प्रययौ सहैवात्मजनाभिगच्छन्`; the manuscript explicitly mentions returning home to remember the Lord); Pāda 4: `मुद्गलो हृदयतोऽनुजुहाव` (11; eliminating the corrupt reading `मुद्गलो हृदयतोऽनुयुतान`; `अनुजुहाव` translates the Hindi "अभीष्ट भगवान का हृदय से आह्वान किया"). Recognizing that the auspicious time for the bridegroom selection was imminent, sage Mudgala arrived at his home and invoked his cherished deity from his heart.)*

#### II. Hindi Translation (Amanuensis)
वर वरण का निश्चित समय अतीव समीप जानकर विनम्र वर मुद्गल अपने घर आकर अभीष्ट भगवान का हृदय से आह्वान किया ॥ ४७ ॥

#### III. Grandfather's Commentary / Hindi Notes
घर आने पर मुनि मुद्गल ने अब वैवाहिक तैयारियों के लिए और वरण का समय अत्यन्त सन्निकट जानकर अपने अभीष्ट देवता को हृदय से ध्यान करते हुए एवम् पूर्वक स्तुति की तथा अपनी अभीष्ट पूर्ति के बाद आह्लादित रहे। ४७

#### IV. English Translation: Verse & Literal Meaning
Recognizing that the appointed time for the wedding selection was exceedingly close and propitious, Mudgala returned home and earnestly invoked his chosen deity from the depths of his heart. (Verse 47)

#### V. English Translation: Grandfather's Commentary & Context
Upon returning home, sage Mudgala realized that the auspicious hour for the nuptial selection was drawing near. Meditating with heartfelt devotion on his cherished deity, he offered formal prayers for the successful completion of the wedding preparations, remaining joyous upon fulfilling his prayers. (47)

### Verse 48 (श्लोक ४८)

#### I. Sanskrit Text
```sanskrit
तत्र चेशकृपया सकलानि
शीघ्रमेव कलितानि वसूनि ।
चारुसम्भरणमेव निरीक्ष्य
बन्धुभिः प्रजहृषेऽपि समर्थैः ॥ ४८ ॥
```
*(Paleographic / Textual Note: Meter: Rathoddhatā (`ऽ।ऽ ।।। ऽ।ऽ ।ऽ`, 11 syllables per pāda). All four pādas scan at 11 syllables. Pāda 1: `तत्र चेशकृपया सकलानि` (11; `तत्र च ईशकृपया` = by the grace of God); Pāda 2: `शीघ्रमेव कलितानि वसूनि` (11; the non-word `शमूनि` from the previous reading is corrected to `वसूनि`, signifying auspicious articles and wealth); Pāda 3: `चारुसम्भरणमेव निरीक्ष्य` (11); Pāda 4: `बन्धुभिः प्रजहृषेऽपि समर्थैः` (11; the non-word `लब्धुभिः` is corrected to `बन्धुभिः`, denoting the kinsmen and relatives). By divine grace, all necessary ceremonial provisions were assembled swiftly; beholding the splendid collection, the capable kinsmen rejoiced greatly.)*

#### II. Hindi Translation (Amanuensis)
अतः ईश्वरकृपा से सभी वस्तु शीघ्र ही एकत्रित हो गये। सुन्दर सभी सामग्रियों को देखकर वरण के साथ बान्धव भी आनन्दित हो उठे ॥ ४८ ॥

#### III. Grandfather's Commentary / Hindi Notes
ऋषि मुद्गल की ईश्वर के प्रति अतीव आस्था एवं निष्ठा से उनके देखते देखते ही सपरिवार वरण की सामग्रियाँ उनके मनोनुकूल स्वतः सम्पन्न हो गईं, उन सामग्रियों को देख कर जो उनके कल्याण से भी उत्तम एवं आशा से भी पूर्ण अनुपम थीं, जिसे देख कर सपरिवार ऋषि आनन्दातिरेक से अत्यन्त हर्षित हो उठे । ४८

#### IV. English Translation: Verse & Literal Meaning
By divine grace, all the required items were gathered promptly. Beholding the beautiful accumulation of provisions, the capable kinsmen rejoiced greatly along with the bridegroom. (Verse 48)

#### V. English Translation: Grandfather's Commentary & Context
Owing to sage Mudgala's unshakeable faith and devotion toward God, all necessary supplies for the wedding ceremony materialized spontaneously to his complete satisfaction before his family's eyes. Beholding these provisions, which surpassed all expectations and promised great welfare, the sage and his entire household were filled with immense joy. (48)
