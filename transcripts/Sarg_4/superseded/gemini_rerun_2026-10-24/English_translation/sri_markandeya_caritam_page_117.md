# Śrī Mārkaṇḍeya Caritam — Canto 4 (चतुर्थः सर्गः)
**Page 117 (पृष्ठ ११७)**

---

### Verse 61 (श्लोक ६१)

#### I. Sanskrit Text
```sanskrit
स्वास्तिवाचकवरैः प्रतितान्तो-
-धाक्षतान् स्वशिरसा विभ्रदतः ।
देवमण्डलविसर्जनपूर्वं
प्रागतो स्वकुलदेवमुपास्त ॥ ६१ ॥
```
*(Paleographic / Textual Note: Meter: Rathoddhatā (`ऽ।ऽ ।।। ऽ।ऽ ।ऽ`, 11 syllables per pāda). All four pādas scan at 11 syllables. Corrects earlier garbled transcriptions (`स्वाहिति वाच्य वरैः प्रतितान्तयोः` and `धातशतन्`). The verse portrays Sage Mṛkaṇḍu receiving the consecrated grains of benediction (*svasti-akṣata*) on his head and revering his ancestral lineage deities prior to the formal ceremonial dismissal (*visarjana*) of the invited divine assembly.)*

#### II. Hindi Translation (Amanuensis)
उन्होंने स्वस्तिवाचकों के द्वारा दिये गये अक्षतों को अपने सिर पर धारण किया, देवों के विसर्जन के पूर्व ही अपने कुलदेवता की उपासना की ॥ ६१ ॥

#### III. Grandfather's Commentary / Hindi Notes
मुनि मृकण्डु ने स्वस्तिवाचन करनेवाले उच्चतम विद्वन्मण्डली द्वारा प्रदत्त पूज्य पुञ्ज अक्षत समूह को बड़ी विनम्रता के साथ विनम्रतापूर्वक अपने सिर पर धारण किया साथ ही सम्मुख संस्थापित गौरी गणपति आदि देवों के विसर्जन के पूर्व अपने पूज्य कुलदेवताओं की भी अभ्यर्थना की। ६१

#### IV. English Translation: Verse & Literal Meaning
He placed upon his head the sacred unbroken grains (*akṣata*) bestowed by the eminent chanters of benedictions (*svasti-vācaka*), and prior to the ceremonial farewell (*visarjana*) of the assembled deities, he devoutly worshipped his own lineage deity (*kula-devatā*). (Verse 61)

#### V. English Translation: Grandfather's Commentary & Context
With deep humility and reverence, Sage Mṛkaṇḍu placed upon his head the holy heap of grains (*akṣata*) presented by the eminent assembly of scholars chanting benedictory verses (*svasti-vācana*). Concurrently, prior to the ritual dismissal (*visarjana*) of the consecrated deities installed before him—headed by Gaurī and Gaṇapati—he offered fervent prayers and worship unto his venerable ancestral family guardians. (61)

### Verse 62 (श्लोक ६२)

#### I. Sanskrit Text
```sanskrit
सम्भ्रमेण परया किल भक्त्या
विप्रवृन्दमपि दक्षिणनाथम् ।
आशिषा किल विवर्धिततेजा-
स्ते पराशरमुनिं प्रननाम ॥ ६२ ॥
```
*(Paleographic / Textual Note: Meter: Rathoddhatā (`ऽ।ऽ ।।। ऽ।ऽ ।ऽ`, 11 syllables per pāda). In the manuscript, the marginal heading `शम्भुभक्त` stands above the verse as an epithet/topic heading and is not part of line 1 (resolving the previous error where it was improperly incorporated into the Sanskrit text). All four pādas scan at 11 syllables. Replaces `विप्रयुङ्केभ्यो` with `विप्रवृन्दमपि दक्षिणनाथम्`, and `प्रणानाम` with the classical perfect `प्रननाम`. Having honored the assembly of southern sages and being enriched by their blessings, Mṛkaṇḍu bowed in deep veneration before Maharṣi Parāśara.)*

#### II. Hindi Translation (Amanuensis)
पूर्णभक्ति के साथ दक्षिण से समागत विप्रवृन्दों को प्रणाम कर आशीर्वाद से वर्धिततेज वाले उन्होंने पराशर मुनि को भी प्रणाम किया ॥ ६२ ॥

#### III. Grandfather's Commentary / Hindi Notes
कलश विसर्जन के अनन्तर मुनि मृकण्डु ने दक्षिण प्रान्त के सभी ऋषियों विद्वज्जणों को ससम्मान सादर प्रणाम किया, सभी ऋषियों ब्राह्मण जनों के द्वारा आशीर्वाद प्राप्त कर आत्मतेजोद्दीप्त मृकण्डु जी ने महर्षि पराशर जी को भी सश्रद्ध विनयावनत होकर प्रणाम किया। ६२

#### IV. English Translation: Verse & Literal Meaning
With deep devotion, having bowed unto the assembly of brāhmaṇas arrived from the South, his inner luster magnified by their benedictions, he reverently prostrated before Sage Parāśara. (Verse 62)

#### V. English Translation: Grandfather's Commentary & Context
Following the formal culmination of the pitcher rites (*kalaśa-visarjana*), Sage Mṛkaṇḍu respectfully bowed unto all the venerable sages and learned scholars arrived from the southern realms. Enriched by the heartfelt blessings bestowed by all the assembled brāhmaṇas, Mṛkaṇḍu, radiant with spiritual brilliance, bowed low in humble reverence before Maharṣi Parāśara. (62)
