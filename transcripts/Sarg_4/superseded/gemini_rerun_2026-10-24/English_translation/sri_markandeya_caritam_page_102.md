# Śrī Mārkaṇḍeya Caritam — Canto 4 (चतुर्थः सर्गः)
**Page 102 (पृष्ठ १०२)**

---

### Verse 31 (श्लोक ३१)

#### I. Sanskrit Text
```sanskrit
स्पष्टवाचि न हि वञ्चकतेति
स्पष्टमेव निखिला निगदन्ति ।
आर्य! तेन कथयामि भवन्तं
प्रार्थनां सफलयन्तु भवन्तः ॥ ३१ ॥
```
*(Paleographic / Textual Note: Meter: Rathoddhatā (`ऽ।ऽ ।।। ऽ।ऽ ।ऽ`, 11 syllables per pāda). All four pādas scan at 11 syllables. In earlier versions, syllable count was irregular (9 and 12 syllables). Here the verse scans cleanly. Mudgala explains that speaking plainly carries no deceit, and petitions the venerable assembly to bring his prayer to fulfillment.)*

#### II. Hindi Translation (Amanuensis)
स्पष्ट बोलने में वञ्चकता नहीं होती, सभी स्पष्ट ही बोलते हैं। हे आर्य! आप सभी मेरी प्रार्थना को सफल करें ॥ ३१ ॥

#### III. Grandfather's Commentary / Hindi Notes
भगवन् स्पष्ट बात कह देने में कोई धूर्तता नहीं हो सकती, सभी लोग स्पष्ट बातें ही कहते हैं। अतः हे श्रेष्ठ मुनिवर! आपसे इसीलिए मैं अपना स्पष्ट निवेदन कर रहा हूँ, आप मेरी यह कन्या विवाह की प्रार्थना पूर्ण करने की कृपा करें। ३१

#### IV. English Translation: Verse & Literal Meaning
"There is no deception in plain speech; indeed, all righteous persons speak clearly. Therefore, O noble sage, I submit plainly to you: may your reverences make my petition fruitful." (Verse 31)

#### V. English Translation: Grandfather's Commentary & Context
"O Lord! There can be no duplicity in stating a matter candidly; truthful people speak only with clarity. Therefore, O foremost sage, I place my straightforward supplication before you: please be gracious enough to fulfill this petition of mine regarding the marriage of my daughter." (31)

### Verse 32 (श्लोक ३२)

#### I. Sanskrit Text
```sanskrit
दाक्षिणाच्छ्रुतमपूर्वगुणौघं
स्नातकं किल मृकण्डुमहर्षिम् ।
आगतोऽस्मि तत एव चिकीर्षुः
पाणिपीडनमहं तनयायाः ॥ ३२ ॥
```
*(Paleographic / Textual Note: Meter: Rathoddhatā (`ऽ।ऽ ।।। ऽ।ऽ ।ऽ`, 11 syllables per pāda). All four pādas scan at 11 syllables. Crucial textual resolution of the speaker controversy: The speaker is Sage Mudgala himself, who states that having heard from Dākṣi (`दाक्षिणात् श्रुतम्`) regarding the bachelor status and unprecedented virtue of Sage Mṛkaṇḍu, he has travelled specifically to propose the marriage of his daughter. There is no speaker contradiction across verses 23–39.)*

#### II. Hindi Translation (Amanuensis)
दाक्षी से स्नातक महर्षि मृकण्डु के गुणसमूह को सुना है, उसी कार्य के सम्पादना अर्थ अपनी कन्या के विवाहार्थ मैं यहाँ आया हूँ ॥ ३२ ॥

#### III. Grandfather's Commentary / Hindi Notes
मुनिश्रेष्ठ दाक्षी से मुनि मृकण्डु की अद्भुत पूर्व गुण राशि का मैंने निरन्तर श्रवण किया है, ये महर्षि अभी स्नातक हैं। इसीलिए इस महर्षि के साथ अपनी कन्या का विवाह संस्कार करने की अभिलाषा से मैं यहाँ आया हुआ हूँ। ३२

#### IV. English Translation: Verse & Literal Meaning
"Having heard from Dākṣi of the unprecedented treasure of virtues of the bachelor sage Mṛkaṇḍu, for that very reason have I arrived here, desiring to perform the hand-taking (marriage) of my daughter." (Verse 32)

#### V. English Translation: Grandfather's Commentary & Context
"I have repeatedly heard from the revered sage Dākṣi about the wonderful, unparalleled virtues of Sage Mṛkaṇḍu, and that this great sage remains a celibate scholar (*snātaka*). It is precisely for this reason that I have come here with the ardent wish to solemnize the sacred marriage of my daughter with this exalted sage." (32)
