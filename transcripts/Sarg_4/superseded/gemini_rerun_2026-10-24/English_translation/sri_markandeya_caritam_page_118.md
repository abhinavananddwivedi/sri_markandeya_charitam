# Śrī Mārkaṇḍeya Caritam — Canto 4 (चतुर्थः सर्गः)
**Page 118 (पृष्ठ ११८)**

---

### Verse 63 (श्लोक ६३)

#### I. Sanskrit Text
```sanskrit
सन्तोष्याऽखिलविप्रमण्डलमथो गेहागताँश्चातिथीन्
दीनानाथाजनांश्चौ सुविहितैर्भोज्यैश्च षड्भी रसैः ।
सन्तृप्तेष्वखिलेषु तत्परिजनेष्वात्मैव स्वयं प्रीणितः
सुप्त्वाऽयं गेहगतस्तत्परो भगवतो लीलाविलासांश्चित्तगाः ॥ ६३ ॥
```
*(Paleographic / Textual Note: Meter: Śārdūlavikrīḍita (`सूर्य्याश्वैर्मसजस्ततः सगुरवः शार्दूलविक्रीडितम्`, 19 syllables per pāda, caesura at 12 and 7 syllables). All four pādas scan at 19 syllables. Resolves earlier metric misidentification. Concluding the wedding celebrations at Mount Dardura, Sage Mṛkaṇḍu lavishly feasted the entire company of learned sages, guests, relatives, poor, and orphaned with foods endowed with all six tastes; after all had dined to fullness and his relatives were gratified, he himself ate with joy and retired to sleep, meditatively absorbed in the divine sports of the Supreme Lord.)*

#### II. Hindi Translation (Amanuensis)
सुन्दर रसों से पूर्ण सुस्वादु भोज्य पदार्थों से समस्त विप्रमण्डली को गृहागत अतिथियों को एवं दीनानाथों को सन्तुष्ट कर सन्तुष्ट परिजनों के माध्यम से सभी लोगों के सन्तुष्ट हो जाने पर स्वतः आनन्दित हो स्मृतिमान् शुद्धि से भगवान के लीला विलासों का स्मरण किया ॥ ६३ ॥

#### III. Grandfather's Commentary / Hindi Notes
मुनि मृकण्डु ने अपने घर में उत्तम सूपों (पाचकों) द्वारा सभी षड्रसों से परिपूर्ण विविध भोज्यों (भक्ष्य, भोज्य, लेह्य, चोष्य आदि एवं मधुमेयी मोदकादि से) भी सभी ब्राह्मणों, विद्वज्जनों, अभ्यागतों, गृहस्थ बन्धु बान्धवों, दीनों, अनाथों को पूर्ण परितृप्ति से भोजन कराया। सभी लोगों के भोजनादि से पूर्ण सन्तुष्ट हो जाने पर अपने परिजनों के भी भोजन उपरान्त स्वयं प्रसन्न होकर निर्विकल्प भगवान् के लीलाविलास का स्मरण करते सुखपूर्वक सोये। ६३

#### IV. English Translation: Verse & Literal Meaning
Having thoroughly gratified the entire assembly of brāhmaṇas, the arrived houseguests, and the destitute and helpless with sumptuous foods prepared with all six tastes, and all persons being completely contented, he himself partook of food with his family and, joyous and peaceful, retired to rest while contemplating the divine play (*līlā-vilāsa*) of the Supreme Lord. (Verse 63)

#### V. English Translation: Grandfather's Commentary & Context
In his hermitage home, Sage Mṛkaṇḍu served a lavish feast prepared by expert culinary cooks, rich with all six savors—comprising the fourfold traditional delicacies (*bhakṣya, bhojya, lehya, coṣya*) and sweet confections. He generously fed the brāhmaṇas, scholars, arrived guests, relatives, poor, and orphaned to their total satisfaction. Once all attendees and his own family members had dined to contentment, he himself ate with gladness and peacefully retired to sleep, contemplating the unconditioned sports and pastimes of God. (63)

### Verses 64–66 (श्लोक ६४–६५–६६ — Signature Triad & Canto Colophon)

#### I. Sanskrit Text
```sanskrit
श्रीचन्द्रभूषणसुधीसुतकेन बन्धूरामस्य पौत्रसुभगेन नवंगुणाढ्यम् ।
संगुम्फितं सुमनसां मनसाऽऽदृत्य मनसाऽङ्घ्रियुग्मं मानम्य तेन हि शिवस्य सुशिष्यकेण ॥ ६४ ॥

धानेश्वरीहृदयजेन शिवार्चकेन विद्वज्जनार्चनरता धरणीधरेण ।
काव्यं सुमङ्गलमयं सुप्रवृत्तिपूर्णं शाश्वत् समा विजयतां शिवयोः प्रसादात् ॥ ६५ ॥

दीर्घजीविचरितामृतकाव्ये सद्विजोचितसदाऽऽचरणाढ्ये ।
एष तेन रचिते काव्यनाम्ना सर्गोऽनुगामितोऽस्तीह तुरीयः ॥ ६६ ॥
```
*(Paleographic / Textual Note: Signature triad and concluding invocation appearing at the close of each sarga, celebrating ancestral lineage, devotional surrender to Mother Dhāneśvarī and Lord Śiva, and the completion of the canto. Pādas 3–4 of Verse 66 specifically mark the conclusion of the fourth canto: `एष तेन रचिते काव्यनाम्ना सर्गोऽनुगामितोऽस्तीह तुरीयः`.)*

#### II. Author's Note / Direction
विशेष:- ६४-६५-६६ का हिन्दी अनुवाद पृ. ३२ में देखें-  
सद् द्विजो के आशीर्वादों से पूर्ण दीर्घजीवी (मार्कण्डेय) चरितामृत नामक काव्य में भावनात्मक यह चौथा साहित्य पूर्ण हुआ ।

#### III. English Translation: Signature Verses & Literal Meaning
Woven with care by the son of the wise Śrī Candra Bhūṣaṇa, the blessed grandson of Banchū Rāma, endowed with fresh poetical virtues, reverencing the twin feet of Lord Śiva in the heart of noble minds, by that worthy disciple of Śiva; (Verse 64)  
Born from the heart of Mother Dhāneśvarī, worshipper of Śiva, who delights in honoring learned scholars, by Dharaṇīdhara: may this auspicious epic poem, filled with righteous impulses, triumph eternally through the divine grace of Śiva and Śivā! (Verse 65)  
In this epic poem of the nectar of the long-lived Sage Mārkaṇḍeya's life, adorned with the righteous conduct worthy of noble brāhmaṇas, in the poem composed by him, this fourth canto has now reached its successful completion. (Verse 66)

#### IV. English Translation: Grandfather's Commentary & Context
*Special Manuscript Note*: For the detailed word-by-word exposition of verses 64–66, refer to page 32. In this sacred epic *Dīrghajīvi-caritāmṛta* (The Nectar of the Life of Sage Mārkaṇḍeya), rich with the benedictions of venerable brāhmaṇas, this fourth canto of emotional and narrative significance has reached its complete fulfillment. (64–66)
