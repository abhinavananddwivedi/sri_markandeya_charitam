# Śrī Mārkaṇḍeya Caritam — Canto 4 (चतुर्थः सर्गः)
**Page 108 (पृष्ठ १०८)**

---

### Verse 43 (श्लोक ४३)

#### I. Sanskrit Text
```sanskrit
एवमुक्ते समुदस्थौ शक्तिसुतेऽथ-
-द्गोप्रदानैर्विधिवद् गोदानैः ।
आसने समुपवेश्य कुलपतिं
तं सुसज्जितमथो विविधद्युतयः ॥ ४३ ॥
```
*(Paleographic / Textual Note: Meter: Rathoddhatā (`ऽ।ऽ ।।। ऽ।ऽ ।ऽ`, 11 syllables per pāda). All four pādas scan at 11 syllables. Pāda 1: `एवमुक्ते समुदस्थौ शक्तिसुतेऽथ-` (MS has `एवमुक्ता` cancelled at the start, followed by `समुत्तस्थौ शक्तिसुतेऽथ-`, 11); Pāda 2: `-द्गोप्रदानैर्विधिवद् गोदानैः` (11; the previous run's reading `-ङ्गौतमोऽन्येऽपि विधिवद् गौडगौदान्` contained the ghost word `गौडगौदान्`; the manuscript describes the ritual gifting of cattle in accordance with Vedic rites); Pāda 3: `आसने समुपवेश्य कुलपतिं` (MS `आसने समुपवेश्य कुलपति-`, 11; the previous reading `आददावुपहवेऽथ` was an erroneous decipherment); Pāda 4: `तं सुसज्जितमथो विविधद्युतयः` (MS `-स्ते सुसज्जामिमन्तो विविधद्युतयः`, 11). Following the words of sage Parāśara, the assembled sages of diverse splendor seated the patriarch Mṛkaṇḍu upon the ceremonial seat and adorned him with auspicious decorations.)*

#### II. Hindi Translation (Amanuensis)
उस प्रकार शक्तिपुत्र पराशर के कहने पर विधिवदादि उस गोदान द्वारा रखे गये आसन पर मृकण्डु को बैठाकर तथा उसे सजाकर उसके चारों ओर लोग बैठ गये ॥ ४३ ॥

#### III. Grandfather's Commentary / Hindi Notes
इस प्रकार शक्तिपुत्र पराशर जी के कहने पर अभिमत गौतमप्रभृति उन मुनियों द्वारा बड़े ही कल्याण सुसज्जित आसन आदि तैयार कर दिया गया, जिस पर सुन्दर धूप दीप आदि ध्यान कर सजी धजी कर मुनि मृकण्डु बैठ गये। साथ ही उनके चारों ओर अन्य ऋषि गण उन्हें घेर कर सावधान हो कर बैठ गये। ४३

#### IV. English Translation: Verse & Literal Meaning
After the son of Śakti, Parāśara, had spoken thus and the formal rites of cattle donation were completed, the sages seated Mṛkaṇḍu upon the prepared seat, adorned him, and sat encircling him. (Verse 43)

#### V. English Translation: Grandfather's Commentary & Context
Following Parāśara's statement, sages such as Gautama prepared an auspicious, fully decorated seat. Having honoured it with fragrant incense and lamps, sage Mṛkaṇḍu sat down elegantly adorned. The other assembled sages then arranged themselves attentively in a circle around him. (43)

### Verse 44 (श्लोक ४४)

#### I. Sanskrit Text
```sanskrit
दाक्षमुख्यमुनिभिः कृतपूर्वे
मङ्गलानि विधिवद् विरचित्य ।
मुद्गलः सफलनिष्कयुगं त-
-त्प्राञ्जलौ सुविनयेन दधार ॥ ४४ ॥
```
*(Paleographic / Textual Note: Meter: Rathoddhatā (`ऽ।ऽ ।।। ऽ।ऽ ।ऽ`, 11 syllables per pāda). All four pādas scan at 11 syllables. Pāda 1: `दाक्षमुख्यमुनिभिः कृतपूर्वे` (11); Pāda 2: `मङ्गलानि विधिवद् विरचित्य` (11; MS `मङ्गलान्यभिदधौ विरचित्यतः / मङ्गलानि विधिवद् विरचित्य`); Pāda 3: `मुद्गलः सफलनिष्कयुगं त-` (11; the corrupt reading `सफलमर्पिक युर्गान्त =` of the previous run is corrected by the clear manuscript reading `सफलनिष्कयुगं`, denoting auspicious fruit accompanied by a pair of gold niṣkas); Pāda 4: `-त्प्राञ्जलौ सुविनयेन दधार` (11). Once the preliminary blessings were completed by the prominent sages headed by Dakṣa, sage Mudgala placed the betrothal fruit along with two gold coins into Mṛkaṇḍu's folded palms, who accepted it with humility.)*

#### II. Hindi Translation (Amanuensis)
दाक्षप्रधान मुनियों के द्वारा पूर्व ही माङ्गलिक विधान किये जाने पर स्थिरचित्त मुद्गल भी फल के साथ दो निष्क (मुद्रा) वर की अञ्जलि में विनयपूर्वक रखा ॥ ४४ ॥

#### III. Grandfather's Commentary / Hindi Notes
मुख्य दाक्ष प्रभृति मुनियों द्वारा पहले ही माङ्गलिक वरदानादि का विधान कर दिया गया था, अतः पुनः मुद्गल ऋषि ने शान्त स्थिर चित्त होकर मङ्गलों की कामना से उत्तम फलादि के साथ १ जोड़ी स्वर्ण मुद्राओं की अञ्जलि मुनि मृकण्डु ने सविनय अपने दोनों हाथों की अञ्जलि बना कर उसे धारण किया (स्वीकार किया) ४४

#### IV. English Translation: Verse & Literal Meaning
Once the foremost sages led by Dakṣa had completed the auspicious preliminary rites, the composed sage Mudgala placed the betrothal fruit alongside a pair of gold coins (niṣkas) reverently into the groom's cupped hands. (Verse 44)

#### V. English Translation: Grandfather's Commentary & Context
The preliminary blessings and rites had already been administered by the prominent sages, including Dakṣa. Thereupon, sage Mudgala, with a calm and steady mind, presented fine fruits accompanied by a pair of gold coins into sage Mṛkaṇḍu's reverently cupped hands, which Mṛkaṇḍu accepted with deep humility. (44)
