# Śrī Mārkaṇḍeya Caritam — Canto 4 (चतुर्थः सर्गः)
**Page 87 (पृष्ठ ८७)**

---

### Verse 1 (श्लोक १)

#### I. Sanskrit Text
```sanskrit
तं प्रणम्य कृतनित्यविधिकः प्राक्
स्वापतोऽथ दिवसे विरहितेऽह्नि ।
प्रातरेव सह चारुगवा युक्तः
सत्सहायवशगोऽनु चचाल ॥ १ ॥
```
*(Paleographic / Textual Note: Meter: Rathoddhatā (`ऽ।ऽ ।।। ऽ।ऽ ।ऽ`, 11 syllables per pāda). All four pādas scan at exactly 11 syllables. In the manuscript, the amanuensis accidentally copied lines from verse 2 into the Hindi translation and struck them through; an interlinear insertion reads `समृद्ध तेजोध्वज मुनिवर`. Accompanied by the auspicious cow (*cāru-gau*) and sage Aṅgiras, Sage Mṛkaṇḍu departs after paying homage.)*

#### II. Hindi Translation (Amanuensis)
समृद्ध तेजोध्वज मुनिवर नित्यकर्म करने के पूर्व उन्हें प्रणाम कर अपनी आशीर्वाद से प्रातःकाल ही चारु गौ के साथ सत्सहायक के वशीभूत हो चल पड़े ॥ १ ॥

#### III. Grandfather's Commentary / Hindi Notes
इसके बाद ऋषि मृकण्डु ने नित्य क्रिया आदि कर लेने पर पहले मुनिश्रेष्ठ पराशर जी को प्रणाम किया, तथा उनकी आशीर्वाद प्राप्त कर वे और भी अधिक तेजोदीप्त हो उठे, और प्रातःकाल ही अपने सवत्सा धेनु गायों (चारु गायों) के साथ अपने सहयोगी आङ्गिरस ऋषि के साथ जो उच्च कोटि के सहयोगी हैं, अतः उनके अनुकूल ही चल पड़े। १

#### IV. English Translation: Verse & Literal Meaning
The resplendent, illustrious sage, having bowed before him prior to his morning rites, set forth early at dawn under his blessings, accompanied by the beautiful cow and happily guided by his noble companion. (Verse 1)

#### V. English Translation: Grandfather's Commentary & Context
Thereafter, upon concluding his customary morning rites, Sage Mṛkaṇḍu first offered salutations to the exalted sage Parāśara. Having received his blessings, he shone with even greater radiant luster; and at early dawn, accompanied by his milch cows with calves (the auspicious cows) and his assistant Sage Aṅgiras—an associate of the highest stature—he journeyed onward harmoniously in alignment with him. (1)

### Verse 2 (श्लोक २)

#### I. Sanskrit Text
```sanskrit
सत्यमद्य स्वसमाधि स्वसहाय-
न्नाभ्यमन्यत यतो बहुदर्शी ।
ईश्वरोऽपि निजशक्तिसहायः
सृष्टिकर्मणि बभूव समर्थः ॥ २ ॥
```
*(Paleographic / Textual Note: Meter: Rathoddhatā (11 syllables per pāda). Line 3 clearly reads `ईश्वरोऽपि निजशक्तिसहायः` in the scan. The diplomatic reading `नाभ्यमन्यत` signifies "did not disregard/disdain", which the grandfather's note rightly elucidates as `तिरस्कार नहीं किया`, resolving the amanuensis's loose Hindi rendering.)*

#### II. Hindi Translation (Amanuensis)
अपनी तपस्या के विद्यमान होने पर उन्होंने अपने सहायक स्वीकार नहीं किया क्यों कि वे बहुदर्शी थे। ईश्वर भी अपनी शक्ति की सहायता से ही सृष्टि कर्म में समर्थ हुआ ॥ २ ॥

#### III. Grandfather's Commentary / Hindi Notes
मुनि मृकण्डु यद्यपि स्वयं अपनी तपस्या की आध्यात्मिकता से तेजोदीप्त थे, वे यद्यपि सांसारिक सम्पूर्ण वस्तुओं क्रियाओं के पूर्ण अभिज्ञ थे, उन्हें किसी सहचर या अनुचर की आवश्यकता नहीं थी किन्तु मुनि पराशर जी के दिये सहयोगी मुनि आङ्गिरस को उन्होंने तिरस्कार नहीं किया, क्योंकि ईश्वर भी यद्यपि सृष्टि संरचना स्वयं कर सकता है किन्तु उसने भी अपने सृष्टि संरचना में अपनी शक्ति को ही सहायिका बनाया था। २

#### IV. English Translation: Verse & Literal Meaning
Even in the presence of his own austere contemplation, he did not consider an assistant necessary because he was far-sighted; yet even God became capable in the work of creation only with the aid of His own Divine Power. (Verse 2)

#### V. English Translation: Grandfather's Commentary & Context
Though Sage Mṛkaṇḍu was radiant with the spiritual luster of his own asceticism and intimately versed in all worldly matters and actions—needing neither companion nor attendant—he nevertheless did not disregard the assistant Sage Aṅgiras provided by Sage Parāśara; for even God, though capable of creating the universe entirely by Himself, took His own Divine Power (Śakti) as His assistant in cosmic creation. (2)
