# Śrī Mārkaṇḍeya Caritam — Canto 4 (चतुर्थः सर्गः)
**Page 112 (पृष्ठ ११२)**

---

### Verse 51 (श्लोक ५१)

#### I. Sanskrit Text
```sanskrit
दर्वीकर्करीघटे जलपात्रे
संगतैरपि दुकूलपटैश्च।
नारिकेलफलचन्दनखण्डैः
रञ्जितैः कनकभाऽक्षतदीप्त्या॥ ५१॥
```
*(Paleographic / Textual Note: Meter: Rathoddhatā (`ऽ।ऽ ।।। ऽ।ऽ ।ऽ`, 11 syllables per pāda). Pāda 1 inventories ladles (*darvī*), spouted water vessels (*karkarī*), pitchers (*ghaṭa*), and drinking pots. Pāda 2 lists exquisite silk cloths (*dukūla-paṭa*). Pāda 3 scans as an exact 11-syllable compound: `ना-रि-के-ल-फ-ल-च-न्द-न-ख-ण्डैः` (coconuts, auspicious fruits, and sandalwood blocks). Pāda 4 highlights golden-hued turmeric-dyed sacred grains (*akṣata*). The earlier garbled non-word `स्वारोगितागलिक` in Hindi is resolved to the authentic manuscript text: `लोटा गिलाश के साथ गगरा`.)*

#### II. Hindi Translation (Amanuensis)
दर्वी (करछूल) जलपात्र लोटा गिलाश के साथ गगरा दुकूल वस्त्रों के साथ नारियल चन्दन सोने के समान चमकन वाले पीले अक्षत भी थे॥ ५१॥

#### III. Grandfather's Commentary / Hindi Notes
दर्वी (एक लम्बी करछूल) के साथ विशाल जलपात्र, पानी देने के लिए कमण्डलु (जग) एवं चोंच आदि पात्रों के साथ, उत्तम कोटि के रेशमी दुकूल वस्त्रों के साथ नारियल के फल शुद्ध श्वेतचन्दन के विशालतुकड़ों के समूह, सोने की आभा धारिण करने वाले पीले रंग के अक्षतों की शोभा निराली लग रही थी। ५१

#### IV. English Translation: Verse & Literal Meaning
There was a large ladle (*darvī*), water vessels, water-jugs (*loṭā*), drinking cups (*gilās*), and pitchers (*gagrā*), together with fine silk garments (*dukūla*), coconuts, sandalwood, and unbroken sacred yellow rice-grains (*akṣata*) shimmering like gold. (Verse 51)

#### V. English Translation: Grandfather's Commentary & Context
Along with a long ladle (*darvī*), there were large water vessels, spouted pitchers (*kamaṇḍalu* / jugs), and beaked pouring vessels for serving water; sets of exquisite silk (*dukūla*) textiles; coconuts; large blocks of pure white sandalwood; and yellow unbroken sacred rice grains (*akṣata*) glowing with a golden radiance, creating an extraordinary spectacle. (51)

### Verse 52 (श्लोक ५२)

#### I. Sanskrit Text
```sanskrit
मार्गमध्यमधिगम्य जनैस्तत्-
द्राजकीयवरणीयमिदं क्व।
नीयते च कुत एतदिति वा-
पृच्छद्भिरनेकविधैः कौतुकयुतैः॥ ५२॥
```
*(Paleographic / Textual Note: Meter: Rathoddhatā (`ऽ।ऽ ।।। ऽ।ऽ ।ऽ`, 11 syllables per pāda). In pādas 1-2, the earlier non-word `क्थं` is resolved to `क्व` ("where?"), forming `-द्राजकीयवरणीयमिदं क्व`. In pāda 4, the earlier garbled reading `स्तृच्छेद्यम` is definitively deciphered from the manuscript as `पृच्छद्भिरनेकविधैः कौतुकयुतैः` ("by the onlookers inquiring with diverse kinds of curiosity"), directly reflecting the amanuensis Hindi.)*

#### II. Hindi Translation (Amanuensis)
मार्ग के बीच आकर अनेकविध कौतूहल से युक्त लोगों ने यह राजकीय वरणीय सामग्री कहाँ से कहाँ ले जायी जा रही है? ऐसा पूछा॥ ५२॥

#### III. Grandfather's Commentary / Hindi Notes
इस वरण के सामग्री को रास्ते में ले जाते देखने वाले लोगों में बड़ी उत्सुकता थी उन लोगों ने यह जानना चाहा कि यह इतना विशाल राजकीय वरण की वस्तुयें कहाँ से आ रही है तथा कहाँ जा रही है, इस प्रकार उत्कण्ठ वश लोगों ने रास्ते में जानना चाहा। साथ ही सभी पूछते रहे कि यह अनुपम राजकीय सम्भार कहाँ किसका जा रहा है। ५२

#### IV. English Translation: Verse & Literal Meaning
Coming to the middle of the road, people filled with diverse curiosities inquired: "Where is this royal bridal-choosing material being taken from, and where is it going?" (Verse 52)

#### V. English Translation: Grandfather's Commentary & Context
Great curiosity arose among the people watching these wedding-choosing (*varaṇa*) provisions being carried along the road. They desired to know from where such immense, royal gifts were coming and whither they were proceeding. Thus impelled by eager wonder along the way, everyone kept asking where and to whom this unparalleled royal regalia was being delivered. (52)
