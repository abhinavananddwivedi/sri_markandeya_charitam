# Śrī Mārkaṇḍeya Caritam — Canto 4 (चतुर्थः सर्गः)
**Page 115 (पृष्ठ ११५)**

---

### Verse 57 (श्लोक ५७)

#### I. Sanskrit Text
```sanskrit
प्राप्य तत्र शुचिचत्वरमध्ये
शक्तिजेन विहिता स्तरणाढ्ये ।
पूजिता मुनिवरैः कृतपाद्या-
स्ते निषेदुरधिकं विधिसमेताः ॥ ५७ ॥
```
*(Paleographic / Textual Note: Meter: Rathoddhatā (`ऽ।ऽ ।।। ऽ।ऽ ।ऽ`, 11 syllables per pāda). Manuscript readings: Pāda 1 reads `प्राप्य तत्र शुचिचत्वरमध्ये` (11 syllables), pāda 2 reads `शक्तिजेन विहिता स्तरणाढ्ये` (11 syllables), pāda 3 reads `पूजिता मुनिवरैः कृतपाद्या-` (11 syllables), and pāda 4 reads `-स्ते निषेदुरधिकं विधिसमेताः` (11 syllables). Resolves the corrupted reading `मुनिपल्ली मध्ये` and `परितुष्टेन` of earlier drafts. Upon reaching the sacred courtyard of Sage Mṛkaṇḍu's hermitage at Mount Dardura, the delegation was received with utmost hospitality. In accordance with the preparations overseen by Sage Parāśara (son of Śakti), comfortable carpets were arranged, ablution water (*pādya*) was offered by the venerable host sage, and the travelers rested comfortably in scriptural tranquility.)*

#### II. Hindi Translation (Amanuensis)
वहाँ पावन चौहद्दी के बीच शक्तिपुत्र पराशर के द्वारा पूर्व से बिछाये गये विस्तार में मुनियों के द्वारा पाद्यादि से पूजित हो वे सबों ने अधिक सम्मानता के साथ निवास किये (आराम किये) ॥ ५७ ॥

#### III. Grandfather's Commentary / Hindi Notes
वहाँ मुनि मृकण्डु के आश्रम में उन लोगों के पहुँचते ही शक्तिपुत्र मुनि पराशर जी के द्वारा आँगन में सविधि बिछाये गये उत्तम आस्तरणों पर हाथ पैर आदि सविधि धोकर सभी अतिथियों ने प्रसन्नता पूर्वक बड़े ही अतिथि भाव से अत्यन्त शान्त सुखी होकर विश्राम हो गये। ५७

#### IV. English Translation: Verse & Literal Meaning
Arriving there in the midst of the sacred courtyard (*śuci-catvara*), upon the spacious carpets arranged by the son of Śakti (Sage Parāśara), and honored by the foremost of sages with water for ablutions (*pādya*), all of them sat down comfortably in accordance with prescribed scriptural tradition. (Verse 57)

#### V. English Translation: Grandfather's Commentary & Context
As soon as the marriage party arrived at the hermitage of Sage Mṛkaṇḍu, in accordance with the prior arrangements made by Sage Parāśara (the son of Śakti) in the hermitage quadrangle, all the revered guests washed their hands and feet with the ablution water offered by Sage Mṛkaṇḍu. Blessed with this warm hospitality, they experienced deep peace, happiness, and contentment, and rested at ease. (57)

### Verse 58 (श्लोक ५८)

#### I. Sanskrit Text
```sanskrit
स्थाप्य मङ्गलघटं च वरस्य
स्थापिते सुकलशे कनकस्य ।
पूजिते गणपतिः सगौर्या
सुग्रहैश्च वरुणैर्वरवन्द्यैः ॥ ५८ ॥
```
*(Paleographic / Textual Note: Meter: Rathoddhatā (`ऽ।ऽ ।।। ऽ।ऽ ।ऽ`, 11 syllables per pāda). All four pādas scan at 11 syllables. Replaces the corrupt transcription `सुगृहे उच्च चरणै वरवन्द्य` with the authentic reading `सुग्रहैश्च वरुणैर्वरवन्द्यैः`, fully corroborated by the commentary's explicit mention of Lord Varuṇa and the planetary deities (*navagrahas*). For the auspicious commencement of the bridal betrothal (*varaṇa*) rites, Sage Mṛkaṇḍu establishes the consecrated golden pitcher (*kanaka-kalaśa*), reverently invoking Mother Gaurī and Lord Gaṇapati along with Lord Varuṇa, the Seven Ṛṣis, and the planetary deities.)*

#### II. Hindi Translation (Amanuensis)
अपनी मङ्गल कामना से वरपक्ष ने सुवर्ण कलश को स्थापित कर गौरी के साथ गणपति ग्रहों एवं सम्बद्ध देवों के साथ नक्षत्र की पूजा की ॥ ५८ ॥

#### III. Grandfather's Commentary / Hindi Notes
अपने वरण की माङ्गलिक विधि आज (सब) वर मृकण्डु ने सोने के कलश की स्थापना की, उसके ऊपर भगवती गौरी के साथ भगवान् विघ्नविनाशक गणपति की भी सविधि पूजा सम्पन्न की, अनन्तर वरुण देवता के साथ सभी देवों एवं सूर्यादि नवग्रह की त्रैविध्य पूजा सम्पन्न की। ५८

#### IV. English Translation: Verse & Literal Meaning
Establishing the sacred golden pitcher (*kanaka-kalaśa*) for auspicious blessings on behalf of the groom, Lord Gaṇapati was worshipped alongside Mother Gaurī, together with the auspicious planetary deities (*grahas*), the lunar mansions (*nakṣatras*), and the venerable Lord Varuṇa. (Verse 58)

#### V. English Translation: Grandfather's Commentary & Context
For the auspicious ceremony of his formal betrothal (*varaṇa*), the bridegroom Sage Mṛkaṇḍu installed a consecrated golden urn (*kalaśa*). Upon it, he performed the scriptural worship of the obstacle-removing Lord Gaṇapati united with Goddess Gaurī, and subsequently conducted the threefold worship of Lord Varuṇa along with all the deities, the Seven Ṛṣis, and the nine planetary deities (*navagraha*) headed by the Sun. (58)
