# Śrī Mārkaṇḍeya Caritam — Canto 3 (तृतीयः सर्गः)
**Page 83 (पृष्ठ ८३)**

---

### Verse 46 (श्लोक ४६)

#### I. Sanskrit Text
```sanskrit
एष आङ्गिरसगोत्रसम्भवः
स्यात् सहायकस्तथा पदे पदे।
आश्रमान्वयमनोरथानुगं
सर्वकर्मनिपुणोऽप्यधुना विधास्यति॥ ४६॥
```
*(Paleographic / Textual Note: Maharṣi Parāśara deputes an expert disciple of the illustrious Āṅgirasa gotra to assist Mṛkaṇḍu step by step in constructing the hermitage according to his exact specifications. Meter: Rathoddhatā [– ⏑ – | ⏑ ⏑ ⏑ | – ⏑ – | ⏑ –])*

#### II. Hindi Translation (Amanuensis)
इस आङ्गिरस गोत्रीय को मैं तुम्हारे सहायतार्थ दे रहा हूँ यह कर्मठ है अतः सम्प्रति तुम्हारे मनोरथ्रानुरूप आश्रम का निर्माण कर करेगा॥ ४६॥

#### III. Grandfather's Commentary / Hindi Notes
इस आङ्गिरस गोत्र में उत्पन्न होने वाले विशिष्ट व्यक्ति को मैं तुम्हारी उचित सहायता करने में सर्वथा सक्षम होने के कारण तुम्हारे सहयोग के लिए दे रहा हूँ। यह व्यक्ति अपने कार्य निपुण एवं पूर्ण कर्मठ है यह तुम्हारी परिकल्पना के अनुरूप जैसा चाहोगे वैसे आश्रम का निर्माण करवा देगा। ४६

#### IV. English Translation: Verse & Literal Meaning
"This capable sage born of the venerable Āṅgirasa lineage shall be your steadfast helper at every step. Exceptionally skilled in all practical tasks, he will now construct the hermitage in perfect accordance with your cherished conception." (Verse 46)

#### V. English Translation: Grandfather's Commentary & Context
"I am assigning to your service this distinguished scholar born into the exalted Āṅgirasa gotra, as he is eminently capable of rendering you all appropriate assistance. He is exceptionally skilled in his duties and completely dedicated to work; he will oversee and construct the hermitage precisely as you envision it." (46)

### Verse 47 (श्लोक ४७)

#### I. Sanskrit Text
```sanskrit
श्वोऽधुनेव सह दत्तयाऽमुया
तत्र गच्छतु सवत्सया गवा।
सर्वमेव भगवान् मनोरथं
सुप्रपूरयतु ते ममाशिषा॥ ४७॥
```
*(Paleographic / Textual Note: Line 1–2 instructs Mṛkaṇḍu to depart on the morrow accompanied by the Āṅgirasa sage and the sacred cow with her young calf (*savatsā gauḥ*) gifted during the reception rites. Line 4 conveys Parāśara’s heartfelt blessing. Meter: Rathoddhatā [– ⏑ – | ⏑ ⏑ ⏑ | – ⏑ – | ⏑ –])*

#### II. Hindi Translation (Amanuensis)
कल मधुपर्क में समर्पित सवत्सा गौ के साथ यह वहाँ जावे। भगवान् तुम्हारे समस्त मनोरथ को पूर्ण करे यह मेरा आशीर्वाद है॥ ४७॥

#### III. Grandfather's Commentary / Hindi Notes
इस मेरे द्वारा दी गई सवत्सा गौ के साथ कल तुम इसी आंगिरस ऋषि को साथ लेकर दर्दुर स्थान जावो, वहाँ भगवान् तुम्हारे सारे मनोरथ पूर्ण करेंगे यह मेरी हार्दिक अभिलाषा एवं पूर्ण आशीर्वाद है। ४७

#### IV. English Translation: Verse & Literal Meaning
"Tomorrow, depart for that sacred place in the company of this sage, along with this gifted milch-cow accompanied by her tender calf. May the Supreme Lord completely fulfill all your cherished desires through my blessings!" (Verse 47)

#### V. English Translation: Grandfather's Commentary & Context
"Tomorrow morning, taking this sacred cow with her nursing calf—presented to you during your reception—and accompanied by this noble Āṅgirasa sage, set forth toward the land of Dardura. There, the Supreme Lord shall fulfill every single aspiration of yours. This is my deepest heartfelt wish and my complete spiritual benediction upon you." (47)
