# Śrī Mārkaṇḍeya Caritam — Canto 3 (तृतीयः सर्गः)
**Page 79 (पृष्ठ ७९)**

---

### Verse 38 (श्लोक ३८)

#### I. Sanskrit Text
```sanskrit
याच्यतां नरपतिः पुनः कतः
सम्भृतेश्च कृत-सम्भरेत्।
कारुकैश्च परिश्रमेण वा
कुर्वते स्म मम कार्यमुत्सुकम्॥ ३८॥
```
*(Paleographic / Textual Note: The handwritten page reads `याच्यतां नरपतिः पुनः कते / सम्भ्रितेश्च कृत सम्भरेत् / कारुकैश्च प्रतिमन्तरण कथं / कुर्वते स्म मम कार्यमन्वरम्`. As confirmed by both Hindi renderings, it raises three practical questions: 1. From which king should land be requested? 2. From where shall construction materials be assembled? 3. How will craftsmen perform the work diligently without wages? Meter: Rathoddhatā [– ⏑ – | ⏑ ⏑ ⏑ | – ⏑ – | ⏑ –])*

#### II. Hindi Translation (Amanuensis)
पृथ्वी की याचना किस राजा से की जाय? सामग्रियों कहाँ से सम्पादित करूँ? बिना मजदूरी के कारीगर मेरे कार्य सावधान होकर कैसे करें?॥ ३८॥

#### III. Grandfather's Commentary / Hindi Notes
मुनि मृकण्डु ने बड़ी विनम्रता से निवेदन किया कि प्रभो! कृपया आप यह बतावें कि किस राजा से जाकर पृथ्वी माँगी जाय जो घर बनाने के योग्य हो, उस घर के बनाने के लिए अन्य सामग्रियों कहाँ से जुटाई जायँ, बिना पारिश्रमिक (मजदूरी) के कारीगर मेरा कार्य भी भलीभांति उत्साह पूर्वक कैसे कर सकेंगे? ३८

#### IV. English Translation: Verse & Literal Meaning
"From which sovereign king should land be petitioned? From where should the building materials be procured? And how will artisans and craftsmen carry out my work attentively without wages?" (Verse 38)

#### V. English Translation: Grandfather's Commentary & Context
With profound humility, Sage Mṛkaṇḍu submitted: "O Lord! Kindly instruct me: To which earthly king should I go to ask for land suitable for building a hermitage? From where are the various construction materials to be gathered? And how will craftsmen and laborers perform my work skillfully, attentively, and enthusiastically without monetary wages (*pāriśramika*)?" (38)

### Verse 39 (श्लोक ३९)

#### I. Sanskrit Text
```sanskrit
इत्युदीर्य विरते हि वै मुनौ
चारुचन्द्रवदनः पराशरः।
हर्षयन् स्वजनमार्द्रया गिरा
सस्मितः स तमभाषत द्विजम्॥ ३९॥
```
*(Paleographic / Textual Note: In line 3, the amanuensis writes `हर्षयन् स्वजनमार्जया गिरा`, representing `स्वजनम् आर्द्रया गिरा` [gladdening his kin with tender, soothing words]. Meter: Rathoddhatā [– ⏑ – | ⏑ ⏑ ⏑ | – ⏑ – | ⏑ –])*

#### II. Hindi Translation (Amanuensis)
ऐसा कह कर जैसे ही महर्षि मृकण्डु मौन हुए उसी समय चारुचन्द्र वदन पराशर महर्षि ने अपनी स्निग्ध वाणी से आनन्दित करते हुए मुस्कुराहट के साथ बोल पड़े॥ ३९॥

#### III. Grandfather's Commentary / Hindi Notes
मुनि मृकण्डु के विनयपूर्वक इतना कहकर शान्त होते ही अत्यन्त उत्फुल्ल मुख कमल वाले महर्षि पराशर ने अतिशय प्रेमपूर्ण उदार मधुर स्निग्ध वाणी से मुस्कराते हुए आगत उस ब्राह्मण ऋषि मृकण्डु से कहना शुरू किया। ३९

#### IV. English Translation: Verse & Literal Meaning
Having spoken thus, as soon as Sage Mṛkaṇḍu fell silent, the revered Parāśara—whose countenance beamed like a charming full moon—gladdening his devotee with gentle, affectionate speech, smiled gently and addressed the noble brāhmaṇa. (Verse 39)

#### V. English Translation: Grandfather's Commentary & Context
As soon as Sage Mṛkaṇḍu concluded his humble entreaty and became quiet, Maharṣi Parāśara, whose lotus-like face was radiantly blooming, looked upon the arriving brāhmaṇa sage with a serene smile. He began to speak in a deeply loving, generous, melodious, and soothing voice that filled Mṛkaṇḍu’s heart with delight. (39)
