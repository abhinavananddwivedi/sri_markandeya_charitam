# Śrī Mārkaṇḍeya Caritam — Canto 3 (तृतीयः सर्गः)
**Page 77 (पृष्ठ ७७)**

---

### Verse 34 (श्लोक ३४)

#### I. Sanskrit Text
```sanskrit
प्रत्युवाच सकलज्ञ ते धिया
किं तिरोहितमथो ब्रवीमि यत्।
किन्तु शासनमुपेत्य गद्यते
त्वामधीतमपि वत्सलत्वतः॥ ३४॥
```
*(Paleographic / Textual Note: In line 3, the cursive script has an ambiguous cluster resembling `शासनसुचेतथ`, which corresponds to `शासनमुपेत्य गद्यते` [spoken having received your holy instruction]. Line 4 conveys his speech uttered under the shelter of the sage's boundless affection [*vatsalatvataḥ*]. Meter: Rathoddhatā [– ⏑ – | ⏑ ⏑ ⏑ | – ⏑ – | ⏑ –])*

#### II. Hindi Translation (Amanuensis)
महर्षि मृकण्डु बोले हे सकलज्ञ! आपकी बुद्धि से कुछ तिरोहित (छिपा) नहीं है, जिसे मैं बोलूँ। कि आपकी आज्ञा पाकर मैं कह रहा हूँ क्योंकि मुझ पर आपकी महती वत्सलता युक्त ॥ ३४॥

#### III. Grandfather's Commentary / Hindi Notes
महर्षि पराशर की उपर्युक्त बाते सुनकर मुनि मृकण्डु कहने लगे, हे महर्षि! आपकी बुद्धितथा त्रिकालज्ञता से इस विश्व की कौन सी वस्तु वा बात ही है आप से कौन बात छिपी हुई है, कि आपके यह आदेश पाकर कहता हूँ क्योंकि आपकी मेरे ऊपर अतिशय स्नेह वा वात्सल्यता जो है। ३४

#### IV. English Translation: Verse & Literal Meaning
Sage Mṛkaṇḍu replied: "O omniscient Master (*sakalajña*)! What in this world could ever remain concealed from your boundless intellect that I should need to speak of it? Yet, having received your benign command, I speak openly solely because of your immense paternal tenderness toward me." (Verse 34)

#### V. English Translation: Grandfather's Commentary & Context
Hearing the aforementioned words of Maharṣi Parāśara, Sage Mṛkaṇḍu began: "O great sage! Given your transcendent intellect and omniscience spanning all three periods of time, what matter in this entire universe could ever be hidden from you? What is there that you do not already know? Yet, having received your direct directive, I speak, emboldened only by the extraordinary affection and fatherly compassion (*vātsalya*) you bestow upon me." (34)

### Verse 35 (श्लोक ३५)

#### I. Sanskrit Text
```sanskrit
तात! यद्वदसि तत् तथैव मे
सर्वमेव भवता समर्थितम्।
सोऽहमास्मि न कृतज्ञतां त्यजे
यावदायुषमितीरये पुनः॥ ३५॥
```
*(Paleographic / Textual Note: Line 3 has scribal orthography `सोऽहमाह्मि न कृतज्ञतान्त्यजे`, representing standard `सोऽहमास्मि न कृतज्ञतां त्यजे`. Meter: Rathoddhatā [– ⏑ – | ⏑ ⏑ ⏑ | – ⏑ – | ⏑ –])*

#### II. Hindi Translation (Amanuensis)
हे तात! जो आप कह रहे हैं, मेरे विषय में वह सब वैसा ही है जिसे आपने समर्थन किया है। मैं वही हूँ, किन्तु जीवन पर्यन्त मैं कृतज्ञता नहीं भूल सकता यह मैं पुनः कह रहा हूँ॥ ३५॥

#### III. Grandfather's Commentary / Hindi Notes
पूज्यपाद! आप मेरे सम्बन्ध में जो जितना जैसा जानकर कह रहे हैं वह सब बिल्कुल वैसा ही है जिसे आपने स्वयं समर्थन किया है। प्रभो! मैं सचमुच वही हूँ, आपकी कृतज्ञता क्या मैं आजीवन भूल सकता हूँ? इसे फिर भी आप की आज्ञा शिरोधार्य कर अपनी बातें कह रहा हूँ। ३५

#### IV. English Translation: Verse & Literal Meaning
"O revered Father! Whatever you have stated concerning me is precisely so; every word has been affirmed by your divine insight. I am indeed that very person; never shall I abandon my deep debt of gratitude as long as life breathes in me—this I declare again and again." (Verse 35)

#### V. English Translation: Grandfather's Commentary & Context
"O venerable Master! Everything you have discerned and uttered regarding me is entirely accurate, just as you have affirmed. Lord! I am indeed truly that person. How could I ever forget the gratitude owed to you throughout my entire life? Still, holding your command reverently upon my head, I submit my innermost thoughts before you." (35)
