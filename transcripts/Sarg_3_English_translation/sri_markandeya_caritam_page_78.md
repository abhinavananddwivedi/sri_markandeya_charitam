# Śrī Mārkaṇḍeya Caritam — Canto 3 (तृतीयः सर्गः)
**Page 78 (पृष्ठ ७८)**

---

### Verse 36 (श्लोक ३६)

#### I. Sanskrit Text
```sanskrit
यन्नारेण महर्षिणा विबोधितो
दाक्षिणात्यमवलोकितः पथि।
तं प्रवृत्तिमयमाश्रयोन्मुखो
योक्तुमात्मनमवेहि तेऽन्तिकम्॥ ३६॥
```
*(Paleographic / Textual Note: In line 1, the scribal hand writes `यन्नरेण महषिरणा`, which the grandfather’s commentary and context identify as referring to the counsel of Maharṣi Nārada (`यन्नारदेन महर्षिणा विबोधितो`). Line 2 mentions his encounter on the road with Sage Dākṣi. Lines 3–4 convey his resolve to immerse himself into Pravṛtti-mārga under Parāśara’s guidance. Meter: Rathoddhatā [– ⏑ – | ⏑ ⏑ ⏑ | – ⏑ – | ⏑ –])*

#### II. Hindi Translation (Amanuensis)
जो नारद ऋषि ने समझाया, मार्ग में दाक्षी ने जो समझाया उसे प्रवृत्तिमार्ग को बढ़ाने की इच्छा से आपके समीप मेरा आगमन हुआ है इसे आप जाने मुझे नियुक्त करें॥ ३६॥

#### III. Grandfather's Commentary / Hindi Notes
प्रभो! मुझे परमपूज्य महर्षि नारद ने जो बताया, पुनः आते हुए मार्ग में मुझे ऋषि दाक्षी ने भी उसे ही सम्पन्न करने के लिए मुझे पुनः प्रेरित किया मैं फिर उसी प्रवृत्तिमार्ग के सर्वथा अभ्युत्थान के लिए जुड़ जाना चाहता हूँ अतः आप मुझे पुनः उस मार्ग के संवृद्धि के लिए ही आपके पास आया हुआ हूँ, ऐसा आप समझें। ३६

#### IV. English Translation: Verse & Literal Meaning
"What was imparted to me by the supreme sage Nārada, and subsequently reinforced when I encountered Sage Dākṣi on the way—desiring now to devote myself fully to that very path of righteous active life (*pravṛtti-mārga*), know that I have approached your holy presence to dedicate myself." (Verse 36)

#### V. English Translation: Grandfather's Commentary & Context
"O Lord! That sacred counsel which the most venerable Maharṣi Nārada revealed to me, and which Sage Dākṣi further exhorted me to accomplish as I traveled along the path—I now desire to dedicate myself whole-heartedly toward the complete elevation and flourishing of that very *pravṛtti-mārga* (the householder’s path of dynamic spiritual action). Therefore, please understand that it is solely for the advancement of that holy objective that I have sought refuge at your feet." (36)

### Verse 37 (श्लोक ३७)

#### I. Sanskrit Text
```sanskrit
आश्रमं समुचितं विनिर्दिश
स्वात्मनः सुखदं तु तत्र मे।
पूर्त्तिरेतु मम समीहितं कथं
वेति सम्यगनुशाधि मां प्रभो!॥ ३७॥
```
*(Paleographic / Textual Note: Line 2 in the amanuensis hand reads `स्वात्मनत्सुखदं ते तत्र क्रियाम्`, and line 3 reads `पूर्त्तिं मम समीहितं कथं`. The underlying sense is to point out a conducive hermitage and how his heartfelt mission may be fulfilled. Meter: Rathoddhatā [– ⏑ – | ⏑ ⏑ ⏑ | – ⏑ – | ⏑ –])*

#### II. Hindi Translation (Amanuensis)
आत्मसुखार्थ समुचित आश्रम का निर्माण मैं कहाँ करूँ इसका निर्देश दें। मेरी इच्छापूर्ति कैसे होगी यह भी मुझे बतावें॥ ३७॥

#### III. Grandfather's Commentary / Hindi Notes
इसीलिए हे महर्षि! मैं अपने कार्यसाधन के लिए कहाँ पर सुखपूर्वक रहने के लिए स्थान बनाऊँ वह समुचित स्थान आप बतावें, और मेरी इस कार्य पूर्त्ति कैसे होगी, आदि बातें भी मुझे बताने की कृपा करें। ३७

#### IV. English Translation: Verse & Literal Meaning
"Please direct me to an appropriate hermitage where I may establish a peaceful dwelling conducive to my spiritual duties. How shall my cherished aspiration reach fulfillment? Instruct me fully and definitively in all this, O Master!" (Verse 37)

#### V. English Translation: Grandfather's Commentary & Context
"Therefore, O great Maharṣi! Kindly designate a suitable sacred location where I may build an ashram to dwell in comfort and successfully accomplish my spiritual responsibilities. Be so gracious as to guide me on how this sacred endeavor shall reach fruition, and advise me on all the necessary steps." (37)
