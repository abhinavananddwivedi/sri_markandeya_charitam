# Śrī Mārkaṇḍeya Caritam — Canto 3 (तृतीयः सर्गः)
**Page 81 (पृष्ठ ८१)**

### Verse 42 (श्लोक ४२)

#### I. Sanskrit Text
```sanskrit
यान्ति सिद्धिरिह ते द्विजोत्तमाः
यद्यदेव मनसा समीहितम्।
तेन तेऽत्र किल भारती प्रजा
कुर्वते समुपनेतुमादृताः॥ ४२॥
```
*(Paleographic / Textual Note: The verse extols the traditional spiritual power of exalted brāhmaṇas whose mere mental resolve (*saṅkalpa*) prompts the righteous subjects of Bhāratavarṣa to supply whatever is needed. Meter: Rathoddhatā [– ⏑ – | ⏑ ⏑ ⏑ | – ⏑ – | ⏑ –])*

#### II. Hindi Translation (Amanuensis)
ब्राह्मणों की वाणी में सिद्धि है, तुमने मन से जो-जो इच्छा की है, वे सभी भारती प्रजा तुम्हारे लिये उपस्थित करेंगे॥ ४२॥

#### III. Grandfather's Commentary / Hindi Notes
उदारशय उच्च द्विजश्रेष्ठों की वाणी स्वयं सिद्ध हुआ करती हैं, वे जिन वस्तुओं की मानस परिकल्पना करते हैं, वे सभी वस्तुयें इस भूभाग में रहने वाली प्रजा स्वतः उन्हें उपलब्ध करा देती हैं। ब्राह्मणों द्वारा मानस परिकल्पना मात्र से उनके आराध्यामूल्य सभी कार्य स्वतः पूर्ण हो कर देने वाली इस भारतवर्ष देश की प्रजा की विशेषता रही है। ४२

#### IV. English Translation: Verse & Literal Meaning
"Whatever the most exalted brāhmaṇas cherish in their minds attains immediate fruition in this sacred realm. Therefore, the devoted people of this land of Bhārata will eagerly bring forth and present all necessary offerings unto you." (Verse 42)

#### V. English Translation: Grandfather's Commentary & Context
"The speech and intentions of noble, magnanimous, foremost brāhmaṇas are inherently fulfilled (*siddha*). Whatever objects or arrangements they mentally envision, the devoted populace inhabiting this sacred territory voluntarily and joyfully provides. It has forever been the unique glory of the people of Bhāratavarṣa that, by the mere mental conception of holy brāhmaṇas, all their sacred undertakings are spontaneously brought to complete fulfillment." (42)

### Verse 43 (श्लोक ४३)

#### I. Sanskrit Text
```sanskrit
यावती च खलु भूमिरपेक्ष्यते
यानि चोपकरणानि साम्प्रतम्।
मन्निदेशवशवर्तिनी प्रजा
तानि ते समुपढौकयिष्यति॥ ४३॥
```
*(Paleographic / Textual Note: Line 4 clearly reads `तानि ते समुपढौकयिष्यति` [shall reverently present unto you]. Meter: Rathoddhatā [– ⏑ – | ⏑ ⏑ ⏑ | – ⏑ – | ⏑ –])*

#### II. Hindi Translation (Amanuensis)
तुम्हें जितनी भूमि की आवश्यकता है उस समय जो जो सामग्री अपेक्षित है, वे सभी की पूर्ति मेरी आज्ञा के वशीभूत प्रजाएँ पूर्ण करेंगी॥ ४३॥

#### III. Grandfather's Commentary / Hindi Notes
प्रिय! आपको जितनी भूमि की आवश्यकता है और इस समय जिन-जिन अन्य आवश्यक वस्तुओं की भी आवश्यकता है वे सारी वस्तुये मेरे मात्र आदेश मात्र से मेरे अधीन भूभाग की मेरी ये प्रजा स्वतः आपके पास पहुँचा दिया करेगी। ४३

#### IV. English Translation: Verse & Literal Meaning
"However much land is required by you, and whatever building materials and implements are needed at this moment, the devoted subjects abiding obediently by my direction will bring and offer them respectfully to you." (Verse 43)

#### V. English Translation: Grandfather's Commentary & Context
"Beloved child! However much land you require, and whatever additional essential articles and materials are needed at this juncture, all of them shall be delivered directly into your hands spontaneously by these subjects inhabiting the territories under my spiritual jurisdiction, solely upon my word." (43)
