# Śrī Mārkaṇḍeya Caritam — Canto 3 (तृतीयः सर्गः)
**Page 86 (पृष्ठ ८६)**

### Verse 52 (श्लोक ५२)

#### I. Sanskrit Text
```sanskrit
इति समुदितवान् शौचमभ्येत्य संप्रणम्य मुदा
मनसि पुलकितेऽङ्गे सन्निहत्य स्वकार्यम्।
कलित बहु विचाराश्चाङ्कपाल्यां निमग्नस्तत्परो
व्ययगतसुखभोगो निद्रया मन्द मन्दं निनाय शर्वरीम्॥ ५२॥
```
*(Paleographic / Textual Note: This marks the climactic concluding narrative verse of Canto 3. Having received the blessing, cow, companion sage, and directions, Sage Mṛkaṇḍu bows with joy, rapturous horripilation (*pulakita-aṅga*), and spends the wondrous night reflecting on the auspicious fulfillment of his divine mission.)*

#### II. Hindi Translation (Amanuensis)
इस प्रकार मुनिवर पराशर की आज्ञा संवत्स (अथवा कृपा) से अवनत कम्पित मन में आनन्द से एवं रोमाञ्चित अङ्गों से जगते जागते हुए ही सुन्दर अपने लिए आश्रम की प्राप्ति मनन में संलग्न भयशून्य होकर मुनि मृकण्डु ने सबके समान रात्रि को व्यतीत किया (लेटने में आनन्दित हुए)॥ ५२॥

#### III. Grandfather's Commentary / Hindi Notes
इस प्रकार ऋषिप्रवर पराशर जी के अभिलाष पूर्ण सरसवचनों को सादर शिर झुकाकर अत्यन्त रोमांचपूर्ण पुलकित शरीर वाले मुनि मृकण्डु ने सस्नेह विचारते हुए, उनींदी आँखों से सारी रात बितायी, महर्षि पराशर जी द्वारा कथित विविध बातों को निरन्तर अपने मन में विचारते हुए अपनी कामनाओं की प्राप्ति की भावना में सदा डूबते उतराते हुए, विस्मृति शान्त मन से मुनि मृकण्डु ने लम्बी सारी रात एक क्षण के समान बिता दी। ५२

#### IV. English Translation: Verse & Literal Meaning
Having spoken thus, performing his purifications and bowing low with immense joy, with body thrilling with horripilation and heart absorbed in the upcoming holy undertaking, immersed in countless sublime reflections and sweet anticipation, Sage Mṛkaṇḍu passed the tranquil night peacefully in gentle slumber. (Verse 52)

#### V. English Translation: Grandfather's Commentary & Context
Bowing his head with profound reverence before the nectar-like, wish-fulfilling words of the foremost sage Parāśara, Sage Mṛkaṇḍu—his entire physical frame trembling with ecstatic joy—spent the night in affectionate contemplation with half-wakeful, drowsy eyes. Continuously turning over in his mind the multifaceted advice bestowed by Maharṣi Parāśara, and gently undulating in the blissful anticipation of his impending marital and spiritual fulfillment, the tranquil sage found the long, arduous night passing away swiftly as though it were but a single fleeting moment. (52)

### Verses 53–54–55 (Concluding Verses / Phalaśruti)

#### I. Sanskrit Text
```sanskrit
श्रीचन्द्रभूषणसुधीसुतकेन बन्धुपौत्रेण वै गुणवता दृढम्।
संगुम्फितं सुमनसां मनसाऽऽदृत्युपक्रमान्मग्न्यन्तेन हि शितस्य सुशीलकेण॥ ५३॥

ज्ञानेश्वरी चरणयोजन शिवार्चनेन विद्बुद्धजनानर्चनवता वरेण्येन।
संकाव्यं सुसङ्गलमयं सुपवित्रिपूर्णं शाश्वतसमा विजयतां शिवयोः प्रसादात्॥ ५४॥

दीर्घजीविचरितामृत काव्ये सङ्गितोचित सदाऽऽदरणीयम्।
एवमेतेन रचिते सरतेयुः सर्गोऽनुगामितोऽयं तृतीयः॥ ५५॥
```

#### II. Manuscript Editorial Note (Grandfather)
**विशेष:- ५३-५४-५५ श्लोकों का अर्थ कृपया पृष्ठ ३२ में अवलोकन करें।**
*(Author's Note: For the detailed grammatical analysis and Hindi word-for-word exposition of Verses 53–55, please refer to Page 32 of the manuscript at the conclusion of Canto 1).*

#### III. English Translation & Commentary
These three signature benedictory verses (*mudrā-padya* / *phalaśruti*) are placed at the culmination of each Canto by the author:
1. **Verse 53**: Identifies the noble lineage of the poet—composed by the virtuous son of the wise Śrī Candra Bhūṣaṇa and grandson of Bandhu, woven with devout affection for the delight of pure-hearted rasikas (*sumanasām*).
2. **Verse 54**: Prays for the eternal triumph of this sacred, supremely auspicious epic poem through dedicated devotion at the lotus feet of Mother Jñāneśvarī and Lord Śiva, by the grace of Śiva and Śakti (*śivayoḥ prasādāt*).
3. **Verse 55**: Culminates the third canto of this immortal nectar-epic singing the life of the long-lived sage (*Dīrghajīvi-caritāmṛta* / *Śrī Mārkaṇḍeya Caritam*).
