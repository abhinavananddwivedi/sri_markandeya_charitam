# Śrī Mārkaṇḍeya Caritam

## Verse 13 (श्लोक १३)

### Sanskrit Shloka
> सन्निधाय च शिरः प्रदेशके  
> वारिपूर्णकलशं सकटोरम् ।  
> सन्निवेश्य भगवत्स्मृतिं हृदि  
> मातुरङ्कपतितो यथा शिशुः ॥ १३ ॥

### Hindi Translation (Amanuensis)
> शिरःप्रदेश में जलपूर्ण कलश तथा कटोरा रखकर भगवान् का स्मरण करते हुए माता के गोद में पड़ा हुआ शिशुवत् सो गये ॥ १३ ॥

### Grandfather's Commentary / Hindi Notes
> इसके बाद मुनि मृकण्ड ने जल से भरे हुए पूर्ण कलश को काँसे के कटोरे से ढंककर अपने सिरहाने रखा, भगवान् का स्मरण करते हुए जैसे माता की गोद में शान्तिपूर्वक निश्चिन्त कोई बालक सो जाये वैसे ही स्वस्थ शान्त मातृ सदृशी भगवती धरा की गोद में स्वस्थ हो सो गये । १३

### English Translation (Verse & Meaning)
> Placing near his head a pitcher filled to the brim with pure water covered with a bowl, and centering the remembrance of the Supreme Lord deep within his heart, he slept peacefully like a child resting securely in its mother's lap.

### English Translation (Grandfather's Notes & Context)
> Following this, Sage Mṛkaṇḍu covered a vessel filled with water with a bronze bowl and placed it near his head. Enshrining the divine remembrance of God in his consciousness, just as an innocent child sleeps serenely and without care in the lap of its loving mother, so did the sage rest in undisturbed tranquility upon the bosom of Mother Earth (Bhagavatī Dharā).

## Verse 14 (श्लोक १४)

### Sanskrit Shloka
> निद्रया व्यपगतस्मृतिर्बभौ  
> सत्समाधिमिव शीलयन्मुनिः ।  
> अरुणे समुदञ्चिते पुरो  
> जाग्रतः स्व नियमं चकार सः ॥ १४ ॥

### Hindi Translation (Amanuensis)
> निद्रा से लुप्त स्मृति वाले महात्मा सत् समाधि का अभ्यास करता हुआ दीप्तिमान हो गये । आगे, अरुणोभा छा जाने पर जागकर अपने सन्ध्यावन्दनादि किये ॥ १४ ॥

### Grandfather's Commentary / Hindi Notes
> यद्यपि मुनि मृकण्ड गहरी नींद में पूर्ण सो गये थे, उनकी स्मृतियाँ समाप्त हो गई थीं, लगता था कि मुनि अत्यन्त तेजोदीप्त उदात्ततम समाधि की अवस्था में समाहित हो गये हैं, प्रातःकाल अरुणोभा के पूर्व ब्राह्मवेला में ही उठकर प्रात्याहिक दैनन्दिन सन्ध्यावन्दनादि का कार्य सविधि सम्पन्न किया । १४

### English Translation (Verse & Meaning)
> With worldly recollections wholly absorbed in deep slumber, the resplendent sage shone as if immersed in the profoundest state of divine absorption (samādhi). Then, as the first ruddy hues of dawn arose in the east, he awoke and faithfully performed his sacred morning observances.

### English Translation (Grandfather's Notes & Context)
> Although Sage Mṛkaṇḍu had fallen into profound sleep and all waking awareness had subsided, he radiated brilliance as though merged into the highest, most exalted state of samādhi. Rising at the auspicious pre-dawn hour (Brāhma-muhūrta) before the first blush of red spread across the sky, he duly fulfilled his daily morning prayers, ablutions, and sandhyā rites according to sacred ordinance.
