# Śrī Mārkaṇḍeya Caritam

**Canto 2 (द्वितीयः सर्गः)**  
*(Page Number: 50 / पृष्ठ संख्या: ५०)*

## Verse 35 (श्लोक ३५)

### Sanskrit Shloka
> समाल्लपन्तं धृतवारिभिर्निजैः  
> सुतैराहूयत भोजनार्थतः ।  
> ततो गृहान्तर्गृहमेधिकर्मवित्  
> सवैश्वदेवं बलिमर्पयत्पुरा ॥ ३५ ॥

### Hindi Translation (Amanuensis)
> इस प्रकार बात करते हुए हाथ में पानी लिये लड़को ने भोजन के लिए बुलाया। बाद में उस गृहस्थ ने गृह के भीतर गृहस्थधर्मानुकूल वैश्वदेवबलि अर्पित किया ॥ ३५ ॥

### Grandfather's Commentary / Hindi Notes
> अभी यह बातें चल ही रही थीं कि गृहस्थ-मुनि के लड़के ने सजल कलश के साथ आकर भोजन करने के लिए सादर निवेदन किया, तदनन्तर उस गृही मुनि ने घर के भीतर जाकर गृहस्थ धर्माचरणानुरूप विश्वेदेवबलि का प्राथमिक कार्य सम्पन्न किया । ३५

### English Translation (Verse & Meaning)
> While they were thus conversing, the householder's son arrived carrying water in a ceremonial ewer, respectfully announcing that the meal was served. Thereupon, the host, well-versed in the rituals of Gṛhastha Dharma, stepped inside and offered the preparatory Vaiśvadeva sacrificial oblations to all cosmic deities.

### English Translation (Grandfather's Notes & Context)
> While their cordial conversation was underway, the son of the householder approached with a vessel of clean water, politely inviting them to dine. Following the strict Vedic code of a householder, the host first went inside to perform the obligatory Vaiśvadeva-bali—offering food portions to all universal deities and living creatures before serving the household.

## Verse 36 (श्लोक ३६)

### Sanskrit Shloka
> ततोऽतिथिं सत्कृतमभ्युपाविश-  
> न्नेकशाकैः सहसूपसंयुतम् ।  
> पयोविधानैः स किलाटकूर्चिकैः  
> समानिनायौदनमिन्दुसन्निभम् ॥ ३६ ॥

### Hindi Translation (Amanuensis)
> उसके बाद सत्कृत अतिथि के समीप बैठकर अनेक प्रकार के शाक, व्यञ्जन, दाल से युक्त कूर्चिक तथा पयोविधानों (दुग्ध प्रयोगों) के साथ चन्द्रोज्ज्वल भात परसा गया ॥ ३६ ॥

### Grandfather's Commentary / Hindi Notes
> इसके बाद पूर्व सत्कृत किए गये अतिथि के सन्निकट गृही मुनि ने बैठकर विविध प्रकार के सामयिक शाकों के साथ दाल और विविध प्रकार के दही, घी, पनीर, रबड़ी आदि स्निग्ध भोज्य पदार्थों के साथ चन्द्रमा के कान्ति के सदृश अत्यन्त धवल चावल का भात भी परसा । ३६

### English Translation (Verse & Meaning)
> Then, seating himself attentively beside his honored guest, the host served him steamed rice shining pure white like the autumn moon, accompanied by seasoned lentil soups (sūpa), a variety of spiced vegetables, and rich dairy delicacies including fresh curd, ghee, paneer, and thickened milk puddings (kūrcikā).

### English Translation (Grandfather's Notes & Context)
> Sitting reverently beside the honored ascetic guest, the host personally supervised the serving of a feast: pristine rice radiant like the moonlight, aromatic lentil broths, diverse seasonal vegetable preparations, and an assortment of rich milk delicacies—fresh butter, clarified ghee, curd, paneer, and sweet thickened milk (rabrī).
