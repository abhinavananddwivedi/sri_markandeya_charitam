## Verse 21 (श्लोक २१)

### Sanskrit Shloka
> ततः सुदूरादुपयान्तमात्मनो  
> गृहोन्मुखं वर्णिबरं विलोकयन् ।  
> कृतानतिः संप्रणमय्य बालकान्  
> चकार सम्मोदिममुं सदार्चितम् ॥ २१ ॥

### Hindi Translation (Amanuensis)
> उसके बाद बहुत दूर से अपने घर के तरफ आते हुए ब्रह्मचारी को देखकर प्रणाम किया, बालकों से भी प्रणाम कराकर उसने उनकी पूजा की ॥ २१ ॥

### Grandfather's Commentary / Hindi Notes
> तब तक काफी दूर से अपने घर की ओर आते हुए उस ब्रह्मचारी को देखकर उस गृहपति ने सविनय प्रणाम किया, साथ ही अपने घर के सभी सदस्यों एवं बालकों से भी उनका सादर प्रणाम करा सूर्यास्त के समय में अपने घर आये हुए अनजान उस ब्रह्मचारी अतिथि की सादर अर्चना की । २१

### English Translation (Verse & Meaning)
> Spotting the radiant young brahmacārī (Sage Mṛkaṇḍu) approaching his home from afar, the householder bowed with humility, had all his children and household members pay their respects, and joyfully received the guest with devout hospitality.

### English Translation (Grandfather's Notes & Context)
> Beholding the effulgent young ascetic walking toward his dwelling at sunset, the noble householder bowed low in reverence. Gathering his family members and children, he instructed them all to offer their prostrations to this unexpected divine guest, welcoming him with heartfelt adoration and traditional hospitality.

## Verse 22 (श्लोक २२)

### Sanskrit Shloka
> उपाजहाराथ स यज्ञियां वृषीं  
> सुविष्टरं पाद्यमथार्घ्यमुत्तमम् ।  
> ततः समाचान्तममुं सुबोधितं  
> सवत्सधेन्वा मधुपर्कमाहरत् ॥ २२ ॥

### Hindi Translation (Amanuensis)
> उन्होंने यज्ञिय आसन, बैठने के आसन, पाद्य, उत्तम अर्घ्य ब्रह्मचारी को प्रदान किया। पाद्यार्घ्य लेने के बाद सुखपूर्वक बैठे हुए ब्रह्मचारी को सवत्सा धेनु के माध्यम से मधुपर्क समर्पित किया ॥ २२ ॥

### Grandfather's Commentary / Hindi Notes
> उस अतिथि ब्रह्मचारी के बैठने के लिए यज्ञीय आसन तथा पादप्रक्षालनार्थ जल, एवं अर्घ्य आदि उचित सत्कार सम्पादित कर उनके हाथ पैर धोकर आचमन कर सुखपूर्वक बैठ जाने के बाद अपनी सवत्सा गो देवी द्वारा सम्पादित मधुमिश्रित मधुपर्क भी समर्पित किया । २२

### English Translation (Verse & Meaning)
> He offered the ascetic a sacred kuśa-grass seat (vṛṣī), a comfortable mat, water for washing the feet (pādya), and the auspicious oblation (arghya). Once the sage had washed, sipped water (ācamana), and seated himself comfortably, the host offered him the traditional honey-curd concoction (Madhuparka) prepared from the milk of his lactating cow and calf.

### English Translation (Grandfather's Notes & Context)
> The pious host provided a sanctified ritual seat for the ascetic guest, washed his feet with cool water, and performed the customary welcoming offerings (arghya). Once Sage Mṛkaṇḍu had refreshed himself and sat at ease, the householder respectfully presented the sacred sweet offering of Madhuparka, prepared with pure honey and fresh milk from his cow with calf.
